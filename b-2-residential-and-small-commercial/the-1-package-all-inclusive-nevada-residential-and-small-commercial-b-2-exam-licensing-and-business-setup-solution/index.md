## Exam Details

The Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial General Building Contractor examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial General Building Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 3 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 56 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination covers a broad range of general building subjects:

- **Sitework:** 4 questions
- **Concrete:** 10 questions
- **Masonry:** 10 questions
- **Metals:** 5 questions
- **Carpentry:** 12 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 9 questions
- **Doors and Windows:** 3 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 4 questions
- **Safety:** 10 questions
- **General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating:** 10 questions
- **Nevada One Call:** 3 questions

**Carpentry** is the largest individual examination category. Candidates should review framing, structural wood construction, floor systems, wall framing, roof framing, trusses, sheathing, openings, fastening, and practical construction methods.

**Concrete** and **Masonry** are also major subjects. Preparation should include concrete placement, reinforcement, forms, structural requirements, masonry units, mortar, block and brick construction, and applicable building-code requirements.

**Safety** is an important part of B-2 preparation. Candidates should study construction hazards, personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolds, excavation safety, fall protection, tools, material handling, and other OSHA-related concepts.

**General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating** requires candidates to connect building-code knowledge with practical project interpretation. Candidates should be comfortable reading construction information, identifying applicable code requirements, understanding dimensions and details, and applying estimating principles.

## How does the Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) exam work?

The Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
