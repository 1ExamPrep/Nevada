## Exam Details

The Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial General Building Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for the Nevada State Contractors Board. Candidates must first submit their contractor license application and receive authorization before scheduling the required examination.

The B-2 trade examination contains **80 questions**. Candidates must answer at least **56 questions correctly** to achieve the required passing score, and the examination allows **3 hours** for completion.

The published B-2 examination content outline includes:

- **Sitework:** 4 questions.
- **Concrete:** 10 questions.
- **Masonry:** 10 questions.
- **Metals:** 5 questions.
- **Carpentry:** 12 questions.
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 9 questions.
- **Doors and Windows:** 3 questions.
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 4 questions.
- **Safety:** 10 questions.
- **General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating:** 10 questions.
- **Nevada One Call:** 3 questions.

The exam therefore requires a broad understanding of residential and small commercial construction rather than expertise in only one trade. Carpentry is the largest single category, while concrete, masonry, safety, and general code, plan reading, and estimating each represent significant portions of the examination.

Nevada also requires a business and law examination known as the Construction Management Survey, or CMS. The CMS examination contains 60 scored questions, requires 45 correct answers to pass, and allows 120 minutes for the scored portion. Its subjects include licensing, estimating and bidding, lien law, financial management, tax laws, labor laws, project management, contracts, risk management, and environmental and safety topics.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial Contractor trade examination is an **open-book test**. The Nevada Construction Management Survey business and law examination is also open book.

Open-book testing makes familiarity with the approved references an important part of exam preparation. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted books to the examination center. Reference books are not automatically supplied at the testing location.

PSI permits approved references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. References may not contain handwritten notes. Candidates are also prohibited from writing or highlighting in their references during the examination session.

Permanent tabs may be used to organize the approved books. Temporary tabs, such as removable flags or Post-It notes, are not allowed. Additional loose papers or papers attached to the references are also prohibited.

Because the B-2 trade examination contains 80 questions and allows three hours, candidates benefit from learning where major code provisions and technical topics are located before test day. ## Licensing Steps

1. **Select the Nevada B-2 classification.** The Residential and Small Commercial classification is intended for qualifying construction and remodeling work on structures that do not exceed three stories above ground and one story below ground.
2. **Identify the qualifying individual or individuals.** Nevada requires each contractor license to have a qualified individual. One individual may serve in both management and trade qualifying roles, or separate individuals may fulfill those responsibilities.
3. **Document the required trade experience.** The trade qualified individual generally must demonstrate at least four full years of applicable experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor within the 15 years immediately preceding the application.
4. **Submit the Nevada contractor license application.** The application is submitted to the Nevada State Contractors Board with the required business, qualifier, financial, background, and experience information.
5. **Receive examination eligibility.** Candidates may not schedule the Nevada contractor examinations until the application has been submitted, experience has been reviewed, and the Board issues authorization.
6. **Pass the required examinations.** A management qualifier must pass the Nevada Construction Management Survey examination. The trade qualifier must satisfy the B-2 trade examination requirement unless an applicable waiver is granted.
7. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** Applicants must satisfy the Board's financial responsibility and licensing requirements before the contractor license is issued.
8. **Provide the required contractor bond after approval.** Nevada requires contractor licenses to be supported by a license bond, with the required amount determined by the Board.

## State Requirements

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates contractor licensing throughout Nevada. A B-2 license is classified under General Building and is specifically designated for Residential and Small Commercial work.

The official B-2 scope covers construction and remodeling of houses and other structures that support, shelter, or enclose persons, animals, or other property when those structures do not extend more than three stories above ground and one story below ground.

Nevada requires each contractor license to have an appropriate qualified individual. The trade qualified individual generally must have four full years of experience within the requested classification during the 15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application. Qualifying experience must be at the journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor level.

The management qualified individual is responsible for satisfying the business and law examination requirement. The trade qualified individual is responsible for meeting the technical experience and applicable trade examination requirements. One person may serve in both roles when that individual satisfies both sets of qualifications.

Nevada contractor applicants must also demonstrate financial responsibility. The Board requires financial documentation, and the type of financial statement needed depends on the monetary limit requested.

After license approval, the applicant must provide the contractor bond required by the Nevada State Contractors Board. The Board determines the bond amount based on factors including the license type, monetary limit, financial responsibility, experience, and character of the applicant.

## How does the Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) exam work?

The Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
