## Exam Details

The Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial General Building Contractor examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial General Building Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 3 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 56 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination covers the following major construction areas:

- **Sitework:** 4 questions
- **Concrete:** 10 questions
- **Masonry:** 10 questions
- **Metals:** 5 questions
- **Carpentry:** 12 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 9 questions
- **Doors and Windows:** 3 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 4 questions
- **Safety:** 10 questions
- **General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating:** 10 questions
- **Nevada One Call:** 3 questions

**Carpentry** is the largest individual examination category with 12 questions. Candidates should devote significant study time to framing systems, floor construction, wall framing, roof framing, trusses, sheathing, openings, fastening, structural wood components, and general carpentry methods.

**Concrete** and **Masonry** each contain 10 questions. Concrete preparation should include reinforcement, forms, placement, structural requirements, and quality construction practices. Masonry study should include brick, block, stone, mortar, layout, construction methods, and related building-code requirements.

**Safety** also contains 10 questions. Candidates should review fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, excavations, personal protective equipment, tools, material handling, and other construction hazards covered by OSHA-related preparation.

**General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating** contains 10 questions. Candidates should practice reading construction information, interpreting dimensions and details, locating code provisions, estimating quantities, and connecting project plans with applicable building requirements.

**Thermal and Moisture Protection** contains 9 questions. Preparation should include roofing, weather protection, flashing, moisture control, insulation-related concepts, and other methods used to protect residential and small commercial buildings from environmental exposure.

Sitework, Metals, Doors and Windows, Drywall and Finishes, and Nevada One Call make up smaller portions of the exam but remain important. Candidates should avoid concentrating only on the largest categories because every question can contribute toward the passing score.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada B-2 Residential and Small Commercial examination is an **open-book test** with specific approved references permitted in the examination room.

The approved exam-room references used for this preparation are:

- **International Building Code, 2018**
- **International Residential Code for One- and Two-Family Dwellings, 2018**
- **ACI 318-14: Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete and Commentary**

The International Building Code supports preparation involving general building requirements, structural provisions, construction materials, occupancy-related requirements, fire and life-safety provisions, and other building-code topics relevant to small commercial and general building work.

The International Residential Code is especially important for residential construction. Candidates should become comfortable locating requirements involving foundations, floor systems, wall construction, roofs, framing, openings, stairs, weather protection, and other residential building subjects.

ACI 318-14 supports structural concrete preparation. Candidates should practice finding requirements involving concrete materials, reinforcement, structural provisions, and related concrete construction requirements.

The following books are used for study but are **not allowed into the B-2 trade examination:**

## How does the Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) exam work?

The Residential and Small Commercial Contractor (B-2) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
