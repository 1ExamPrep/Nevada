## Exam Details

The Nevada C-21C Sheet Metal Contractor examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada State Contractors Board licensing process. Candidates must receive examination eligibility through the Board before scheduling the trade examination.

The C-21C examination contains **60 questions**. Candidates must answer at least **42 questions correctly** to achieve the published minimum passing score. The examination allows **2.5 hours** for completion.

The published examination content outline includes:

- **Ducts:** 20 questions.
- **Chimneys, Flues, and Vents:** 8 questions.
- **Hoods, Ventilation, Equipment, and Food Service Equipment:** 10 questions.
- **Metal Roofing Systems, Flashing, and Architectural Metals:** 10 questions.
- **Gutters and Downspouts:** 5 questions.
- **Tools and Equipment:** 4 questions.
- **Safety:** 3 questions.

Ducts account for one-third of the examination, making duct fabrication, construction, installation, materials, and related mechanical code knowledge especially important. Hoods and ventilation equipment, along with metal roofing, flashing, and architectural metal subjects, also represent substantial portions of the test.

Candidates should not ignore smaller areas such as tools, safety, gutters, and downspouts. The examination is designed to test broad competence within the sheet metal scope, and successful preparation should address the entire published outline.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-21C Sheet Metal Contractor trade examination permits specific approved code books in the examination room. Candidates should distinguish between the complete list of references used for exam preparation and the smaller group of books permitted during the actual test.

The **Uniform Mechanical Code, 2018** and **International Building Code, 2018** are approved for use in the C-21C examination center. The other books in this package are study references and are not approved for use in the C-21C trade examination room.

PSI allows approved exam-room references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. References may not contain handwritten notes. Permanent tabs may be used, while removable or temporary tabs are not permitted.

Because only specific code references are allowed during the examination, candidates should develop strong trade knowledge before testing. ## Licensing Steps

1. **Select the C-21C Sheet Metal classification.** Applicants seeking to perform work within Nevada's defined sheet metal scope must apply for the appropriate C-21C subclassification.
2. **Identify the qualifying individual.** Nevada contractor applicants must have an appropriate qualified individual who meets the experience and qualification requirements for the requested classification.
3. **Document qualifying experience.** The qualifying individual generally must demonstrate four full years of qualifying experience in the requested classification within the 15 years immediately preceding the application.
4. **Submit the contractor license application.** The application is submitted to the Nevada State Contractors Board with the required business, qualifier, financial, experience, and supporting documentation.
5. **Receive examination eligibility.** Candidates cannot schedule the contractor examination until the application has been filed, the Board has verified the applicable experience, and an Examination Eligibility form has been issued.
6. **Complete the C-21C trade examination.** The qualifying individual must pass the Sheet Metal Contractor trade examination unless an applicable examination waiver is approved.
7. **Complete the Nevada Business and Law examination.** Nevada generally requires the applicable qualifying individual to pass the Construction Management Survey, or CMS, business and law examination unless an approved waiver applies.
8. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** The applicant must satisfy the Nevada State Contractors Board's financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other applicable licensing requirements before the license is issued.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A practical C-21C study plan should start with ductwork because the published examination contains 20 duct questions. Candidates should review duct materials, fabrication, fittings, seams, joints, reinforcement, supports, pressure-related requirements, installation methods, and mechanical code provisions.

The HVAC Duct Construction Standards reference is especially useful for building familiarity with accepted duct construction practices. Candidates should learn how to interpret tables, construction details, reinforcement information, duct classifications, and fabrication illustrations rather than relying only on memorization.

Hoods, ventilation systems, and food-service equipment account for another 10 questions. Preparation should include ventilation concepts, exhaust systems, hoods, duct arrangements, clearances, and related mechanical code requirements. Regular use of the Uniform Mechanical Code can help candidates connect technical concepts with enforceable code provisions.

Metal roofing systems, flashing, and architectural metals also account for 10 questions. The SMACNA Architectural Sheet Metal Manual and roofing reference can be used to study flashing principles, drainage, sheet metal roof details, joints, terminations, penetrations, weather protection, and architectural metal applications.

Chimneys, flues, and vents make up eight questions. Candidates should understand the purposes of these systems, material requirements, clearances, installation principles, and how the applicable mechanical and building codes address them.

Gutters and downspouts account for five questions. Study drainage principles, fabrication, slope, joining, expansion, outlets, leaders, downspouts, and common architectural sheet metal details associated with roof drainage.

Tools and equipment are also tested. Candidates should recognize common sheet metal hand tools, cutting tools, forming tools, machines, measuring instruments, fastening methods, and fabrication equipment. Understanding what a tool is designed to do is more useful than simply memorizing tool names.

Safety represents three questions but remains an important part of professional sheet metal work. OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 can be used to review fall protection, ladders, scaffolding, personal protective equipment, material handling, cutting operations, and other construction hazards.

During practice, separate questions into two categories: questions that require trade knowledge and questions likely to be answered using the permitted code books. This approach can help candidates develop a realistic testing strategy. Since only the Uniform Mechanical Code and International Building Code are permitted as references, many trade questions must be answered from knowledge developed during preparation.

## How does the Sheet Metal Contractor (C-21C) exam work?

The Sheet Metal Contractor (C-21C) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
