## Exam Details

The Nevada C-3B Finish Carpentry trade examination is administered through PSI as part of Nevada's contractor licensing examination process. Candidates must first satisfy the applicable Nevada State Contractors Board qualification requirements before receiving examination eligibility.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-3B Finish Carpentry
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 30
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 21 correct answers

The C-3B examination is divided into eight technical subject areas:

- **Fabrication:** 6 questions
- **Installation:** 5 questions
- **Materials:** 5 questions
- **Finishing:** 4 questions
- **Hand Tools:** 3 questions
- **Safety:** 3 questions
- **Machines:** 2 questions
- **Plans and Estimating:** 2 questions

**Fabrication** is the largest single category. Candidates should review measurements, layout, cutting, fitting, assembly, cabinet components, millwork, joinery, and the methods used to fabricate finish-carpentry products accurately.

**Installation** should include cabinets, casing, baseboards, countertops, wooden flooring, trim, millwork, fastening, alignment, fitting, and installation sequences. Candidates should understand how finish materials are placed, adjusted, secured, and completed in a professional installation.

**Materials** preparation should include wood products, manufactured materials, fasteners, adhesives, hardware, cabinet components, trim products, and the characteristics that affect how those materials are fabricated, installed, and finished.

**Finishing** should include sanding, filling, surface preparation, coating concepts, final appearance, and methods used to prepare finish-carpentry work for a completed result.

**Hand Tools** and **Machines** require knowledge of proper selection, safe operation, common applications, and the relationship between tools, machines, materials, and tasks. Candidates should understand both function and safe use.

**Safety** should be studied with attention to construction hazards, personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolds, power tools, machines, electrical hazards, fall protection, housekeeping, and other jobsite practices relevant to finish carpentry.

**Plans and Estimating** should include dimensions, quantities, measurements, plan interpretation, material calculations, and basic estimating concepts. Even though this category contains only two questions, those questions can still affect the final score on a 30-question examination.

## How does the Finish Carpentry Contractor (C-3B) exam work?

The Finish Carpentry Contractor (C-3B) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
