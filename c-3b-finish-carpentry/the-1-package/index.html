Exam Details

The Nevada C-3B Finish Carpentry examination is administered through PSI as part of Nevada's contractor licensing examination program. Candidates must receive the applicable examination eligibility before scheduling the trade test.

  • Exam: Nevada C-3B Finish Carpentry
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Questions: 30
  • Time Allowed: 1 hour
  • Minimum Passing Score: 21 correct answers

The C-3B examination is organized into eight technical areas:

  • Fabrication: 6 questions
  • Installation: 5 questions
  • Materials: 5 questions
  • Finishing: 4 questions
  • Hand Tools: 3 questions
  • Safety: 3 questions
  • Machines: 2 questions
  • Plans and Estimating: 2 questions

Fabrication is the largest single content area. Candidates should review measuring, layout, cutting, fitting, assembly, cabinet components, millwork, joints, and techniques used to fabricate finish-carpentry products accurately.

Installation should include cabinets, casing, baseboards, countertops, wooden flooring, millwork, trim, alignment, fastening, fitting, and the procedures required to produce a finished installation.

Materials preparation should include wood products, manufactured materials, fasteners, adhesives, hardware, trim products, cabinet components, and the characteristics that affect fabrication and installation.

Finishing should include surface preparation, sanding, filling, coating concepts, final appearance, and methods used to prepare and complete finish-carpentry work.

The remaining categories still matter on a 30-question test. Candidates should review proper hand-tool use, safe machine operation, OSHA-based safety principles, plan reading, measurements, quantities, and basic estimating concepts.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-3B Finish Carpentry classification authorizes the installation, repair, and finishing of cabinets, sash casing, door casing, wooden flooring, baseboards, countertops, and other millwork.

The classification specifically excludes installation of ceramic tile, marble, and artificial or cultured stone. Contractors should understand the boundaries of the C-3B scope and operate within the classification for which the business is licensed.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, a trade qualifying individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the classification requested.

The qualifying experience generally must have been obtained within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Experience records should clearly demonstrate finish-carpentry work that relates to the C-3B classification.

Nevada contractor licensing also involves financial responsibility, applicable contractor bonding, background requirements, and additional Board conditions. Passing the C-3B examination does not by itself issue a contractor license.

Applicants generally must satisfy both the applicable trade qualification and contractor management requirements unless a waiver is approved. The contractor license is associated with the business entity, while qualifying individuals satisfy the applicable qualification roles.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong C-3B study plan should follow the examination outline instead of reviewing finish carpentry as one broad subject. Candidates can divide preparation into fabrication, installation, materials, finishing, tools, safety, machines, plans, and estimating.

Prioritize Fabrication. Six questions are assigned to fabrication, making it the largest individual category. Review measurements, layout, cutting, fitting, joinery, assembly, and cabinet or millwork components.

Study Installation carefully. Five questions focus on installation. Review cabinets, casing, flooring, baseboards, countertops, trim, millwork, fastening, fitting, alignment, and installation sequences.

Build material knowledge. Materials account for five questions. Study wood and manufactured products, fasteners, adhesives, hardware, trim materials, and the characteristics that affect finish-carpentry work.

Review Finishing. Four questions address finishing. Study surface preparation, sanding, filling, coating concepts, appearance, and methods used to complete finish woodwork.

Know hand tools and machines. Candidates should be familiar with appropriate tool selection, safe use, common machine operations, and the relationship between the tool, material, and task.

Prepare safety without relying on the book. OSHA is not available during the trade exam. Study construction hazards and safe work practices until the core principles can be applied independently.

Practice plans and estimating. Review dimensions, quantities, measurements, basic plan interpretation, material calculations, and estimating concepts relevant to finish-carpentry work.

Use timed practice. Thirty questions in one hour provides an average of about two minutes per question. Candidates should practice reading carefully, eliminating weak choices, and managing time without depending on reference lookups.

How does the Finish Carpentry Contractor (C-3B) exam work?

The Finish Carpentry Contractor (C-3B) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
