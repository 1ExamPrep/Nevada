## Exam Details

The Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling Contractor
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 3 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 56 correct answers

The examination covers the following subject areas:

- **Concrete:** 4 questions
- **Masonry:** 4 questions
- **Metals:** 3 questions
- **Carpentry:** 7 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 10 questions
- **Doors and Windows:** 5 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 17 questions
- **Tile:** 10 questions
- **Safety:** 10 questions
- **Plan Reading and Estimating:** 10 questions

**Drywall and Finishes** is the largest individual exam category with 17 questions. Candidates should review gypsum systems, wall and ceiling assemblies, finishing methods, materials, installation practices, and common interior remodeling applications.

**Carpentry** accounts for seven questions and may involve framing relationships, interior construction, finish work, openings, and related remodeling concepts. **Doors and Windows** contributes five questions, while **Concrete** and **Masonry** each account for four.

**Metals** is the smallest section with three questions, but candidates should still prepare for it because every correct answer contributes toward the passing score.

## State Requirements

The Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling subclassification authorizes remodeling and improvement of qualifying interior spaces in structures that support, shelter, or enclose persons, animals, or other property.

The classification includes important limitations. A B-6 contractor is not authorized to install, remove, or modify structural members, mechanical systems, fire protection systems, or other life-safety systems solely under the authority of the B-6 classification.

The limitations are important in both exam preparation and licensed practice. Commercial remodeling can involve many interior building trades, but work that extends into restricted structural or life-safety systems requires the proper licensing and qualified trade involvement.

Typical B-6 knowledge areas include drywall, finishes, tile, doors, windows, carpentry, moisture protection, concrete, masonry, metals, estimating, and plan reading. Candidates should understand how these systems and materials come together in a commercial interior remodeling project.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the qualifying individual generally must document at least four years of qualifying experience within the applicable experience period required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Applicants should maintain organized records describing the work performed, responsibilities held, project types, scope of work, and the period during which qualifying experience was obtained.

Passing the B-6 trade examination alone does not automatically issue a Nevada contractor license. Applicants must also satisfy applicable experience, management qualification, financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other licensing requirements.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A productive B-6 study plan should match the weighting of the examination. Drywall and Finishes deserves significant attention because it is the largest category, while Thermal and Moisture Protection, Tile, Safety, and Plan Reading and Estimating are also major sections.

**Prioritize drywall and finishes.** Study gypsum products, interior wall and ceiling systems, installation practices, finishing methods, materials, and typical commercial interior assemblies.

**Review thermal and moisture protection.** Understand insulation relationships, waterproofing, moisture control, sealants, and other protective systems used in commercial remodeling.

**Study tile thoroughly.** Review substrate preparation, setting materials, layout, installation methods, joint requirements, and practices involving ceramic, glass, and stone tile.

**Prepare for safety questions.** Use OSHA during study to reinforce fall protection, personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolds, tools, material handling, and common construction hazards. Because OSHA is not allowed into the B-6 examination, candidates should develop enough familiarity to answer safety questions without relying on the book.

**Practice plan reading and estimating.** Candidates should be comfortable reading dimensions, interpreting plans, calculating quantities, identifying materials, and working with basic estimating information.

**Review carpentry, doors, and windows.** Study framing relationships, interior carpentry, openings, installation concepts, hardware, and finish work.

**Use ACI 318-14 during preparation.** Become familiar with the organization of the concrete reference so you can find relevant requirements efficiently.

**Practice with ACI 530/530.1-13.** Learn where key masonry topics are located and use the highlighted and tabbed sections as part of regular study.

**Practice International Building Code navigation.** Focus on occupancy, egress, accessibility, fire-resistance, interior finishes, and other topics relevant to commercial remodeling.

**Use timed reference exercises.** Candidates have three hours for 80 questions. Practice identifying the proper reference and locating information efficiently instead of searching through multiple books unnecessarily.

## How does the Commercial Remodeling Contractor (B-6) exam work?

The Commercial Remodeling Contractor (B-6) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
