## Exam Details

The Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates who have received examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 80
- **Time Allowed:** 3 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 56 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The B-6 trade examination covers the following major subject areas:

- **Concrete:** 4 questions
- **Masonry:** 4 questions
- **Metals:** 3 questions
- **Carpentry:** 7 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 10 questions
- **Doors and Windows:** 5 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 17 questions
- **Tile:** 10 questions
- **Safety:** 10 questions
- **Plan Reading and Estimating:** 10 questions

Drywall and Finishes is the largest individual category, with 17 questions. Candidates should make gypsum systems, partitions, ceilings, assemblies, installation procedures, finishing methods, and commercial interior finish work a major part of their study plan.

Thermal and Moisture Protection, Tile, Safety, and Plan Reading and Estimating each contain 10 questions. Together, those four categories represent a substantial portion of the examination. A strong study plan should give each of them dedicated review time rather than treating them as secondary material.

Tile preparation can include substrates, layout, setting materials, mortar, grout, installation methods, tolerances, and accepted ceramic, glass, and stone tile practices. The included ANSI specifications, tile handbook, and Setting Tile reference provide focused study material for this portion of the exam.

Plan Reading and Estimating preparation can include construction drawings, dimensions, symbols, details, material quantities, measurements, takeoffs, and information commonly used to estimate commercial remodeling work.

Concrete, Masonry, Metals, Carpentry, and Doors and Windows make up the remaining examination categories. Although they contain fewer questions individually, each contributes toward the required passing score and should remain part of a complete B-6 preparation plan.

## State Requirements

The Nevada B-6 Commercial Remodeling classification covers remodeling and improvement of qualifying interior spaces in commercial structures. The classification can apply to structures exceeding three stories in height and certain lower structures connected to buildings exceeding three stories.

The B-6 scope does not authorize a contractor to install, remove, or modify structural members, mechanical systems, fire protection systems, or other life-safety systems. Work outside the B-6 authority must be performed under an appropriate contractor classification.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the trade qualifying individual generally must have a minimum of four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific trade category requested.

The four years of qualifying experience must generally have been gained within the 15 years immediately preceding the contractor application. Candidates should maintain organized experience records that demonstrate their background in the requested classification.

Nevada contractor applicants generally must also satisfy both trade and management qualification requirements. One individual may serve as both the Management and Trade Qualified Individual when qualified, or separate individuals may fill the two roles.

The Management Qualified Individual generally satisfies Nevada's Contractor Management Survey Business and Law requirement, while the Trade Qualified Individual establishes the applicable experience and trade examination qualifications.

Nevada contractor licensing also evaluates financial responsibility. The Board uses applicable financial information in determining the monetary limit associated with a license. Final licensing requirements also include the applicable contractor bond and other items required before the license becomes active.

## How does the Commercial Remodeling Contractor (B-6) exam work?

The Commercial Remodeling Contractor (B-6) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
