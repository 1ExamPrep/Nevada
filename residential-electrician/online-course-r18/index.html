Open Book Test

The Nevada Residential Electrician (ICC - R18) exam is an open book test. Open book testing allows candidates to use approved references during the exam, but it still requires steady preparation, accurate reference use, and strong time management. Candidates must be able to identify the topic, choose the correct reference, locate the controlling section, and apply the rule within the time limit.

The National Electrical Code Handbook, 2023 should be used as the main code reference during preparation. Candidates should practice using the table of contents, index, article layout, chapter structure, definitions, tables, notes, commentary, examples, and exceptions. Residential electrical questions may require quick movement between Article 100 definitions, branch circuit requirements, box fill rules, wiring method articles, grounding and bonding provisions, service rules, equipment articles, and special condition sections.

Open book success depends heavily on reference navigation. Candidates should practice looking up topics repeatedly until the process becomes familiar. A question about required receptacle outlets should lead toward dwelling unit branch circuit and outlet requirements. A question about box fill should lead toward the applicable box fill rules and conductor volume allowances. A question about grounding should lead toward the correct grounding or bonding rule instead of a general memory-based answer.

Timed practice is essential for open book readiness. Candidates should answer mixed-topic questions with the references open and a clock running. How does the Residential Electrician (ICC G18) exam work?

The Residential Electrician (ICC G18) card is part of the Electrical Qualifications ID card program run by Electrical Safety Professionals for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno. You pass the ICC national exam, then apply to ESP with the score report.

  • In Reno, the G17 and G18 exams need no pre-approval: you book them through ICC and send the score report to ESP.
  • Nevada has no state electrician license; Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno require an Electrical Qualifications ID card on every job site.
  • Electrical Safety Professionals (ESP) issues the cards, after you pass an ICC national exam: G17 journeyman, G16 master, G18 residential or G21 low voltage.
  • ICC electrician exams are open book with the NEC, and ICC offers them in more than one code edition, so match the edition to the exam you book.
  • Cards are valid for 3 years; one expired for more than a year means taking the exam again.
