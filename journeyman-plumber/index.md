## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber card is issued by a local plumbing board. The board approves you to test, then tells you how to book the open-book code exam; the card arrives after you pass.

- Nevada journeyman plumber cards are issued by local boards, not by the Contractors Board.
- In Reno the journeyman plumbing exam is open book and written to the 2024 Uniform Plumbing Code.
- Plumbing contractors also need an NSCB license, such as C-1 Plumbing and Heating or C-1D Plumbing, with the CMS exam.
- Packages here cover the 2024 IPC and the 2021 UPC, so check which code your local board tests before you order.

It comes from your local plumbing board, not the Contractors Board. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

No. Journeyman cards come from local plumbing boards. The NSCB licenses plumbing contractors, such as C-1 and C-1D, which also need the Business and Law exam.
