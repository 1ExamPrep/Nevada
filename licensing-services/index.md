## How does the NSCB License Application and Business Setup Services exam work?

The NSCB License Application and Business Setup Services side of a Nevada license is the same for every classification: the NSCB application, the Board's review of experience and finances, and the open-book CMS at PSI.

- The Business and Law exam (CMS) is required for every Nevada contractor license, whatever the classification.
- It is open book: the only reference allowed is the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada.
- The CMS has 60 scored questions in 120 minutes, and 45 right (75 percent) is a pass.
- The NSCB application fee is $300, and the Board sets your bond and monetary limit when it approves the license.

### Who issues the NSCB License Application and Business Setup Services license or card in Nevada?

It comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB), with exams by PSI. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### How many questions are on the Nevada Business and Law exam?

The CMS has 60 scored questions plus 3 unscored ones, with 120 minutes for the scored part. You need 45 correct, which is 75 percent.

### How many tries do I get?

Three. If you fail the third attempt the application becomes void, and you can apply again after 30 days.
