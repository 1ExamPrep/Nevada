## Exam Details

The Nevada C-17 Lathing and Plastering trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-17 Lathing and Plastering
- **Number of Questions:** 45
- **Time Allowed:** 1.5 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 32 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The C-17 examination content outline is divided into six major technical areas:

- **Safety:** 5 questions
- **Surface Preparation:** 11 questions
- **Drywall:** 5 questions
- **Application:** 10 questions
- **Base and Finish Coat:** 9 questions
- **Sheet Metal:** 5 questions

Surface Preparation is the largest category with 11 questions. Candidates should understand how substrates are inspected and prepared before lath, plaster, stucco, gypsum, or related materials are installed. Study should include cleaning, condition of the base, bonding considerations, moisture-related conditions, attachment surfaces, and the preparation steps required before finish materials are applied.

Application contains 10 questions and is another major part of the examination. Preparation should include hand and mechanical application methods, work sequence, plaster and stucco procedures, material handling, tools, and techniques used to create a consistent finished system.

Base and Finish Coat contains nine questions. Candidates should understand the relationship between base materials, plaster coats, finish coats, curing, preparation between coats, thickness considerations, and finishing techniques.

Safety contains five questions. Candidates should review construction safety involving scaffolds, ladders, personal protective equipment, tools, fall hazards, material handling, respiratory concerns, and other jobsite conditions associated with lathing, plastering, drywall, and stucco work.

Drywall contains five questions. Study should include gypsum wallboard and sheathing, installation concepts, suspended gypsum ceilings, finishing, accessories, and practical gypsum construction subjects relevant to the broader C-17 classification.

Sheet Metal contains five questions. Candidates should review sheet-metal studs used to frame ceilings and nonstructural partition walls and understand how these framing components relate to drywall and interior finish construction.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-17 Lathing and Plastering classification includes several related types of specialty construction work. Understanding the classification scope can help candidates connect exam preparation with the work they may perform under the license.

The lathing portion includes selecting and applying wood and metal lath and other materials designed to provide a suitable base for plaster coatings. Candidates should understand how the lath system supports the plaster application and why proper installation is important to the completed surface.

The plastering portion includes coating surfaces with plaster materials and installing lath or another suitable base for plaster. Preparation should include materials, mixing, application, base coats, finish coats, curing, and finishing procedures.

The classification also includes drywall work involving the installation and coating of gypsum wallboard and sheathing, including suspended ceiling systems made of gypsum wallboard.

Acoustical tile work within the classification includes installation of appropriate acoustical materials and preparation of the surface receiving the tile. Candidates should understand how this type of finish work fits within the broader C-17 scope.

The C-17 classification also includes coatings of stucco and cement applied by manual or mechanical methods. Surface preparation, appropriate bases, application methods, and finishing procedures are therefore important trade subjects.

Sheet-metal stud work includes the installation of sheet-metal studs used to frame ceilings and nonstructural partition walls. This area is represented directly on the trade examination.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the requested classification.

Nevada contractor applicants must also satisfy applicable financial responsibility, background, bonding, trade qualification, and management qualification requirements before the contractor license can be issued.

## How does the Lathing and Plastering Contractor (C-17) exam work?

The Lathing and Plastering Contractor (C-17) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
