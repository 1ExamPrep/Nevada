2. **Identify the qualifying individual.** Nevada contractor applicants must have an individual who satisfies the applicable management and trade qualification requirements. One person may serve in both qualifying roles, or separate individuals may fulfill the management and trade functions.
3. **Document qualifying trade experience.** The trade qualified individual must meet Nevada's experience requirement for the classification requested.
4. **Submit the contractor license application.** The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews the application and supporting documentation.

7. **Schedule required examinations through PSI.** Nevada generally requires the applicable Business and Law, also known as the CMS examination, and the trade examination for the requested classification unless an examination waiver applies.
8. **Complete remaining Board licensing requirements.** Passing an examination is one component of the overall licensing process. The Nevada State Contractors Board processes the licensing application and determines whether all requirements for issuance have been satisfied.

## State Requirements

Nevada contractor licensing is regulated by the Nevada State Contractors Board. For a trade qualified individual, the Board requires qualifying experience in the specific classification requested.

The trade qualified individual generally must have at least **four full years of experience** acquired within the **15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application**. Qualifying experience may include work as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the applicable classification.

Accepted education or training may be used toward a portion of the experience requirement under Nevada's licensing rules, subject to Board requirements. The Board reviews the applicant's submitted documentation and determines whether the qualifications satisfy the license classification requested.

The qualifying individual must also have an appropriate relationship with the applying business. Nevada provides for a qualifier to be an owner, officer, member, manager, or employee of the company, and the qualifier must be actively engaged in the contracting business.

Applicants should distinguish between the **trade qualified individual** and the **management qualified individual**. The management qualifier is responsible for the applicable construction management examination requirement, while the trade qualifier establishes the required technical trade experience and meets the applicable trade examination requirement. The same individual may perform both roles when qualified to do so.
