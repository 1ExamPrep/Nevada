## Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Nevada C-1K preparation plan should combine code navigation with practical industrial piping knowledge. Candidates should be able to recognize piping materials, system components, joining methods, valves, supports, equipment connections, and common installation practices.

**Plumbing Code Preparation** should center on the Uniform Plumbing Code. Practice locating requirements using the index and table of contents instead of reading the book only from beginning to end. Become familiar with definitions, materials, installation provisions, testing requirements, tables, and technical terminology.

**Mechanical Code Preparation** should center on the Uniform Mechanical Code. Candidates should practice finding piping-related and equipment-related requirements and understand how the mechanical code is organized.

**Process Piping** should be studied through ASME B31.3. Industrial piping candidates should develop familiarity with process piping terminology, materials, fabrication, joining, examination, testing, and the general technical concepts associated with piping used in industrial facilities.

Because ASME B31.3 is not allowed inside the examination center, candidates should use active recall. Review a section, close the book, and explain the concept or answer practice questions without consulting the reference.

**Welding** preparation should include welding processes, equipment, electrodes or filler materials, joint preparation, positions, inspection concepts, and safety. Modern Welding can help candidates build familiarity with welding terminology and methods encountered in industrial piping work.

**Trade Mathematics** should receive regular practice. Industrial piping work can require accurate measurements, fractions, decimals, geometry, elevations, offsets, angles, and other calculations. Work problems repeatedly until the process becomes familiar without depending on the mathematics book.

**Facility Piping Systems** should be studied for system arrangement, components, valves, supports, equipment connections, piping materials, and the way industrial piping operates as part of a larger facility.

**Mechanical Systems** preparation can be reinforced through Modern Refrigeration and Air Conditioning. Understanding pumps, controls, heat transfer, equipment, and circulating systems can strengthen a candidate's overall ability to interpret industrial piping questions.

**Safety** should be reviewed through OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926. Industrial piping work may involve welding, lifting, tools, ladders, scaffolds, equipment, material handling, and other hazards that require strong safety awareness.

For the two exam-approved books, candidates should practice timed lookups. Read a question, identify the main subject, decide whether the Uniform Plumbing Code or Uniform Mechanical Code is more appropriate, and then locate the applicable requirement.

## How does the Industrial Piping Contractor (C-1K) exam work?

The Industrial Piping Contractor (C-1K) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
