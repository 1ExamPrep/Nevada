## Exam Details

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for qualifying candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 60
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 42 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The C-15 examination covers roofing and siding subjects, including safety, roofing substrates, steep roofing, roofing repairs, elastomeric roofing, built-up bituminous roofing, siding knowledge, sheet metal, and flashing.

**Steep Roofing** is a major examination area. Candidates should review asphalt shingles, tile, slate, wood shakes and shingles, underlayments, roof decks, fastening, valleys, ridges, roof penetrations, flashing, drainage, and installation methods.

**Roofing Substrates** requires candidates to understand roof decks, sheathing, surface preparation, deterioration, structural conditions, fastening surfaces, and compatibility between roofing materials and the supporting substrate.

**Siding** is an important difference between the full C-15 classification and roofing-only preparation. Candidates should understand siding materials, installation principles, weatherproofing, fastening, joints, soffit applications, exterior-wall protection, and PVC siding installation concepts.

**Built-Up Bituminous Roofing** and **Elastomeric Roofing** require knowledge of low-slope systems, membranes, insulation, attachment, surfacing, flashing, drainage, repair methods, and roof-system performance.

**Roofing Repairs** may involve leaks, damaged roof coverings, deterioration, failed flashing, substrate problems, membrane damage, drainage concerns, and other conditions that reduce roof performance.

**Sheet Metal and Flashing** is essential to both water management and roof-system durability. Candidates should understand flashing at walls, roof edges, valleys, penetrations, curbs, transitions, and other vulnerable areas.

**Safety** should receive focused preparation because roofing and siding work can involve fall hazards, ladders, scaffolds, material handling, power tools, roof edges, elevated work, and other construction risks.

## State Requirements

Nevada designates C-15 as the Roofing and Siding specialty classification. A contractor holding the full C-15 classification may perform work authorized under the applicable C-15 subclassifications.

The roofing portion includes qualifying installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof roofing materials. These materials can include asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and photovoltaic roof tiles intended to serve in place of other roofing materials.

The siding portion includes qualifying installation, alteration, and repair of siding using materials such as wood, aluminum, enameled steel, plastic, and glass to make the walls of a structure watertight and weatherproof.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must establish four years of qualifying experience within the applicable experience period used by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Applicants should maintain organized experience documentation describing roofing and siding work performed, responsibilities held, and the period during which that experience was obtained.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes management qualification. The Management Qualified Individual generally satisfies the Contractor Management Survey and Business and Law requirement unless an approved waiver applies.

Financial responsibility, background requirements, contractor bonding, and other conditions are separate from the trade exam. Passing the C-15 examination alone does not complete the Nevada contractor licensing process.

## How does the Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) exam work?

The Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
