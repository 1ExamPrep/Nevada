## State Requirements

Nevada recognizes **C-15 Roofing and Siding** as a specialty contractor classification. The classification encompasses roofing and siding work and includes the work authorized under its applicable subclassifications.

The roofing portion includes installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof materials used on roofs. Roofing systems can include a variety of coverings and materials used to protect buildings from weather.

The siding portion covers installation, alteration, and repair of siding used to make exterior walls watertight and weatherproof. Candidates should understand how siding integrates with sheathing, openings, soffits, flashing, trim, and other exterior wall components.

The broader C-15 classification also encompasses related insulation and waterproofing work authorized under its subclassifications. Candidates should therefore understand the building envelope as a complete weather-protection system rather than treating roofs and exterior walls as unrelated components.

The C-15 trade examination is one part of Nevada contractor licensing. Applicants must also satisfy applicable qualification, application, business and law, financial, and other licensing requirements before a contractor license is issued.

## How does the Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) exam work?

The Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
