## Exam Details

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada State Contractors Board licensing process. A contractor applicant must first submit the required license application and receive examination eligibility before scheduling the trade examination.

The C-15 trade examination contains **60 questions**. Candidates must answer at least **45 questions correctly** to achieve the published minimum passing score. The examination allows **2 hours** for completion.

The published Nevada C-15 examination content outline includes:

- **Safety:** 5 questions.
- **Roofing Substrates:** 8 questions.
- **Steep Roofing:** 16 questions.
- **Roofing Repairs:** 4 questions.
- **Elastomeric Roofing:** 7 questions.
- **Built-up Bituminous Roofing:** 7 questions.
- **Sheet Metal and Flashing:** 3 questions.
- **Siding:** 8 questions.
- **Photovoltaic Systems:** 2 questions.

Steep roofing represents the largest individual portion of the examination, accounting for 16 of the 60 questions. Roofing substrates and siding each represent eight questions, while elastomeric roofing and built-up bituminous roofing each represent seven. Safety, roofing repairs, sheet metal and flashing, and photovoltaic systems make up the remainder of the published outline.

Candidates should prepare across the complete outline. A strong study strategy gives additional attention to the larger categories while still reviewing the smaller sections because questions can come from every published subject area.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor examination is an **open-book test**. The International Building Code, 2018 is identified as an approved reference for the C-15 examination room.

PSI requires candidates to bring their own approved reference material to the testing center. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Written notes are not permitted in the references, and candidates may not write in or add highlighting to the books during the examination session.

Permanent tabs may be used to organize approved examination references. Temporary or removable tabs are not permitted. Loose or attached additional papers are also prohibited under PSI reference-material rules.

Open-book testing does not eliminate the need to learn the trade. The C-15 examination is based on roofing and siding knowledge, general industry practices, and technical references. Candidates should understand common systems, materials, installation methods, repair practices, safety considerations, and code concepts before test day.

Reference navigation should be practiced along with technical review. When working with the International Building Code, become familiar with the index, chapter structure, definitions, tables, roof assembly provisions, exterior wall requirements, materials, and other sections connected to roofing and siding work.

## Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board licenses businesses and individuals that perform contracting work within the state. Candidates pursuing the C-15 classification must satisfy the licensing requirements applicable to the business and the qualifying individual.

1. **Select the C-15 Roofing and Siding classification.** Applicants seeking authority to perform work within the complete Nevada roofing and siding specialty apply for the C-15 classification.
2. **Identify the qualifying individual.** Nevada requires the applicant to identify a person who has the necessary experience, knowledge, and skills to qualify the business in the requested classification.
3. **Document qualifying trade experience.** The trade qualified individual must generally demonstrate at least four full years of applicable experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the requested classification within the 15 years immediately before the application is filed.
4. **Submit the contractor license application.** The applicant submits the required business, qualifying individual, experience, financial, and supporting information to the Nevada State Contractors Board.
5. **Receive examination eligibility.** The trade examination cannot be scheduled until the application has been submitted and the Board has issued examination eligibility.
6. **Complete the C-15 trade examination.** The trade qualifying individual must satisfy the Roofing and Siding examination requirement unless an applicable waiver has been approved.
7. **Complete the Business and Law requirement.** Nevada generally requires the appropriate qualifying individual to pass the Construction Management Survey business and law examination unless a qualifying waiver applies.
8. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** The applicant must satisfy the Nevada State Contractors Board's applicable financial responsibility, background, bonding, and other licensing requirements before the license can be issued.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-15 classification is the Roofing and Siding specialty classification. A contractor holding the full C-15 classification may perform work authorized within the associated roofing and siding subclassifications and related areas included in the classification scope.

The roofing portion of the classification includes installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof roofing materials. Nevada regulations identify materials such as asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and qualifying photovoltaic roof tiles.

The siding portion includes installation, alteration, and repair of siding materials used to make the walls of a structure watertight and weatherproof. Materials can include wood, aluminum, enameled steel, plastic, and glass. The broader C-15 scope also includes insulation and waterproofing work within the limits established by Nevada regulations.

The trade qualified individual generally must have four years of qualifying experience in the requested classification within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Qualifying experience may be obtained as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor.

Nevada does not require a specific college degree to obtain a contractor license. Accepted education or training may be used toward a portion of the experience requirement when it meets Nevada State Contractors Board standards and is properly documented.

The Nevada State Contractors Board also requires applicants to demonstrate financial responsibility. Contractor licensing can include financial statement requirements, bank verification, bonding, and other application documentation in addition to examination qualification.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Nevada C-15 study plan should follow the official examination weighting. Steep roofing deserves substantial attention because it accounts for 16 questions. Candidates should review steep-slope roof coverings, materials, slopes, underlayment, flashing, valleys, penetrations, installation practices, and related system components.

Roofing substrates and siding each account for eight questions. For roofing substrates, focus on the conditions and components that support roofing assemblies, including deck materials, preparation, compatibility, and how substrate conditions affect the completed system. For siding, review common siding materials, attachment methods, weather protection, installation principles, and exterior-wall considerations.

Elastomeric roofing and built-up bituminous roofing each account for seven questions. Candidates should understand basic system components, materials, installation methods, surfacing, flashing, common defects, and repair considerations. The NRCA membrane roofing material is particularly useful for developing familiarity with low-slope systems.

Safety is another dedicated examination category. Roofing and siding work frequently involves elevated work surfaces, ladders, scaffolds, material handling, fall hazards, and other construction risks. OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 provides an important foundation for reviewing federal construction safety requirements.

Sheet metal and flashing receive three questions. Although this is a smaller portion of the examination, flashing is closely connected to roofing performance. Candidates should understand how flashings, edges, penetrations, intersections, valleys, and similar details help control water movement.

The examination also includes photovoltaic systems. Candidates should review how rooftop photovoltaic components interact with roof assemblies, including penetrations, flashing, water management, roof access, system placement, and protection of the underlying roofing system.

Use practice questions to develop a consistent exam strategy. First identify the subject of the question. Determine whether it is testing trade knowledge, a code requirement, a safety principle, a material, an installation method, or a calculation. Then select the most appropriate reference or rely on established trade knowledge as appropriate.

Timed practice is also valuable. With 60 questions in two hours, candidates should avoid spending too much time searching for a single answer. Developing a disciplined process for answering, marking difficult questions, and returning to them can improve time management during preparation.

## How does the Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) exam work?

The Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
