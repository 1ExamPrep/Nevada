Exam Details

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for qualifying applicants pursuing licensure through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor
  • Number of Questions: 60
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Minimum Passing Score: 42 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The examination requires preparation across both roofing and siding work. Candidates should understand roofing substrates, steep roofing systems, roof repairs, membrane and low-slope systems, elastomeric roofing, built-up roofing, siding applications, sheet metal and flashing, safety, estimating, and relevant building-code requirements.

Steep-slope roofing should receive significant attention during preparation. Candidates should review shingles, tile, slate, wood shakes and shingles, roof decks, underlayments, fastening methods, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, drainage, and installation practices.

Roofing substrates are also important. Study should cover roof decks, sheathing, substrate condition, deterioration, surface preparation, compatibility with roofing materials, and how supporting construction affects the finished roofing system.

Siding distinguishes the full C-15 classification from roofing-only preparation. Candidates should review exterior siding systems, weatherproofing principles, fastening, joints, soffits, installation considerations, and PVC siding practices.

Membrane, built-up, elastomeric, metal panel, and SPF roofing systems should be part of the study plan. Candidates should understand materials, installation methods, attachment, flashing, insulation, drainage, repairs, and common system details.

Sheet metal and flashing are essential to weather-resistant exterior construction. Review flashing at roof edges, walls, valleys, transitions, curbs, penetrations, and other vulnerable locations where water intrusion can occur.

Safety should also be studied carefully because roofing and siding work can involve fall hazards, ladders, scaffolds, elevated work, material handling, tools, and other construction-site risks.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference included with this Ultimate package is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should use the IBC throughout preparation rather than waiting until the final days before testing. Practical navigation experience can make it easier to locate roofing, exterior-wall, weather-protection, material, and structural requirements under timed conditions.

An open-book exam still requires strong trade knowledge. With 60 questions and two hours available, candidates have an average of about two minutes per question. Effective preparation should combine direct knowledge with selective code lookups rather than relying on the book for every answer.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates contractor licensing for the C-15 Roofing and Siding classification. Passing the trade exam is one part of the complete licensing process.

  1. Confirm the C-15 classification. Determine that Roofing and Siding corresponds with the work the contracting business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the Trade Qualified Individual. Designate the person who will establish the required experience and technical qualification.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare the required experience documentation showing relevant work in the classification.
  4. Prepare the contractor license application. Organize business information, qualifier information, experience documentation, and supporting materials.
  5. Submit the application to the Nevada State Contractors Board. Examination eligibility is established after the application and experience have been reviewed.
  6. Complete applicable background requirements. Follow the required background and fingerprint procedures associated with Nevada contractor licensing.
  7. Address financial responsibility. Nevada contractor applicants must satisfy applicable financial responsibility requirements.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Once authorized, the qualifying individual can proceed with required testing.
  9. Schedule the C-15 trade examination through PSI. Arrange the Roofing and Siding Contractor examination after receiving eligibility.
  10. Prepare for the trade exam. Study roofing systems, siding, substrates, repairs, flashing, safety, estimating, photovoltaic considerations, and IBC navigation.
  11. Prepare for Nevada Business and Law. The included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for the applicable management examination.
  12. Complete required examinations. Nevada generally requires the applicable trade and Business and Law examinations unless the qualifier meets applicable waiver requirements.
  13. Complete final licensing requirements. Address applicable bonding, financial responsibility, and remaining Nevada State Contractors Board requirements.

State Requirements

Nevada contractor applicants must demonstrate that the business has a qualified individual with the experience and skills necessary for the classification being requested.

For the standard licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual must have at least four full years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the filing of the contractor license application. Qualifying experience can include work as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific classification requested.

Applicants should prepare detailed documentation describing the roofing and siding work performed, responsibilities held, and time periods represented by that experience. The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews experience as part of the application and examination eligibility process.

Nevada also requires a management qualification. The person serving as the Management Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable Business and Law examination requirement unless an authorized waiver applies.

Contractor licensing includes requirements beyond examination preparation. Financial responsibility is part of the licensing review, and Nevada contractor licenses require a contractor license bond. Background-related requirements and other application conditions may also apply.

The license belongs to the contracting business rather than to the qualifying individual personally. Nevada licenses may be issued to qualifying business structures including individuals, partnerships, corporations, limited liability companies, and joint ventures when applicable requirements are met.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Nevada C-15 study plan should balance roofing and siding preparation rather than concentrating exclusively on one area. Candidates pursuing the full classification need a working understanding of exterior weather protection across multiple systems.

Prioritize steep-slope roofing. Review shingles, tile, slate, wood shakes and shingles, underlayments, roof decks, fasteners, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, and drainage.

Study siding systems. Review siding materials, fastening methods, joints, weather protection, soffits, alignment, exterior-wall conditions, and PVC siding installation principles.

Review membrane and low-slope roofing. Study membrane systems, built-up bituminous roofing, elastomeric systems, metal panels, SPF systems, insulation, attachment, drainage, flashing, and repairs.

Prepare safety concepts for recall. Since OSHA is not allowed into the C-15 trade exam, candidates should study fall protection, ladder safety, scaffolding, PPE, material handling, and tool safety before test day.

Practice IBC navigation. Work regularly with the International Building Code and practice finding roofing and exterior-building requirements within a limited amount of time.

Review estimating. Study roof measurements, materials, quantities, waste considerations, and practical roofing estimating concepts.

Study rooftop photovoltaic conditions. Review how photovoltaic components can affect roofing penetrations, flashing, drainage, maintenance access, and overall roof-system performance.

Use timed practice. With 60 questions in two hours, candidates should develop a consistent pace and become comfortable deciding when a code lookup is useful and when direct trade knowledge is the faster approach.

How does the Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) exam work?

The Roofing and Siding Contractor (C-15) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
