Exam Details

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada State Contractors Board licensing process. Candidates must submit the contractor license application and receive examination eligibility before scheduling the required test.

The current C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor examination contains 60 questions. Candidates must answer at least 45 questions correctly to achieve the published passing score. The examination allows 2 hours for completion.

The published C-15 examination content outline includes:

  • Safety: 5 questions.
  • Roofing Substrates: 8 questions.
  • Steep Roofing: 16 questions.
  • Roofing Repairs: 4 questions.
  • Elastomeric Roofing: 7 questions.
  • Built-up Bituminous Roofing: 7 questions.
  • Sheet Metal and Flashing: 3 questions.
  • Siding: 8 questions.
  • Photovoltaic Systems: 2 questions.

Steep roofing is the largest individual portion of the examination, followed by roofing substrates and siding. Elastomeric roofing and built-up bituminous roofing are also significant categories. Candidates should nevertheless prepare across the entire outline because the test measures both roofing and siding knowledge.

Nevada contractor applicants must also satisfy the applicable business and law examination requirement. The Nevada Construction Management Survey, commonly called the CMS examination, covers contractor licensing, business management, estimating, bidding, contracts, project management, financial management, lien law, labor and tax subjects, risk management, and other areas involved in operating a contracting business.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding Contractor examination is an open-book test. The International Building Code, 2018 is the approved reference supplied with this package for use in the C-15 trade examination room.

The Nevada Construction Management Survey examination is also open book. The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada is the permitted reference supplied for that separate business and law examination.

PSI allows approved reference materials to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. References may not contain handwritten notes. Candidates also may not write in or add highlighting to the reference books during the examination session.

Permanent tabs are permitted. Temporary or removable tabs, including Post-It-style flags, are not permitted. Loose papers and additional attached materials are also prohibited under the examination reference rules.

Open-book testing should not be confused with an exam that can be completed by searching every answer from scratch. The C-15 trade exam contains 60 questions and allows two hours, so candidates need to manage their time carefully. Strong preparation includes learning the trade material while also becoming familiar with the organization of the International Building Code.

The highlighted and tabbed preparation included with this package supports that process. Candidates should practice using the prepared code book before exam day so that important chapters, sections, terminology, and tab locations become familiar.

Licensing Steps

  1. Select the Nevada C-15 Roofing and Siding classification. Applicants seeking to perform work within the full roofing and siding scope apply for the C-15 specialty contractor classification.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Nevada contractor licensing requires an appropriate qualified individual connected with the contracting business.
  3. Document qualifying trade experience. The trade qualified individual generally must document four full years of applicable experience within the 15 years immediately before the application is filed.
  4. Submit the Nevada contractor license application. The application is filed with the Nevada State Contractors Board along with the required qualifier, business, financial, experience, and other supporting information.
  5. Receive examination eligibility. Candidates cannot schedule the contractor examinations until the application has been submitted, the applicable experience has been verified, and examination authorization has been issued.
  6. Complete the C-15 trade examination. The trade qualified individual must satisfy the Roofing and Siding Contractor examination requirement unless an applicable waiver is approved.
  7. Complete the CMS business and law examination. The qualifying individual responsible for the management examination must satisfy Nevada's Construction Management Survey requirement unless an applicable waiver applies.
  8. Complete the remaining licensing requirements. Applicants must satisfy the Nevada State Contractors Board's applicable financial responsibility, bonding, and licensing requirements before the license is issued.

State Requirements

The Nevada State Contractors Board licenses contractors by classification based on the specific type of construction work the contractor will perform. C-15 is the Roofing and Siding specialty classification.

A contractor holding the full C-15 classification may perform work authorized under the C-15 roofing and siding subclassifications. Nevada's classification structure includes roofing, siding, insulation, and waterproofing work within the broader C-15 category.

The roofing portion includes the installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof roofing materials. The siding portion includes installation, alteration, and repair of siding materials used to make exterior walls watertight and weatherproof. The classification also encompasses insulation and waterproofing work within the scope defined by Nevada regulations.

The qualifying individual must meet Nevada's experience requirements. In general, the Board requires four full years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately before the contractor application is filed. Experience may include qualifying work performed as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor.

Nevada does not require a specific educational program to qualify for a contractor license. Certain acceptable education or training may be credited toward part of the required experience when documented and accepted by the Board.

The Nevada licensing process also includes financial responsibility requirements. The Board reviews financial documentation and uses applicable information when establishing the contractor's approved monetary limit.

A contractor bond is required before the license is issued. The required bond amount is determined through the Nevada State Contractors Board licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

C-15 exam preparation should address the complete roofing and siding scope rather than concentrating only on roofing. The current examination allocates eight questions specifically to siding, making siding knowledge an important distinction between the full C-15 classification and the more limited C-15A Roofing subclassification.

Steep roofing receives the greatest emphasis, with 16 questions. Candidates should devote substantial study time to steep-slope roofing systems, materials, installation principles, roof coverings, and related construction practices.

Roofing substrates are another major area. Candidates should understand how roof decks, substrate conditions, preparation, and material compatibility affect roofing installation. Elastomeric roofing and built-up bituminous roofing together account for 14 questions, so low-slope roofing systems should also receive substantial attention.

Siding preparation should include knowledge of exterior siding systems, materials, installation practices, weather protection, and related construction principles. Candidates should also review sheet metal and flashing because these components play an important role in directing water away from building assemblies and maintaining a weather-resistant exterior.

Safety is specifically tested. Roofing and siding work involves elevated work areas and other construction hazards, so candidates should include jobsite safety principles in their preparation. Photovoltaic roofing systems also appear in the published examination outline.

Use the International Building Code during study sessions. Practice moving through the index, chapter structure, definitions, tables, and relevant roofing and exterior-wall sections. When completing practice questions, identify whether the answer should come from trade knowledge or the approved code reference before beginning a lookup.

For the Nevada Construction Management Survey examination, use the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada during business and law preparation. Become familiar with the book's organization so subjects such as licensing, contracts, estimating, financial management, lien law, project management, and risk management can be located efficiently.
