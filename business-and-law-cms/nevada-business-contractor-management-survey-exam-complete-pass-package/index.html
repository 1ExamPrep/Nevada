Exam Details

The Nevada Contractor Management Survey exam is the general business and law examination used for Nevada contractor licensing. It is administered through PSI for the Nevada State Contractors Board. The exam is commonly referred to as the CMS exam, Contractor Management Survey exam, Business and Law exam, or Nevada Construction Business and Law exam.

The Nevada CMS exam contains 60 questions. Candidates have 120 minutes to complete the exam. The required passing score is 75%, which equals 45 correct answers. The exam is open book, and the approved reference is the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada.

The exam covers 10 major subject areas: licensing, estimating and bidding, lien law, financial management, tax laws, labor laws, project management, contracts, environmental and safety, and risk management. Candidates should be prepared to understand how these topics affect day-to-day contractor operations in Nevada.

Licensing content focuses on contractor licensing rules, application concepts, classifications, responsibilities, and compliance expectations. Contractor applicants should understand that licensing is not just a paperwork requirement. It affects how the business may operate, what work may be performed, and what responsibilities apply after the license is issued.

Estimating and bidding topics involve preparing bids, understanding scope, reviewing project documents, calculating costs, and recognizing how estimates affect contracts and project performance. Contractors must understand how business decisions made before a project begins can affect the entire job.

Lien law topics focus on payment rights, notices, documentation, and procedures related to construction payment claims. Financial management topics may include accounting concepts, records, cash flow, financial controls, payroll, and business administration. Tax law topics focus on contractor-related tax responsibilities and the importance of maintaining proper records.

Labor law topics involve employee relationships, wages, workplace obligations, and contractor responsibilities connected to workers. Project management topics focus on planning, scheduling, coordinating work, managing subcontractors, documenting progress, and completing projects in an organized way.

Contracts are a major part of contractor business practice. Candidates should understand written agreements, scope of work, change orders, payment terms, documentation, and how contract decisions can affect risk. Environmental and safety topics help candidates understand jobsite obligations, regulatory awareness, and safe operating practices. Risk management topics focus on reducing legal, financial, operational, and project-related exposure.

Licensing Steps

Nevada contractor licensing is handled through the Nevada State Contractors Board. Candidates should begin by identifying the license classification they are pursuing and confirming which examination or examinations are required for that classification.

The general licensing path includes completing the application process, meeting experience requirements, identifying the qualifying individual, providing required business and financial information, and passing required examinations. Most contractor applicants must pass the Contractor Management Survey exam in addition to any required trade exam for the classification being requested.

The Contractor Management Survey exam focuses on business and law topics, while trade exams focus on the technical scope of a particular classification. Candidates should make sure they are preparing for every exam required for their intended Nevada license classification.

How does the Business and Law Exam (CMS) exam work?

The Business and Law Exam (CMS) side of a Nevada license is the same for every classification: the NSCB application, the Board's review of experience and finances, and the open-book CMS at PSI.

  • The CMS has 60 scored questions in 120 minutes, plus 3 unscored ones, and you need 45 right (75 percent) to pass.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion and $140 for a trade exam and the CMS together.
  • The Business and Law exam (CMS) is required for every Nevada contractor license, whatever the classification.
  • The CMS is open book: the only reference allowed is the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada.
  • The CMS has 60 scored questions in 120 minutes, and 45 right (75 percent) is a pass.
