## Exam Details

The Nevada Contractor Management Survey examination is the general business and law examination used as part of the Nevada contractor licensing process. Candidates who are required to complete contractor examinations may need to pass the CMS exam in addition to the trade examination associated with the requested license classification.

- **Exam:** Nevada Contractor Management Survey
- **Common Name:** CMS Examination
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Scored Questions:** 60
- **Scored Testing Time:** 120 minutes
- **Non-Scored Questions:** 3
- **Additional Time for Non-Scored Questions:** 6 minutes
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 45 correct scored answers, or 75%

The scored examination covers the following subject areas:

- **Licensing:** 10 questions
- **Estimating and Bidding:** 7 questions
- **Lien Law:** 3 questions
- **Financial Management:** 12 questions
- **Tax Laws:** 5 questions
- **Labor Laws:** 5 questions
- **Project Management:** 3 questions
- **Contracts:** 6 questions
- **Risk Management:** 4 questions
- **Environmental and Safety:** 5 questions

**Financial Management** is the largest individual content category with 12 questions. Candidates should be familiar with contractor financial concepts, costs, cash flow, overhead, financial statements, business records, and the financial responsibilities involved in operating a contracting business.

**Licensing** accounts for 10 questions. This area covers contractor licensing responsibilities, classification concepts, qualifying-party issues, license requirements, and other Nevada contractor regulatory topics.

**Estimating and Bidding** represents seven questions. Candidates should review estimating principles, labor and material costs, overhead, bid preparation, and other factors involved in preparing construction proposals.

**Contracts** accounts for six questions. Preparation should include contract fundamentals, responsibilities of the parties, documentation, changes, payment issues, and common construction contract concepts.

Tax Laws, Labor Laws, Environmental and Safety, Risk Management, Project Management, and Lien Law make up the remaining scored questions and should all be included in a balanced study plan.

## Licensing Steps

The Contractor Management Survey is one part of the larger Nevada contractor licensing process. The exact requirements vary depending on the classification and applicant circumstances, but the general pathway includes application, qualification review, examinations when required, and completion of the remaining licensing requirements.

1. **Select the appropriate contractor classification.** Determine which Nevada contractor classification matches the type of work the business intends to perform.
2. **Identify the qualifying individual.** Designate the person whose experience and qualifications will support the license application.
3. **Document qualifying experience.** Prepare the experience information required for the requested classification.
4. **Prepare the Nevada contractor license application.** Gather business information, qualifier information, financial documentation, and required supporting materials.
5. **Submit the application to the Nevada State Contractors Board.** The Board reviews the application and determines whether the applicant satisfies the applicable qualification requirements.
6. **Receive examination eligibility when testing is required.** Candidates may proceed with exam scheduling after the application has been reviewed and eligibility has been established.
7. **Schedule the required examinations.** Candidates may need to take the Contractor Management Survey and the applicable trade examination.
8. **Prepare for the CMS examination.** Study the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada and review licensing, estimating, finance, contracts, lien law, taxes, labor, risk, project management, environmental requirements, and safety.
9. **Prepare for the trade examination.** Study the technical topics and approved references associated with the requested contractor classification.
10. **Complete the applicable examinations.** Satisfy the required business and law and trade examination requirements.
11. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** Address applicable financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other Nevada State Contractors Board requirements.
