## Exam Details

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination is divided into two primary content areas:

- **Code Requirements:** 4 questions
- **Metal Framing Installation:** 16 questions

**Metal Framing Installation** is the dominant portion of the C-17F examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should devote most of their technical study time to sheet metal stud framing principles and practical installation knowledge.

Preparation should include identifying metal framing components, understanding how studs and tracks work together, establishing layouts, working around openings, maintaining alignment, coordinating framing with ceilings and nonstructural partitions, and understanding how framing interacts with gypsum and other building materials.

Candidates should also understand practical construction relationships. Metal studs are part of complete assemblies, so exam preparation should include the role of framing in wall systems, ceiling systems, partitions, and adjoining construction. Familiarity with construction terminology can help candidates interpret questions more efficiently.

**Code Requirements** account for four questions. Although this is the smaller exam section, it remains important because the International Building Code is the approved exam-room reference. Candidates should be able to identify the subject of a code-related question and locate the relevant provision efficiently.

The C-17F exam allows one hour for 20 questions, giving candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. A strong test strategy combines direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups instead of attempting to search the IBC for every answer.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Nevada C-17F study plan should reflect the weighting of the examination. Since 16 of the 20 questions focus on Metal Framing Installation, most technical preparation should concentrate on framing knowledge and installation methods.

**Prioritize metal framing installation.** Review studs, tracks, layouts, alignment, connections, openings, framing component relationships, ceiling framing, nonstructural partition walls, and practical installation methods.

**Understand complete assemblies.** Metal studs are part of larger wall and ceiling systems. Candidates should study how framing interacts with gypsum materials, tracks, openings, adjoining construction, and finished assemblies.

**Strengthen framing fundamentals.** Carpentry and Building Construction can reinforce layout, measurements, construction sequencing, framing terminology, and general building relationships that support metal framing preparation.

**Study gypsum construction.** Use the Gypsum Construction Handbook to reinforce understanding of partitions, wall systems, ceilings, and the relationship between gypsum products and sheet metal framing.

**Practice IBC navigation.** Code Requirements account for four exam questions. Use the International Building Code during study sessions and practice locating applicable provisions under timed conditions.

**Study the non-approved books for recall.** Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook cannot be used during the trade exam, so candidates should learn the important concepts from those references before test day.

**Use timed practice.** The exam allows one hour for 20 questions. Timed question sets can help candidates build a consistent pace and make better decisions about when a code lookup is useful.

**Review mistakes by category.** If missed questions involve installation, direct additional study toward metal framing. If mistakes involve code requirements, increase IBC navigation practice.

**Connect study material to field experience.** Candidates with practical construction experience can strengthen preparation by relating exam topics to actual framing layouts, wall and ceiling assemblies, installation sequences, measurements, and jobsite conditions.

## How does the Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) exam work?

The Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
