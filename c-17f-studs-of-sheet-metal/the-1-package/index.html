Exam Details

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal
  • Number of Questions: 20
  • Time Allowed: 1 hour
  • Minimum Passing Score: 14 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The examination is divided into two primary content areas:

  • Code Requirements: 4 questions
  • Metal Framing Installation: 16 questions

Metal Framing Installation represents the majority of the examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should devote most of their technical preparation to metal stud framing principles and practical installation knowledge.

Preparation should include identifying framing components, understanding stud and track relationships, establishing layouts, working around openings, coordinating framing with wall and ceiling assemblies, and understanding how nonstructural metal framing is installed as part of a complete building system.

Candidates should also be comfortable with construction terminology and practical relationships between sheet metal studs, tracks, gypsum assemblies, ceilings, partitions, adjoining walls, and other building components. The goal is to understand the system rather than memorize isolated terms.

Code Requirements account for four questions. Although this is the smaller exam category, it remains important because the International Building Code is the approved exam-room reference. Candidates should practice locating applicable requirements and using the code efficiently.

The exam allows one hour for 20 questions, giving candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. A strong test strategy combines direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups so candidates do not lose unnecessary time searching for every answer.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference included with this package is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should use the IBC regularly throughout preparation. Becoming familiar with the structure of the code makes it easier to identify where a requirement is likely to be located when a code-related question appears during the examination.

The open-book format should not be treated as a substitute for trade knowledge. Metal Framing Installation represents 16 of the 20 exam questions, so candidates should be prepared to answer many questions directly from their understanding of the trade.

Practice sessions should combine technical questions with timed code-navigation exercises. When a question involves a building-code requirement, candidates can practice identifying the subject, locating the appropriate section, and confirming the answer without spending excessive time searching.

Carpentry and Building Construction supports broader framing and construction knowledge, including layouts, measurements, component relationships, construction sequencing, and building methods. The Gypsum Construction Handbook supports understanding of partitions, ceilings, gypsum wall systems, fire-rated assemblies, and the relationship between gypsum construction and metal framing.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal subclassification authorizes the erection and installation of studs made of sheet metal for the framing of ceilings and nonstructural partitioning walls.

The scope is specific to sheet metal stud framing used for ceilings and nonstructural partitions. Candidates should understand this classification boundary when preparing for licensing and when determining the type of contracting work the business intends to perform.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must have at least four years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application.

Qualifying experience may include applicable work performed as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the classification requested. Applicants should maintain organized records describing the work performed, responsibilities held, and the period during which the experience was obtained.

Nevada contractor licensing also requires management qualification. Applicants generally must satisfy the applicable Contractor Management Survey and Business and Law requirements unless an authorized waiver applies.

Financial responsibility review, contractor bonding, background requirements, and other licensing conditions are separate from the C-17F trade examination. Passing the trade exam alone does not complete the contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-17F study plan should reflect the actual examination weighting. Since 16 of the 20 questions focus on Metal Framing Installation, most technical study time should be devoted to framing knowledge and installation practices.

Prioritize metal framing installation. Review metal studs, tracks, layouts, component relationships, installation methods, connections, openings, alignment, and the role of framing within complete ceiling and partition systems.

Understand nonstructural partition framing. The C-17F scope specifically covers sheet metal studs used for ceilings and nonstructural partitioning walls. Candidates should connect exam preparation directly to this authorized scope.

Review framing fundamentals. Carpentry and Building Construction can reinforce layouts, measurements, sequencing, terminology, and general construction principles that support metal framing preparation.

Study gypsum assemblies. Use the Gypsum Construction Handbook to understand wall systems, ceilings, partitions, fire-rated assemblies, and how gypsum products interact with metal framing.

Practice IBC navigation. Code Requirements account for four questions. Use the International Building Code during study sessions and practice finding relevant information under timed conditions.

Learn study-only material for recall. Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook cannot be taken into the trade exam, so candidates should focus on understanding important concepts before test day.

Use timed practice questions. The exam contains 20 questions with one hour available. Timed practice can help candidates become comfortable with pacing and make better decisions about when a code lookup is necessary.

Review missed questions by category. If mistakes are concentrated in installation questions, focus additional study on metal framing. If code questions create difficulty, increase IBC navigation practice.

How does the Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) exam work?

The Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
