## Exam Details

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination is divided into two primary content areas:

- **Code Requirements:** 4 questions
- **Metal Framing Installation:** 16 questions

**Metal Framing Installation** is the dominant portion of the C-17F examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should devote most of their technical study time to understanding metal framing systems and practical installation methods.

Preparation should include identifying studs and tracks, understanding framing layouts, coordinating framing around openings, maintaining alignment, working with ceiling framing and nonstructural partitions, and recognizing how framing interacts with gypsum products and other building components.

Candidates should also develop familiarity with general construction terminology. Questions may require more than simple recall of component names. Understanding how framing members work together as part of a complete assembly can make it easier to interpret practical trade questions.

**Code Requirements** account for four questions. Although this is the smaller exam category, these questions should not be overlooked. The International Building Code, 2018 is the approved exam-room reference, so candidates should practice locating relevant provisions before test day.

The examination allows one hour for 20 questions, giving candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. A productive test strategy combines direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups. Candidates who try to search for every answer can quickly lose valuable time.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal examination is an **open-book test**. The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is:

Candidates should use the IBC regularly throughout preparation. The goal is not to memorize the entire code but to understand its organization well enough to locate relevant requirements when needed.

Effective reference navigation begins with recognizing what a question is asking. Candidates should identify the general subject, determine where that subject is likely to appear in the IBC, and locate the applicable provision without unnecessary searching.

A productive Nevada C-17F study plan should reflect the weighting of the examination. With 16 of the 20 questions devoted to Metal Framing Installation, most technical study time should be directed toward framing knowledge.

## How does the Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) exam work?

The Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
