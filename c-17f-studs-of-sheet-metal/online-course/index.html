About this Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) package

Prepare for the Nevada Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) examination with online exam prep focused on metal framing installation, building-code requirements, gypsum construction, framing fundamentals, construction terminology, and efficient reference navigation.

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal subclassification is part of Nevada's C-17 Lathing and Plastering classification. The authorized scope covers the erection and installation of studs made of sheet metal for framing ceilings and nonstructural partitioning walls. Candidates pursuing this specialty should be comfortable with practical metal framing methods, complete wall and ceiling assemblies, and the building-code requirements that apply to this work.

The C-17F trade examination places most of its emphasis on Metal Framing Installation. Candidates should understand framing components, stud-and-track relationships, layouts, openings, alignment, connections, ceiling framing, nonstructural partition walls, gypsum-related systems, and the relationship between metal framing and adjoining construction.

The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is the International Building Code, 2018. Because the C-17F examination is open book, candidates should develop strong familiarity with the organization of the IBC and practice locating applicable requirements efficiently.

Two additional technical study references are Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 and the Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition. These books support preparation involving framing fundamentals, construction layouts, measurements, gypsum assemblies, partitions, ceilings, installation practices, and related building concepts. These two references are not allowed into the C-17F trade examination.

The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada is included to support preparation for the Nevada Contractor Management Survey and Business and Law requirements associated with contractor licensing.

Online exam prep gives candidates a structured way to separate the technical material into manageable areas. Because Metal Framing Installation represents most of the examination, candidates can devote the majority of their preparation to framing while continuing to practice code navigation and Nevada Business and Law material.

Exam Details

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal
  • Number of Questions: 20
  • Time Allowed: 1 hour
  • Minimum Passing Score: 14 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The examination is divided into two primary content areas:

  • Code Requirements: 4 questions
  • Metal Framing Installation: 16 questions

Metal Framing Installation is the dominant portion of the C-17F examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should devote most of their technical study time to understanding metal framing systems and practical installation methods.

Preparation should include identifying studs and tracks, understanding framing layouts, coordinating framing around openings, maintaining alignment, working with ceiling framing and nonstructural partitions, and recognizing how framing interacts with gypsum products and other building components.

Candidates should also develop familiarity with general construction terminology. Questions may require more than simple recall of component names. Understanding how framing members work together as part of a complete assembly can make it easier to interpret practical trade questions.

Code Requirements account for four questions. Although this is the smaller exam category, these questions should not be overlooked. The International Building Code, 2018 is the approved exam-room reference, so candidates should practice locating relevant provisions before test day.

The examination allows one hour for 20 questions, giving candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. A productive test strategy combines direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups. Candidates who try to search for every answer can quickly lose valuable time.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should use the IBC regularly throughout preparation. The goal is not to memorize the entire code but to understand its organization well enough to locate relevant requirements when needed.

Effective reference navigation begins with recognizing what a question is asking. Candidates should identify the general subject, determine where that subject is likely to appear in the IBC, and locate the applicable provision without unnecessary searching.

The open-book format does not eliminate the need for technical knowledge. Metal Framing Installation represents most of the examination, so candidates should be ready to answer many questions from their understanding of the trade rather than relying entirely on the code book.

The following technical study references are not allowed into the C-17F trade examination:

  • Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016
  • Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition

The books should be used before test day to strengthen framing knowledge and understanding of complete building assemblies. Because they cannot be taken into the examination, important concepts should be learned for recall.

Carpentry and Building Construction supports broader preparation involving layouts, measurements, construction sequencing, building components, framing relationships, and general construction principles.

The Gypsum Construction Handbook supports preparation involving gypsum wall systems, ceilings, partitions, framing relationships, and the way metal studs function within completed assemblies.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-17F Studs of Sheet Metal subclassification authorizes the erection and installation of studs made of sheet metal for framing ceilings and nonstructural partitioning walls.

The classification is specific to sheet metal stud framing used for these applications. Candidates should understand the classification scope when preparing for licensing and when determining whether C-17F corresponds with the work their contracting business intends to perform.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must have at least four years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application.

Qualifying experience should relate to the classification requested and demonstrate practical involvement in the trade. Applicants should maintain organized records describing the work performed, responsibilities held, and the period during which the experience was obtained.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes management qualification. Applicants generally must satisfy the applicable Contractor Management Survey and Business and Law requirements unless an authorized waiver applies.

The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for the management side of contractor licensing and should be studied separately from the technical C-17F trade material.

Financial responsibility review, contractor bonding, background procedures, and other licensing conditions are separate from the trade examination. Passing the C-17F exam alone does not complete the Nevada contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Nevada C-17F study plan should reflect the weighting of the examination. With 16 of the 20 questions devoted to Metal Framing Installation, most technical study time should be directed toward framing knowledge.

Prioritize metal framing installation. Review sheet metal studs, tracks, layouts, alignment, connections, openings, ceiling framing, partition walls, and practical installation methods.

Understand the C-17F scope. The classification focuses on sheet metal studs used to frame ceilings and nonstructural partitioning walls. Connecting exam preparation directly to this scope can help candidates stay focused on relevant trade concepts.

Understand complete assemblies. Metal framing is part of larger systems. Study how studs and tracks interact with gypsum products, openings, ceiling systems, partitions, and adjoining construction.

Strengthen general framing knowledge. Carpentry and Building Construction can reinforce measurements, layouts, framing terminology, construction sequencing, and general building relationships that support C-17F preparation.

Study gypsum construction. The Gypsum Construction Handbook can help candidates understand wall assemblies, ceilings, partitions, and the relationship between gypsum materials and metal framing.

Practice IBC navigation. Code Requirements represent four examination questions. Candidates should practice finding applicable provisions in the International Building Code under timed conditions.

Study non-approved references for recall. Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook cannot be used in the trade examination. Important information from these references should be understood before test day.

Use timed practice. The exam allows one hour for 20 questions. Timed exercises can help candidates build a comfortable pace and decide when a code lookup is necessary.

Review missed questions by topic. If mistakes involve framing installation, focus additional study on components, layouts, or installation methods. If mistakes involve code-related material, spend more time practicing IBC navigation.

Connect preparation to field experience. Candidates with hands-on framing experience can reinforce study by relating questions to actual layouts, wall assemblies, ceiling framing, openings, measurements, and installation sequences encountered in the field.

Prepare for Business and Law separately. The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports management exam preparation and should be studied alongside, but separately from, the C-17F technical exam material.

How does the Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) exam work?

The Studs of Sheet Metal Contractor (C-17F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.
