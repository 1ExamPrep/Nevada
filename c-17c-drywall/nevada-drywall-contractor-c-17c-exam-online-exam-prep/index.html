Exam Details

The Nevada C-17C Drywall trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-17C Drywall
  • Number of Questions: 20
  • Time Allowed: 1 hour
  • Minimum Passing Score: 14 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The examination content is divided into four technical subject areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions
  • Materials: 4 questions
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions

Installation and Taping and Finishing are the two largest categories, with six questions each. Together, these subjects represent the majority of the examination, so candidates should give them substantial attention during preparation.

Installation study should include the handling and placement of gypsum wallboard, layout, cutting, fastening, attachment methods, joints, openings, ceilings, and other procedures associated with properly installed drywall systems. Candidates should understand how drywall work interacts with supporting construction and how installation decisions affect the completed system.

Taping and Finishing preparation should include joint treatment, joint tape, finishing compounds, corners, surface preparation, application sequences, and the procedures used to prepare gypsum board surfaces for completion. Candidates should understand both the materials and the practical steps involved in finishing drywall.

The Materials category includes four questions. Candidates should become familiar with gypsum board products, sheathing, fasteners, compounds, trims, accessories, and other materials associated with drywall systems. Understanding how and where materials are used is more valuable than memorizing product names without understanding their application.

Special Applications and Accessories also includes four questions. Candidates should review specialty gypsum conditions, ceilings, trims, accessories, assemblies, and other applications that extend beyond basic wallboard installation.

Because the examination contains only 20 questions, every question has a meaningful impact on the final result. Candidates should avoid ignoring smaller content areas simply because they contain fewer questions. A balanced preparation strategy should cover all four categories while giving extra attention to Installation and Taping and Finishing.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-17C Drywall trade examination is open book with a specific approved reference permitted in the examination center.

The approved exam-room reference is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

The International Building Code supports preparation for code provisions involving gypsum board, gypsum panel products, interior construction, fire-resistance-rated assemblies, materials, and other building requirements that may apply to drywall systems.

Candidates should practice using the IBC throughout their online preparation. Useful navigation skills include working with the index, chapter organization, definitions, numbered sections, tables, exceptions, and cross-references. The goal is not simply to own the correct code book, but to know how to locate the relevant information efficiently during a timed examination.

The following references are study materials and are not allowed into the C-17C trade examination:

  • Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016
  • Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th Edition

The books should be used before testing to build practical trade knowledge. Candidates can study wall and ceiling construction, framing relationships, gypsum products, attachment methods, taping, finishing, assemblies, and accessories so these concepts can be applied without referring to the books in the examination room.

An open-book examination still requires preparation. With one hour available for 20 questions, inefficient reference searches can use valuable testing time. Candidates should learn which subjects are best answered from trade knowledge and which types of questions may require an International Building Code lookup.

Repeated code-navigation practice can help candidates become familiar with where information is located and reduce unnecessary searching. Using the IBC during study sessions also helps connect technical drywall concepts with the code provisions that govern construction requirements.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates contractor licensing for the C-17C Drywall classification. Applicants must complete the applicable licensing requirements before the license can be issued.

  1. Confirm the C-17C classification. Determine that the Drywall classification matches the type of contracting work the business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Designate the person who will satisfy the trade qualification requirements for the business.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare acceptable records demonstrating relevant drywall experience in the requested classification.
  4. Prepare the contractor license application. Gather the required business, qualifying-party, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application to the Nevada State Contractors Board. The Board reviews the application and supporting qualifications before examination eligibility is issued.
  6. Complete applicable fingerprint and background requirements. Follow the background procedures required during the Nevada contractor licensing process.
  7. Provide applicable financial information. Nevada evaluates financial responsibility as part of the contractor licensing process.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Eligible candidates may proceed with testing after the Board reviews the required qualifications.
  9. Schedule the C-17C examination through PSI. Arrange the trade examination after examination authorization has been issued.
  10. Prepare for the drywall trade examination. Review Installation, Taping and Finishing, Materials, Special Applications and Accessories, and International Building Code navigation.
  11. Prepare for the contractor management requirement. Nevada contractor applicants generally must also satisfy the Contractor Management Survey Business and Law requirement unless an applicable waiver is approved.
  12. Complete the required examinations. Meet the applicable trade and management testing requirements.
  13. Complete final licensing requirements. Address the applicable financial responsibility, bonding, and remaining Board requirements before license issuance.

Candidates should treat exam preparation and application preparation as related but separate tasks. A strong study plan can help with the technical examination, while organized documentation helps support the licensing application process.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-17C Drywall classification is a specialty contractor classification focused on drywall work. Contractors should operate within the scope of the classification for which they are licensed and understand the limitations of that classification before contracting for work.

The C-17C classification covers the installation and coating of gypsum wallboard and sheathing. The scope also includes the installation of suspended ceiling systems made of gypsum wallboard.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific trade category requested.

The qualifying experience must generally have been obtained within the 15 years preceding the application. Applicants should maintain organized documentation showing the type of work performed, the period during which it was performed, and the relationship between that work and the C-17C classification.

Nevada contractor licensing uses both management and trade qualifications. One person may serve as both the Management Qualified Individual and Trade Qualified Individual when the applicable requirements are satisfied, or the business may use separate qualified individuals for those roles.

The Trade Qualified Individual establishes the necessary experience and trade qualification for the C-17C classification. The Management Qualified Individual generally satisfies the contractor Business and Law requirement unless an applicable waiver is approved.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes financial responsibility and bonding requirements. These requirements are part of the broader licensing process and are separate from passing the C-17C trade examination.

Exam preparation should therefore be viewed as one important component of the overall licensing process. Candidates should prepare for the technical test while also making sure their experience documentation and other licensing materials are organized for Board review.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-17C study plan should reflect the examination content outline and the fact that only the International Building Code is available for approved reference use during the trade exam.

Prioritize Installation. Installation accounts for six questions. Review layout, board handling, cutting, fastening, attachment, walls, ceilings, openings, joints, and practical installation procedures.

Give equal attention to Taping and Finishing. The category also contains six questions. Study tapes, joint compounds, corners, finishing sequences, surface preparation, and the steps involved in completing gypsum board surfaces.

Learn the materials. Materials account for four questions. Review gypsum panel types, sheathing, fasteners, compounds, trims, accessories, and other products used in drywall construction.

Review Special Applications and Accessories. The section contains four questions. Study suspended gypsum wallboard ceilings, specialty assemblies, trims, accessories, and conditions that extend beyond basic wall installation.

Practice International Building Code navigation. Use the IBC index, chapters, definitions, sections, and tables during study sessions. Practice locating information under time pressure so navigation becomes more efficient.

Use Carpentry and Building Construction for context. The reference can help reinforce framing, wall and ceiling construction, and related building fundamentals that influence drywall installation.

Use the Gypsum Construction Handbook for trade knowledge. Focus on gypsum materials, wallboard systems, fastening, installation, taping, finishing, ceilings, and accessories. Since the book is not allowed into the exam, use repeated review to strengthen retention.

Work with practice-oriented questions. Practice questions can reveal whether a missed answer resulted from a weak technical concept, an unfamiliar material, a finishing procedure, or slow code navigation.

Review missed questions by category. If a candidate repeatedly misses Installation questions, additional installation review is more productive than randomly repeating the entire course. The same approach can be used for Finishing, Materials, Special Applications, and IBC navigation.

Practice with the one-hour time limit in mind. The C-17C examination contains 20 questions. Timed review can help candidates develop a comfortable pace and determine how much time they can reasonably spend on individual code lookups.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-17C) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-17C) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
