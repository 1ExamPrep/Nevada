## Exam Details

The Nevada C-17C Drywall trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-17C Drywall
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination content is divided into four technical subject areas:

- **Installation:** 6 questions
- **Taping and Finishing:** 6 questions
- **Materials:** 4 questions
- **Special Applications and Accessories:** 4 questions

Installation and Taping and Finishing are the two largest categories, with six questions each. Together, these subjects represent the majority of the examination, so candidates should give them substantial attention during preparation.

Installation study should include the handling and placement of gypsum wallboard, layout, cutting, fastening, attachment methods, joints, openings, ceilings, and other procedures associated with properly installed drywall systems. Candidates should understand how drywall work interacts with supporting construction and how installation decisions affect the completed system.

Taping and Finishing preparation should include joint treatment, joint tape, finishing compounds, corners, surface preparation, application sequences, and the procedures used to prepare gypsum board surfaces for completion. Candidates should understand both the materials and the practical steps involved in finishing drywall.

The Materials category includes four questions. Candidates should become familiar with gypsum board products, sheathing, fasteners, compounds, trims, accessories, and other materials associated with drywall systems. Understanding how and where materials are used is more valuable than memorizing product names without understanding their application.

Special Applications and Accessories also includes four questions. Candidates should review specialty gypsum conditions, ceilings, trims, accessories, assemblies, and other applications that extend beyond basic wallboard installation.

Because the examination contains only 20 questions, every question has a meaningful impact on the final result. Candidates should avoid ignoring smaller content areas simply because they contain fewer questions. A balanced preparation strategy should cover all four categories while giving extra attention to Installation and Taping and Finishing.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific trade category requested.

## How does the Drywall Contractor (C-17C) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-17C) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
