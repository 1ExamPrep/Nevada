## How do you send us a message?

Fill in the form and we will get back to you. You can also email [sales@1examprep.com](mailto:sales@1examprep.com). For anything urgent about an exam date coming up soon, calling [(877) 775-9400](tel:+18777759400) is the quickest way to reach a person.

## What should you include so we can answer faster?

A short message with the right details saves a round of back and forth. Put in whichever of these apply:

- The license or NSCB classification you are going for, such as B-2 Residential and Small Commercial, C-2 Electrical or the Business and Law (CMS) exam.
- Your exam date, if PSI or ICC has already booked it, so we can tell you which packages arrive in time.
- Your order number, for questions about shipping, a rental return or logging in to an online course.
- Whether you need books you can take into the exam room, or study material only.

## Which questions can we help with?

We answer questions about the packages on this site: what each one includes, which code editions the books cover, how rentals and returns work, and how online courses and practice exams are delivered. We can also explain the licensing services and The 1 Package, which add help with the NSCB application and the business setup.

Some things only the boards can do. The [Nevada State Contractors Board](https://www.nvcontractorsboard.com/) approves applications and sets your bond and monetary limit, and [PSI](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/nvcon) schedules the contractor exams. Electrician ID cards for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno come from Electrical Safety Professionals after an ICC exam.

## What do people ask before they contact us?

### Can you tell me which Nevada license I need?

We can point you to the right page. Tell us the work you do and the jobs you want to bid, and we will name the NSCB classification that usually fits, such as B-2 or C-15A. The Contractors Board makes the final call when it reviews your application.

### Can you book my PSI exam or send my application?

We cannot book the exam for you. The NSCB approves your application and sends the eligibility letter, then you schedule with PSI. If you want help with the paperwork itself, the licensing services and The 1 Package include the NSCB application.
