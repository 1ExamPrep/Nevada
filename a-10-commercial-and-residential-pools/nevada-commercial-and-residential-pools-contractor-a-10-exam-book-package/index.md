## Exam Details

The Nevada A-10 Commercial and Residential Pools trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada A-10 Commercial and Residential Pools
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 75
- **Time Allowed:** 2.5 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 53 correct answers

The examination content is divided into the following subject areas:

- **Filtration/Circulation Systems:** 12 questions
- **Maintenance and Operation:** 12 questions
- **Accessories and Associated Trades:** 8 questions
- **Pool and Spa Business Laws and Rules:** 3 questions
- **Concrete, Shotcrete and Gunite:** 15 questions
- **Design:** 16 questions
- **Soils and Earthwork:** 9 questions

**Design** is the largest examination category with 16 questions. Candidates should understand pool and spa layout, dimensions, circulation relationships, equipment considerations, structural concepts, accessibility of components, hydraulic relationships, and practical design factors associated with commercial and residential installations.

**Concrete, Shotcrete and Gunite** accounts for 15 questions. Candidates should review concrete placement, shotcrete application, reinforcement relationships, preparation, curing, finishing, construction quality, and practices used in pool shells and related structures.

**Filtration/Circulation Systems** and **Maintenance and Operation** each account for 12 questions. These categories require familiarity with pumps, filters, circulation, water movement, equipment, operational considerations, maintenance practices, and the systems used to keep pools and spas operating properly.

**Soils and Earthwork** represents nine questions. Candidates should understand excavation, site conditions, soil considerations, grading relationships, preparation, and other earthwork concepts that affect pool construction.

**Accessories and Associated Trades** accounts for eight questions. Candidates should review equipment and related construction activities that interact with the pool or spa installation.

**Pool and Spa Business Laws and Rules** accounts for three questions. Candidates should understand applicable regulatory concepts associated with Nevada pool and spa contracting.

Residential pool and spa contractors in Nevada are also subject to additional consumer-protection requirements. These requirements operate alongside the general contractor licensing rules and are important for businesses performing residential pool and spa work.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the qualifying individual generally must document at least four years of qualifying experience within the applicable experience period required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

## How does the Commercial and Residential Pools Contractor (A-10) exam work?

The Commercial and Residential Pools Contractor (A-10) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
