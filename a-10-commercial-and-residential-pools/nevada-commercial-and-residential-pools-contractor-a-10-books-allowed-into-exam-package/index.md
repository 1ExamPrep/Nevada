## Exam Details

The Nevada A-10 Commercial and Residential Pools trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada A-10 Commercial and Residential Pools
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 75
- **Time Allowed:** 2.5 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 53 correct answers

The examination content is divided into several major subject areas:

- **Filtration/Circulation Systems:** 12 questions
- **Maintenance and Operation:** 12 questions
- **Accessories and Associated Trades:** 8 questions
- **Pool and Spa Business Laws and Rules:** 3 questions
- **Concrete, Shotcrete and Gunite:** 15 questions
- **Design:** 16 questions
- **Soils and Earthwork:** 9 questions

**Design** is the largest individual category on the A-10 examination. Candidates should understand pool and spa layout, system relationships, equipment considerations, dimensional requirements, practical planning, and other design concepts used in residential and commercial installations.

**Concrete, Shotcrete and Gunite** is another major category. Candidates should review pool-shell construction, placement methods, reinforcement relationships, preparation, curing, finishing, and quality practices associated with concrete and shotcrete work.

**Filtration/Circulation Systems** requires knowledge of pumps, filters, piping, water movement, circulation patterns, system components, and equipment relationships. Candidates should understand how water moves through a pool or spa system and how the major components work together.

**Maintenance and Operation** focuses on the practical operation of pools and spas, including equipment performance, circulation, filtration, routine service, and other operational considerations.

**Soils and Earthwork** includes excavation, site preparation, grading relationships, soil conditions, and other factors that affect the construction and stability of a pool installation.

**Accessories and Associated Trades** covers related components and construction work that interact with pool and spa installations, while **Pool and Spa Business Laws and Rules** addresses applicable regulatory topics.

## How does the Commercial and Residential Pools Contractor (A-10) exam work?

The Commercial and Residential Pools Contractor (A-10) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
