## Exam Details

The Nevada C-4F Sheet Metal Studs trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-4F Sheet Metal Studs
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination is divided into two primary subject areas:

- **Code Requirements:** 4 questions
- **Metal Framing Installation:** 16 questions

**Metal Framing Installation** is the dominant portion of the C-4F examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should spend most of their technical study time reviewing sheet metal stud framing concepts, installation methods, component relationships, layout, connections, construction terminology, and practical framing principles.

Metal framing work requires candidates to understand how framing components work together as part of a complete wall, partition, or ceiling assembly. Preparation should include the relationship between studs, tracks, openings, gypsum systems, adjoining construction, and other elements that affect proper installation.

Candidates should also develop familiarity with common framing layouts, measurements, alignment, spacing concepts, construction sequencing, and the practical responsibilities associated with installing metal framing systems.

**Code Requirements** account for four questions. Although this is the smaller portion of the exam, candidates should not ignore it. The International Building Code, 2018 is the approved exam-room reference and should be used throughout preparation to develop efficient navigation skills.

The exam allows one hour for 20 questions, giving candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. Strong preparation should combine direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups so time is not lost searching the IBC for information that can be answered from knowledge.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-4F study plan should reflect the weighting of the examination. Since 16 of the 20 questions focus on Metal Framing Installation, the majority of technical preparation should concentrate on framing concepts and installation knowledge.

**Prioritize metal framing installation.** Review the components, terminology, layout principles, installation methods, connections, and construction relationships associated with sheet metal stud framing.

**Understand framing layouts.** Candidates should be comfortable with how framing is arranged and how studs, tracks, openings, and adjoining construction work together within a complete assembly.

**Review construction fundamentals.** Carpentry and Building Construction can reinforce layouts, measurements, construction sequencing, framing relationships, and general building knowledge that supports sheet metal framing preparation.

**Study gypsum-related assemblies.** The Gypsum Construction Handbook can help candidates understand partitions, ceilings, wall systems, and the role metal framing plays in completed gypsum assemblies.

**Practice IBC navigation.** Four exam questions cover Code Requirements, and the International Building Code is the approved exam-room reference. Practice locating applicable provisions within a limited amount of time.

**Study non-approved books for recall.** Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook are not allowed into the examination. Candidates should learn the concepts contained in these references before test day.

**Use timed practice.** The exam allows one hour for 20 questions. Timed review can help candidates develop a comfortable pace and reduce the temptation to spend too much time on a single code lookup.

**Review mistakes by category.** If missed questions involve installation, direct additional study toward metal framing. If mistakes involve code-related material, increase IBC navigation practice.

**Connect technical knowledge to real installation.** Candidates with field experience can strengthen preparation by connecting study material to actual framing situations, component relationships, measurements, layouts, and completed assemblies.

## How does the Sheet Metal Studs Contractor (C-4F) exam work?

The Sheet Metal Studs Contractor (C-4F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
