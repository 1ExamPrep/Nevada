## Exam Details

The Nevada C-4F Sheet Metal Studs trade examination is administered through PSI for eligible candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-4F Sheet Metal Studs
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination is divided into two primary subject areas:

- **Code Requirements:** 4 questions
- **Metal Framing Installation:** 16 questions

**Metal Framing Installation** is the dominant portion of the examination, accounting for 16 of the 20 questions. Candidates should devote most of their technical study time to the practical principles associated with sheet metal stud framing.

Preparation should include framing layouts, component identification, installation methods, alignment, connections, construction sequencing, openings, adjoining construction, and the relationship between metal framing and complete wall or ceiling assemblies.

Candidates should understand how metal studs and tracks work together, how framing is laid out, and how completed assemblies interact with gypsum products and other building components. Practical construction knowledge can help candidates recognize the intent behind exam questions rather than relying entirely on memorized terminology.

**Code Requirements** account for four questions. Although this section is smaller, it should not be overlooked. The International Building Code is the approved exam-room reference, so candidates should become comfortable locating applicable provisions and understanding how code requirements relate to framing systems.

The examination allows one hour for 20 questions, which provides an average of approximately three minutes per question. Candidates should practice balancing direct trade knowledge with selective code lookups. Spending too much time searching the IBC for every answer can create unnecessary time pressure.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-4F Sheet Metal Studs examination is an **open-book test**. The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is:

- **International Building Code, 2018**

Open-book testing is most effective when candidates already understand the structure of the reference. The goal is not to read the IBC from beginning to end during the examination. Instead, candidates should know where different types of requirements are generally located and practice finding specific information efficiently.

Code-navigation practice should be part of regular study sessions. Candidates can begin with a framing or building-code question, identify the likely code topic, find the relevant provision, and confirm the answer within a limited amount of time.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Nevada C-4F study plan should reflect the weighting of the examination. With 16 of the 20 questions devoted to Metal Framing Installation, the majority of technical study time should be directed toward framing knowledge.

**Prioritize metal framing installation.** Review studs, tracks, framing layouts, component relationships, installation methods, alignment, openings, connections, and practical construction principles.

**Understand complete assemblies.** Metal framing does not function by itself. Study how framing interacts with gypsum systems, openings, adjoining construction, ceilings, partitions, and other building components.

**Strengthen framing fundamentals.** Use Carpentry and Building Construction to reinforce measurements, layouts, sequencing, framing terminology, and broader building relationships that can support C-4F preparation.

**Study gypsum construction.** Use the Gypsum Construction Handbook to understand partitions, wall assemblies, ceilings, and the relationship between gypsum materials and metal framing.

**Practice IBC navigation.** Code Requirements represent four exam questions, and the International Building Code is the approved exam-room reference. Practice locating relevant requirements under timed conditions rather than simply reading the code passively.

**Study the non-approved books for recall.** Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook cannot be used during the trade examination. Important concepts from these books should be learned before test day.

**Use timed practice.** The exam contains 20 questions with one hour available. Timed question sets can help candidates develop a steady pace and become more comfortable deciding when a code lookup is necessary.

**Review mistakes by category.** If missed questions involve framing installation, focus additional study on installation methods and component relationships. If mistakes involve code requirements, spend more time navigating the International Building Code.

**Connect study material to field experience.** Candidates with practical construction experience can strengthen preparation by connecting framing questions to real installations, layouts, assemblies, measurements, and construction sequencing.

**Prepare for the Business and Law requirement separately.** The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports management exam preparation and should be studied alongside the C-4F technical material rather than treated as part of the trade exam reference set.

## How does the Sheet Metal Studs Contractor (C-4F) exam work?

The Sheet Metal Studs Contractor (C-4F) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.

- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
