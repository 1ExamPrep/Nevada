Exam Details

The Nevada B General Building examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada B General Building Contractor
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Questions: 80
  • Time Allowed: 3 hours
  • Minimum Passing Score: 56 correct answers

The examination is divided into multiple trade and construction-management subject areas:

  • Sitework: 6 questions
  • Concrete: 12 questions
  • Masonry: 6 questions
  • Metals: 12 questions
  • Carpentry: 7 questions
  • Thermal and Moisture Protection: 9 questions
  • Doors and Windows: 4 questions
  • Drywall and Finishes: 4 questions
  • Safety: 5 questions
  • Plan Reading and Estimating: 12 questions
  • Nevada One Call: 3 questions

Concrete, Metals, and Plan Reading and Estimating are the largest sections, with 12 questions each. Candidates should dedicate significant preparation time to these areas while maintaining broad knowledge across the remaining categories.

Concrete preparation should include forming, reinforcement, placement, finishing, structural requirements, quality control, and post-tensioning fundamentals. Masonry should include brick, block, stone, mortar, reinforcement, installation methods, and structural masonry requirements.

Metals should include structural steel, steel joists, joist girders, handling, erection, connections, and related construction practices. Carpentry preparation should include framing, structural wood components, layout, assemblies, and common construction methods.

Plan Reading and Estimating requires candidates to interpret drawings, dimensions, details, quantities, specifications, and construction information while applying practical estimating and calculation skills.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates the B General Building classification. Applicants must satisfy the applicable qualification, examination, financial, background, and licensing requirements.

  1. Confirm the B classification. Make sure General Building matches the type of projects the business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Designate the person who will satisfy the applicable trade qualification requirements.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare acceptable records showing General Building construction and supervisory experience.
  4. Prepare the Nevada contractor application. Gather the required business, qualifier, experience, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application. Provide the required materials for Nevada State Contractors Board review.
  6. Complete applicable background requirements. Follow the required fingerprint and background procedures.
  7. Provide required financial information. Nevada evaluates financial responsibility as part of contractor licensing.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Qualified applicants may proceed with required testing after the applicable review.
  9. Prepare for the B trade examination. Review sitework, concrete, masonry, metals, carpentry, moisture protection, doors, windows, drywall, safety, plans, estimating, and Nevada One Call.
  10. Prepare for Business and Law. Use the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada for contractor management preparation.
  11. Complete the applicable examinations. Satisfy trade and contractor management requirements unless an approved waiver applies.
  12. Complete final licensing requirements. Address applicable bonding, financial responsibility, and remaining Board conditions.
  13. Establish the business entity. Business Formation for an LLC or corporation is included with The 1 Package.
  14. Obtain the EIN. EIN Filing with the IRS is included for the new contracting business.

State Requirements

A Nevada General Building contractor is generally associated with construction or remodeling projects that involve three or more unrelated building trades or crafts, with the General Building contractor serving as the prime contractor and building construction or remodeling being the primary purpose of the project.

The B classification should not be treated as unlimited authority to perform every specialty trade. Plumbing, electrical, refrigeration and air-conditioning, and fire-protection work require the applicable specialty licensing authority.

For the standard Nevada licensing pathway, the trade qualifying individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the classification requested.

The qualifying experience generally must have been obtained within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Experience documentation should clearly show work relevant to General Building construction and supervision.

Nevada contractor licensing also involves financial responsibility, background requirements, and contractor bonding. These obligations are separate from passing the trade examination.

How does the General Building Contractor (B) exam work?

The General Building Contractor (B) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
