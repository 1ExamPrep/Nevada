  • ACI 318-14: Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete and Commentary
    A structural concrete reference used to study concrete design requirements, reinforcement concepts, concrete members, strength provisions, detailing, durability, and commentary connected to structural concrete construction.
  • ACI 530/530.1-13 (2011 or 2013): Building Code Requirements and Specification for Masonry Structures and Companion Commentaries
    A masonry structures reference used to study masonry code requirements, specifications, materials, construction provisions, reinforcement, structural masonry concepts, and companion commentary.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    A construction safety reference used to study OSHA standards for construction work, including fall protection, scaffolding, excavation safety, personal protective equipment, equipment safety, material handling, hazard communication, and jobsite safety responsibilities.
  • Technical Digest No. 9 – Handling and Erection of Steel Joists and Joist Girders
    A steel joist reference used to study safe handling, erection, placement, bracing, stability, and field practices related to steel joists and joist girders.
  • Pipe and Excavation Contracting
    A trade reference used to study excavation methods, trenching, pipe installation, site preparation, bedding, backfilling, compaction, drainage, equipment considerations, and underground construction practices.
  • Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition
    A gypsum construction reference used to study gypsum board systems, installation methods, framing coordination, fire-resistance assemblies, finishing, ceilings, partitions, and construction details.
  • Suggested Marking Guidelines and The Nevada One Call Law
    A utility marking and damage prevention reference used to study marking practices, excavation notification concepts, utility location responsibilities, and Nevada One Call requirements.
  • Training and Certification of Field Personnel for Unbonded Post-Tensioning - Level 1 Field Fundamentals
    A post-tensioning reference used to study field fundamentals, tendon placement, stressing concepts, safety awareness, installation practices, and post-tensioning procedures.
  • Modern Masonry - Brick, Block, Stone, Clois E. Kicklighter 10th edition
    A masonry construction reference used to study brick, block, and stone materials; tools; layout; bonding; mortar; reinforcement; flashing; installation methods; and masonry construction practices.
  • The Contractor's Guide to Quality Concrete Construction - 4th Edition
    A concrete construction reference used to study quality concrete practices, materials, placement, finishing, curing, testing concepts, troubleshooting, and construction quality control.

How does the General Building Contractor (B) exam work?

The General Building Contractor (B) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
