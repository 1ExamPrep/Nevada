## Exam Details

The Nevada B General Building trade examination is administered by PSI for the Nevada State Contractors Board. The examination contains **80 questions**, requires **56 correct answers** to meet the listed passing score, and allows **180 minutes** for completion.

The official examination content is broad because the B classification applies to general building construction involving multiple unrelated building trades or crafts. The current content distribution includes:

- **Site Work — 6 questions:** Preparation may involve construction-site considerations, excavation, grading, layout, and related site-work knowledge.
- **Concrete — 12 questions:** Candidates should understand concrete construction, placement, reinforcement, structural requirements, and applicable code provisions.
- **Masonry — 6 questions:** The area covers masonry construction principles and requirements applicable to general building work.
- **Metals — 12 questions:** Candidates should be familiar with metal components, structural applications, and construction practices.
- **Carpentry — 7 questions:** The portion evaluates knowledge of framing, wood construction, and related carpentry practices.
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection — 9 questions:** Candidates should understand materials and methods used to protect building assemblies from weather, water, and thermal transfer.
- **Doors and Windows — 4 questions:** The area addresses installation and construction considerations involving openings, doors, and windows.
- **Drywall and Finishes — 4 questions:** Candidates should be prepared for questions involving interior and exterior finishing systems.
- **Safety — 5 questions:** Construction safety knowledge forms an important part of the examination.
- **General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating — 12 questions:** Candidates should be able to understand applicable building-code information, interpret construction documents, and apply estimating principles.
- **Nevada One Call — 3 questions:** The examination also addresses Nevada requirements associated with excavation notification and underground utility safety.

Concrete, Metals, and General Code, Plan Reading and Estimating are among the largest sections of the examination, with 12 questions in each category. Together they account for a substantial portion of the test. A balanced study plan should still address every listed subject because the Nevada B examination evaluates the broad knowledge expected of a general building contractor.

## Licensing Steps

The Nevada B General Building examination is one part of the contractor licensing process administered by the Nevada State Contractors Board. Applicants should understand the general licensing sequence while preparing for testing.

1. **Select the B General Building classification.** The requested license classification should correspond to the type and scope of construction work the business intends to perform.
2. **Identify the qualifying individual.** The applicant must have a qualifying individual with the required skills and experience to qualify the business in the requested classification.
3. **Document qualifying experience.** The trade qualifying individual must provide documentation demonstrating the experience required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.
4. **Submit the contractor license application.** The completed application and applicable supporting documentation must be submitted to the Board.
5. **Receive examination eligibility.** The Board reviews the application and verifies the qualifying experience before providing the Examination Eligibility form needed to schedule testing.
6. **Schedule the PSI examinations.** Nevada contractor licensing examinations are administered through PSI.
7. **Prepare for the B trade and CMS examinations.** Candidates should study the applicable construction topics and practice using the approved references associated with each examination.
8. **Complete the required examinations.** The qualifying individual generally must satisfy the B trade examination and Business and Law examination requirements unless an applicable waiver is approved.
9. **Complete the remaining licensing requirements.** The Nevada State Contractors Board evaluates additional application and licensing requirements before a contractor license is issued.

## State Requirements

Nevada defines a **Class B General Building Contractor** as a contractor whose principal contracting business involves the construction or remodeling of buildings or structures intended for the support, shelter, and enclosure of persons, animals, chattels, or movable property. The work requires the use of more than two unrelated building trades or crafts, with the general building contractor acting as the prime contractor and construction or remodeling of the building serving as the primary purpose.

The trade qualifying individual generally must have at least **four full years of qualifying experience** during the **15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application**. Qualifying experience can include work performed at the journeyman level or as a foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the classification requested.

Nevada does not impose a general degree requirement for obtaining a contractor license. Training completed through an accredited college or university, or an equivalent program accepted by the Board, may be used to satisfy up to three years of the experience requirement when properly documented.

The qualifying individual must possess the knowledge and skills necessary to manage the daily activities of a construction business, including field supervision, or the applying business must be represented by an appropriately qualified individual who fulfills that role.

Nevada generally requires a qualifying individual to complete the Business and Law examination and the trade examination specific to the classification requested unless the requirements for an examination waiver are met. Applicants for the full B General Building classification may also qualify for a trade examination waiver when the applicable requirements associated with the NASCLA Accredited General Building examination are satisfied.

Examination eligibility is not issued simply by purchasing exam preparation materials. The contractor application must first be submitted to the Nevada State Contractors Board, and the applicable experience requirement must be verified before the qualifying individual receives the information needed to schedule the Nevada examinations.

## How does the General Building Contractor (B) exam work?

The General Building Contractor (B) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
