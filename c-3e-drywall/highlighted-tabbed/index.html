Exam Details

The Nevada C-3E Drywall examination contains 30 questions. Candidates have 90 minutes to complete the test, and the minimum passing score is 21 correct answers.

The examination is divided into six primary content areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.
  • Code Requirements: 3 questions.
  • Metal Framing Installation: 7 questions.

Metal Framing Installation is the largest individual category on the C-3E exam. Installation and Taping and Finishing also make up significant portions of the test. Candidates should still prepare for every category because the exam requires knowledge across the full drywall trade outline.

With 30 questions and 90 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. That makes both trade knowledge and efficient use of the permitted reference important. Spending too much time searching for one answer can reduce the time available for the rest of the exam.

A strong study strategy should combine topic review with reference navigation. Candidates should understand the material well enough to answer many questions without a lookup while also knowing how to use the International Building Code efficiently when a code reference is needed.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-3E Drywall examination is an open-book test. The International Building Code, 2018 is the reference allowed inside the examination center.

Although the examination is open book, not every reference used for study can be taken into the testing room. Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 and the Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition are not allowed in the exam.

This makes preparation especially important. Candidates should use the non-exam-room books before test day to build knowledge in drywall systems, construction practices, materials, installation, finishing, metal framing, and related trade topics.

The International Building Code should be used differently because it can enter the examination room. During your preparation, practice navigating the index, chapter structure, tables, and code sections so that locating information becomes more natural.

The highlighted and tabbed format can help support this process by making important areas easier to locate while studying. Becoming familiar with the physical organization of the book before the exam can help you use your available testing time more efficiently.

Test Information and Study Materials

The C-3E examination covers a broad range of drywall and construction subjects. Candidates should organize their study schedule around all six content categories rather than focusing on only one or two areas.

Metal Framing Installation contains seven questions and is the largest individual category. Review the terminology, components, systems, and installation concepts associated with metal framing used in drywall construction.

Installation contains six questions. Candidates should study drywall systems, installation methods, components, and practical trade terminology.

Taping and Finishing also contains six questions. Preparation should include the materials, procedures, sequence of work, and terminology associated with completing drywall surfaces.

Materials contains four questions. Review common products, components, accessories, and the appropriate uses of materials encountered in drywall and gypsum construction.

Special Applications and Accessories contains four questions. Study specialized drywall applications and the accessories used in different systems and conditions.

Code Requirements contains three questions. Because the International Building Code, 2018 is permitted in the examination room, candidates should practice locating code information efficiently.

The highlighted and tabbed International Building Code can support faster study navigation. Use the tabs to move between important sections while still learning the structure of the code. Do not rely only on the tabs; becoming familiar with the index and chapter layout can make you more flexible when a question is worded differently than expected.

Use Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 to reinforce broader construction concepts. Since this book cannot be brought into the exam room, your goal should be to understand and retain the material before test day.

The Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition can help reinforce drywall-specific knowledge. Review installation practices, materials, gypsum systems, finishing, and related topics as part of your preparation.

Practice questions are most useful when they reveal what you need to study next. If you miss a question, return to the appropriate book and review the underlying concept rather than memorizing only the correct answer.

Reference-navigation practice should also be part of your routine. Choose a topic, locate it in the International Building Code, and practice finding the relevant section efficiently. Repeat difficult searches until you become comfortable with where information is located.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-3E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-3E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
