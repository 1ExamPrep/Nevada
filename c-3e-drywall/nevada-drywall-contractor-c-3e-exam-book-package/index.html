Exam Details

The Nevada C-3E Drywall examination contains 30 questions. Candidates are given 90 minutes to complete the exam, and the minimum passing score is 21 correct answers.

The examination content is divided into six primary subject areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.
  • Code Requirements: 3 questions.
  • Metal Framing Installation: 7 questions.

Metal Framing Installation is the largest single category on the examination with seven questions. Installation and Taping and Finishing each contain six questions, making those three areas especially important during preparation. Materials and Special Applications and Accessories each account for four questions, while Code Requirements accounts for three.

The 90-minute testing period gives candidates an average of approximately three minutes per question. That makes familiarity with the subject matter important. Spending too much time searching for one answer can reduce the time available for the rest of the test, so preparation should include both knowledge review and efficient reference navigation.

A useful study strategy is to work through each content area individually before combining them in mixed practice. This helps you identify whether you need additional preparation in installation, finishing, materials, metal framing, specialty applications, or code-related topics.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-3E Drywall examination is an open-book test, but only the approved reference may be taken into the examination center. The International Building Code, 2018 is the reference allowed in the exam room.

The fact that the test is open book does not mean every study reference can be used during the examination. Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 and the Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition are study references but are not allowed in the examination center.

This makes advance study particularly important. Information learned from the books that cannot enter the testing center needs to become part of your working trade knowledge before test day. Candidates should not plan on looking up every trade question during the examination.

The International Building Code should be approached differently. Since it is permitted inside the examination center, candidates should practice navigating it before the test. Become familiar with the index, chapter organization, tables, section numbering, and terminology so that finding information becomes more efficient.

Open-book testing rewards familiarity with the approved reference. The more comfortable you are moving through the International Building Code, the easier it can be to locate information when a code-related question requires research during the exam.

Test Information and Study Materials

The C-3E examination requires preparation across several areas of drywall construction. Because Metal Framing Installation has seven questions, candidates should give this area substantial attention. Review the terminology, components, and installation concepts associated with metal framing used in drywall systems.

Installation contains six questions. This area should be approached with an understanding of how drywall materials and systems are installed, the terminology used in the trade, and the practical considerations that affect installation work.

Taping and Finishing also contains six questions. Candidates should study the materials, processes, terminology, and sequence associated with finishing gypsum wallboard surfaces. Practical understanding is important because these questions may require more than simple recognition of a term.

Materials accounts for four questions. Preparation should include familiarity with common drywall and gypsum products, accessories, and components and an understanding of their intended applications.

Special Applications and Accessories accounts for four questions. These topics should not be overlooked simply because they represent a smaller portion of the exam. Candidates should review specialized drywall applications and the accessories associated with different systems and conditions.

Code Requirements accounts for three questions. The International Building Code, 2018 is particularly important for this area because it can be used inside the examination center. Candidates should practice using the book to find relevant requirements rather than waiting until the examination to learn the layout.

When studying with the International Building Code, practice moving from a question to the index, identifying the likely section, and confirming the applicable requirement. Repeated navigation can help reduce the amount of time required to locate information.

Use Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 as a preparation resource for broader construction concepts that support the trade knowledge needed for the examination. Since this book cannot be taken into the exam room, focus on understanding the material rather than depending on future lookups.

The Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition is useful for reinforcing drywall and gypsum construction knowledge. Use it to develop familiarity with materials, installation practices, finishing, systems, and terminology that support the tested trade categories.

A productive study routine can rotate among content review, practice questions, and reference navigation. When a question is answered incorrectly, return to the relevant material and identify the concept behind the correct answer. Avoid simply memorizing individual answers without understanding the subject.

More on test Information and Study Materials

Time management should also be incorporated into practice. With 90 minutes for 30 questions, candidates should learn to recognize when they know an answer, when a code lookup may be worthwhile, and when it is better to move forward and return to a difficult question later.

The open-book format should be viewed as a resource rather than a substitute for preparation. Strong trade knowledge allows candidates to answer many questions without unnecessary searching, while familiarity with the International Building Code helps when a code reference is needed.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-3E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-3E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
