## Exam Details

The Nevada C-3E Drywall examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada State Contractors Board licensing process. The current C-3E trade exam contains **30 questions**, requires a minimum passing score of **21 correct answers**, and allows **90 minutes** for completion.

The official C-3E examination content outline is divided into six subject areas:

- **Installation — 6 questions:** Knowledge associated with proper drywall installation and the methods used to complete drywall systems.
- **Taping and Finishing — 6 questions:** Trade knowledge related to taping, finishing procedures, surface preparation, and completing gypsum wallboard systems.
- **Materials — 4 questions:** Familiarity with materials used in drywall construction and their appropriate applications.
- **Special Applications and Accessories — 4 questions:** Knowledge of accessories and specialized applications encountered in drywall work.
- **Code Requirements — 3 questions:** Understanding and locating applicable requirements from the code reference identified for the examination.
- **Metal Framing Installation — 7 questions:** Knowledge involving sheet-metal studs used for ceiling framing and nonstructural partitioning.

Metal Framing Installation represents the largest individual section of the C-3E exam, followed by Installation and Taping and Finishing. Candidates preparing for the examination should give each topic appropriate attention while recognizing that the exam also tests the expanded C-3E scope involving sheet-metal studs.

The examination may contain questions based on the references used to develop the test as well as general trade knowledge and industry practices. For questions involving building code requirements, candidates should prepare from the specific code edition identified by PSI for the examination: the **2018 International Building Code**.

Nevada's separate Contractor Management Survey examination contains **60 scored questions** with a listed minimum passing score of **45 correct answers**. The scored portion allows 120 minutes. PSI also identifies a small number of non-scored items that receive additional testing time. The Business and Law exam addresses subjects including licensing, estimating and bidding, lien law, financial management, tax laws, labor laws, project management, contracts, risk management, and environmental and safety topics.

## State Requirements

Nevada defines the C-3E Drywall classification as work involving the **installation, taping, and finishing of drywall, panels and assemblies of gypsum wallboard, sheathing, acoustical tile, and studs made of sheet metal for the framing of ceilings and nonstructural partitioning**. Candidates should prepare for an examination that reflects this entire authorized scope rather than drywall finishing alone.

The person acting as the trade qualifying individual generally must have at least **four full years of qualifying experience** obtained within the **15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application**. Nevada recognizes qualifying experience at the journeyman level or as a supervising employee or contractor in the classification requested.

Education is not generally required to qualify for a Nevada contractor license. However, training from an accredited college or university, or an equivalent program accepted by the Board, may be used toward a portion of the experience requirement. Nevada limits the amount of education that may substitute for experience to no more than three years.

The qualifying individual must be associated with the applying business as permitted under Nevada licensing requirements and must actively fulfill the qualifying role. Depending on the business structure, a qualifier may be an owner, officer, member, manager, or employee of the company.

Nevada requires the qualifying individual to pass the Business and Law examination and the trade examination specific to the requested license classification unless the individual meets the requirements for an applicable waiver. Examination eligibility is provided after the contractor license application is submitted and the Nevada State Contractors Board verifies the required experience.

The Drywall Contractor (C-3E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.
