Exam Details

The Nevada C-3E Drywall trade examination contains 30 questions. Candidates have 90 minutes to complete the examination, and the minimum passing score is 21 correct answers.

The C-3E exam is divided into six primary subject areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.
  • Code Requirements: 3 questions.
  • Metal Framing Installation: 7 questions.

Metal Framing Installation is the largest individual category on the C-3E examination. Installation and Taping and Finishing are also major portions of the test. Candidates should prepare for every category because the examination combines practical drywall knowledge with code-related material.

With 30 questions and 90 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. Efficient preparation should therefore include both subject knowledge and reference-navigation practice.

Nevada contractor applicants are also generally required to complete the Business and Law, or CMS, examination. The included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for that separate portion of the licensing examination process.

Licensing Steps

The C-3E trade examination and CMS examination are parts of the Nevada contractor licensing process. Applicants must complete the applicable application and qualification requirements before moving through the testing and licensing process.

  1. Determine the appropriate classification. Confirm that the Nevada C-3E Drywall classification corresponds to the contracting work you intend to perform.
  2. Prepare qualifying experience documentation. Applicants must provide experience information applicable to the requested contractor classification.
  3. Complete the Nevada contractor license application. Supply the required business, qualifying individual, experience, financial, background, and supporting information.
  4. Submit the application for review. Examination eligibility is provided after the application is filed and applicable experience is reviewed.
  5. Receive examination authorization. Eligible candidates receive instructions for scheduling the examinations required for their application.
  6. Prepare for the C-3E trade examination. Review drywall trade subjects and practice navigating the International Building Code, 2018.
  7. Prepare for the Business and Law examination. Study Nevada contractor business and law material and become familiar with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada.
  8. Complete required examinations. Take the trade and CMS examinations required for the licensing process unless an applicable waiver applies.
  9. Complete remaining licensing requirements. Satisfy all other applicable requirements before the contractor license is issued.

State Requirements

Nevada contractor licensing requires applicants to demonstrate qualification for the classification requested. The licensing process includes review of the qualifying individual, applicable trade experience, business information, financial responsibility, background information, and other required documentation.

Examination eligibility is established through the contractor application process. Applicants receive authorization to schedule required examinations after their application is filed and the applicable experience information has been reviewed.

Nevada generally requires applicants to complete a trade examination for the requested classification and a Business and Law examination. Examination requirements may differ when an applicable waiver or endorsement applies.

The trade exam and CMS exam serve different purposes. The C-3E examination focuses on drywall-related trade knowledge, while the CMS examination addresses business and law knowledge applicable to operating as a Nevada contractor.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong C-3E study plan should cover each published trade subject while also building familiarity with the books included in the package. Because some books cannot be used during the trade examination, candidates should understand the material rather than depending entirely on reference lookups.

Metal Framing Installation contains seven questions and is the largest individual category. Candidates should review metal framing components, terminology, installation principles, and framing concepts associated with drywall ceilings and nonstructural partitions.

Installation contains six questions. Study drywall systems, installation practices, components, materials, and practical terminology associated with gypsum board construction.

Taping and Finishing also contains six questions. Preparation should include finishing materials, procedures, terminology, and the sequence of work used to complete drywall surfaces.

Materials contains four questions. Review drywall products, gypsum materials, components, accessories, and their appropriate applications.

Special Applications and Accessories contains four questions. Candidates should review specialized drywall applications and accessory components rather than concentrating only on basic installation.

Code Requirements contains three questions. Practice using the International Building Code, 2018 to locate applicable provisions efficiently. The highlighted and tabbed format can help you identify important sections while developing stronger navigation habits.

Use Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook to build trade knowledge before test day. Since these books cannot be taken into the C-3E exam room, focus on understanding the concepts and applying them through practice.

The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada should be used separately for CMS preparation. Study the business and law material while practicing how to locate information in the manual efficiently.

When you miss a practice question, return to the underlying concept instead of memorizing only the answer. When a lookup takes too long, practice navigating the applicable book again. Repetition can help improve both knowledge and reference familiarity.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-3E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-3E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
