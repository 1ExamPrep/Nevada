Exam Details

In Nevada, the Electrical Qualifications Card Program uses International Code Council (ICC) National Electrician exams for electrician testing (including Master). Program materials identify the ICC Master Electrician exam IDs as G16, T16, or R16 for master-level testing pathways.

For the ICC National Standard Master Electrician exam outline (Exam Outline “16”), the published format is:

  • Question count: 100 multiple-choice questions
  • Time allowed: 5-hour time limit
  • Core content focus: broad NEC-based coverage across services, feeders, branch circuits, wiring methods, equipment/devices, motors/generators, special occupancies/conditions, and general knowledge/plan reading

Master-level exams are designed to test more than definitions. You’re expected to understand code structure, interpret what a question is truly asking, apply NEC requirements to real installation decisions, and handle calculations without losing time to preventable setup mistakes.

How does the Master Electrician (ICC G16) exam work?

The Master Electrician (ICC G16) card is part of the Electrical Qualifications ID card program run by Electrical Safety Professionals for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno. You pass the ICC national exam, then apply to ESP with the score report.

  • Nevada has no state electrician license; Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno require an Electrical Qualifications ID card on every job site.
  • Electrical Safety Professionals (ESP) issues the cards, after you pass an ICC national exam: G17 journeyman, G16 master, G18 residential or G21 low voltage.
  • ICC electrician exams are open book with the NEC, and ICC offers them in more than one code edition, so match the edition to the exam you book.
  • Cards are valid for 3 years; one expired for more than a year means taking the exam again.
