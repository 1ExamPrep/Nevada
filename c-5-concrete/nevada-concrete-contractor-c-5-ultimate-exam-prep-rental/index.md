## Exam Details

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for applicants who have received examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 60
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 42 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The C-5 examination content outline is divided into six major technical areas:

- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Concrete-Earthwork:** 7 questions
- **Concrete:** 15 questions
- **Concrete-Reinforcement:** 10 questions
- **Concrete-Formwork:** 12 questions
- **Concrete Cutting:** 10 questions

Concrete is the largest individual category with 15 questions. Candidates should understand concrete materials, placement, consolidation, finishing, curing, joints, quality considerations, surface preparation, and the practical procedures associated with concrete construction.

Concrete-Formwork contains 12 questions and deserves substantial preparation time. Review form materials, shores, supports, bracing, form pressures, assembly, stripping, molds, slipforms, pans, and safe construction practices. The included SP-4 Formwork for Concrete provides focused preparation for this area.

Concrete-Reinforcement contains 10 questions. Candidates should review reinforcing steel, placement, spacing, support, cover, embedded materials, and related structural concrete requirements. ACI 318-14 is especially important for developing familiarity with reinforcement-related code provisions.

Concrete Cutting also contains 10 questions. Preparation should include sawcutting, concrete joints, flatwork cutting, drilling, coring, cutting equipment, timing of cuts, and practical techniques for cutting concrete and asphaltic paving material.

Concrete-Earthwork contributes seven questions. Candidates should review excavation, trenching, grading, subgrade preparation, compaction, soil conditions, and other site-preparation topics that affect concrete construction.

Safety contributes six questions. Study should include personal protective equipment, excavation hazards, tools, equipment, material handling, fall protection, and other OSHA construction-safety concepts relevant to concrete projects.

Nevada contractor applicants generally must also satisfy a separate Contractor Management Survey requirement covering contractor Business and Law topics. The included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for that portion of the licensing process.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor classification authorizes the license holder to prepare surfaces and place reinforcing steel and other embedded materials that are essential to or form an integral part of concrete construction.

The classification also covers pouring, placing, finishing, and installing concrete. Candidates preparing for the license should understand the sequence of concrete work from site and surface preparation through reinforcement, formwork, placement, finishing, curing, and protection.

C-5 contractors may construct and assemble forms, molds, slipforms, and pans. Formwork is therefore both an important part of the legal scope of the classification and one of the largest technical sections of the trade examination.

The classification also includes applying concrete sealants and waterproofing products. Candidates should understand the importance of surface condition, preparation, application practices, protection, and durability within concrete construction.

Sawing, cutting, drilling, and coring concrete and asphaltic paving material are also included within the C-5 scope. The exam dedicates 10 questions to Concrete Cutting, making specialized cutting knowledge an important part of preparation.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the qualifying individual generally must establish four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor within the applicable period before the application.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes financial responsibility, contractor bonding, background requirements, and a monetary limit associated with the issued license. Applicants generally must satisfy both trade and contractor management qualification requirements unless an applicable waiver is approved.

## How does the Concrete Contractor (C-5) exam work?

The Concrete Contractor (C-5) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
