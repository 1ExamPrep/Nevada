Exam Details

The Nevada Concrete Contractor (C-5) trade examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada contractor licensing examination process. The C-5 trade exam contains 60 questions, requires a minimum passing score of 42 correct answers, and provides candidates with 2 hours to complete the examination.

The official C-5 examination content outline is divided into the following subject areas:

  • Safety: 6 questions.
  • Concrete-Earthwork: 7 questions.
  • Concrete: 15 questions.
  • Concrete-Reinforcement: 10 questions.
  • Concrete-Formwork: 12 questions.
  • Concrete-Cutting: 10 questions.

Concrete, reinforcement, formwork, and concrete cutting represent a significant portion of the examination. Candidates should prepare to work with code provisions, concrete construction concepts, reinforcement requirements, formwork practices, site and earthwork considerations, cutting procedures, and safety-related material.

The examination may also include questions based on general trade knowledge and industry practices. For that reason, successful preparation involves more than simply knowing where a tab is located. Candidates should understand common concrete construction terminology, procedures, and technical concepts while practicing how to use their approved references efficiently.

Nevada contractor applicants are also generally required to complete the Contractor Management Survey, often referred to as the CMS or Business and Law examination, unless an applicable waiver is granted. The CMS exam evaluates subjects related to operating a contracting business in Nevada, including licensing, estimating, bidding, contracts, financial management, project management, labor and tax requirements, lien law, risk management, environmental matters, and safety.

Licensing Steps

  1. Identify the qualifying individual. A Nevada contractor license must have a qualified individual who meets the applicable experience and qualification requirements for the classification requested.
  2. Document qualifying experience. The trade qualified individual generally must have at least four full years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the filing of the application. Qualifying experience may include work at the journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor level in the applicable classification.
  3. Prepare application documentation. Applicants must assemble the business, experience, financial, and other documentation required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.
  4. Submit the contractor license application. The completed application and required supporting materials are submitted to the Nevada State Contractors Board for review.
  5. Complete experience verification. The Board evaluates the qualifying individual’s documented experience and other submitted information.
  6. Receive examination eligibility when testing is required. After the applicable application and experience requirements are reviewed, eligible candidates receive information for completing the required examinations.
  7. Take the required examinations. C-5 applicants generally complete the Nevada CMS Business and Law examination and the C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination unless an applicable examination waiver is granted.
  8. Complete final licensing requirements. After approval, applicants must satisfy the remaining Board requirements, including the required contractor license bond and other applicable licensing documentation before the license can be issued.

State Requirements

Contractor licensing in Nevada is regulated by the Nevada State Contractors Board. The C-5 specialty classification applies specifically to concrete contracting.

The scope of the Nevada C-5 classification includes preparing surfaces and placing reinforcement steel and other embedded materials that are essential to concrete construction; pouring, placing, finishing, and installing concrete; constructing and assembling forms, molds, slipforms, and pans; applying concrete sealants and other waterproofing products; and sawing, cutting, drilling, and coring concrete and asphaltic paving material.

To qualify for a Nevada contractor license, the business must have an individual qualified to oversee the appropriate business and trade activities. The person acting as the trade qualifier generally must have four full years of qualifying experience gained within the 15 years immediately before filing the application.

Nevada may accept qualifying training from an accredited college, university, or equivalent program toward a portion of the experience requirement. Accepted training may satisfy up to three years of the required experience when properly documented. Nevada also recognizes certain other qualifying documentation, including applicable military experience or qualifying certifications under Board requirements.

Financial responsibility is another component of the Nevada contractor licensing process. Applicants must provide financial information appropriate to the monetary limit requested and comply with the Nevada State Contractors Board’s financial documentation requirements.

Every Nevada contractor license also requires a contractor license bond. The Nevada State Contractors Board determines the required bond amount during the approval process. The amount is based on factors including the type of license, monetary limit, financial responsibility, experience, and character of the applicant. The applicant receives the required bond amount after application approval.
