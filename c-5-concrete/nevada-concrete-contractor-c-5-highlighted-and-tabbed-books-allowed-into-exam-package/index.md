## Exam Details

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 60
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 42 correct answers

The C-5 examination is divided into the following subject areas:

- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Concrete-Earthwork:** 7 questions
- **Concrete:** 15 questions
- **Concrete-Reinforcement:** 10 questions
- **Concrete-Formwork:** 12 questions
- **Concrete-Cutting:** 10 questions

**Concrete** is the largest individual exam category with 15 questions. Candidates should review concrete materials, placement, consolidation, finishing, curing, joints, surface preparation, sealants, waterproofing, and general quality practices associated with concrete construction.

**Concrete-Formwork** accounts for 12 questions. Preparation should include forms, molds, slipforms, pans, supports, bracing, layout, alignment, stripping, and practical field procedures used to contain and support concrete during placement.

**Concrete-Reinforcement** accounts for 10 questions. Candidates should understand reinforcing steel, embedded materials, placement, cover, spacing, development concepts, and related structural concrete requirements.

**Concrete-Cutting** also contains 10 questions. Candidates should prepare for sawing, cutting, drilling, coring, joint cutting, equipment use, timing, and practical methods used in concrete and asphaltic paving work.

**Concrete-Earthwork** represents seven questions and may include site preparation, excavation, grading, subgrade conditions, compaction, trenching relationships, and other earthwork concepts that affect concrete construction.

**Safety** represents six questions. Candidates should understand common jobsite hazards involving reinforcement, forms, excavation, concrete placement, saw cutting, tools, personal protective equipment, and related construction operations.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-5 Concrete classification authorizes work involving preparation of surfaces and placement of reinforcing steel and other embedded materials that are essential to or form an integral part of concrete construction.

The classification also covers pouring, placing, finishing, and installing concrete, as well as constructing and assembling forms, molds, slipforms, and pans used in concrete work.

Additional authorized work includes applying concrete sealants and other waterproofing products and sawing, cutting, drilling, and coring concrete and asphaltic paving material.

The broad scope means candidates should prepare for the complete concrete construction process rather than focusing only on finishing. A C-5 contractor may need knowledge of site conditions, earthwork preparation, reinforcement, forms, placement, curing, finishing, sealing, waterproofing, saw cutting, drilling, and coring.

Reinforcement and formwork are especially important areas because together they account for a substantial portion of the examination. Candidates should understand how reinforcing steel and embedded materials are incorporated into concrete and how forms support concrete until the material can sustain itself.

Concrete cutting is another major part of both the classification and the examination. Candidates should be prepared for questions involving saw cutting, joint timing, drilling, coring, equipment, and related field practices.

Passing the C-5 trade examination does not automatically issue a Nevada contractor license. Applicants must also satisfy applicable experience, management qualification, financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other licensing requirements administered by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

## How does the Concrete Contractor (C-5) exam work?

The Concrete Contractor (C-5) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
