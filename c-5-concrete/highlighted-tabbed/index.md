## Exam Details

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 60
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 42 correct answers

The examination is divided into the following subject areas:

- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Concrete-Earthwork:** 7 questions
- **Concrete:** 15 questions
- **Concrete-Reinforcement:** 10 questions
- **Concrete-Formwork:** 12 questions
- **Concrete-Cutting:** 10 questions

**Concrete** is the largest individual exam category with 15 questions. Candidates should review concrete materials, placement, consolidation, curing, finishing, joints, surface preparation, sealants, waterproofing, quality-control concepts, and common field practices.

**Concrete-Formwork** accounts for 12 questions. Preparation should include form materials, bracing, supports, loads, layout, stripping, molds, slipforms, pans, alignment, and practical formwork procedures.

**Concrete-Reinforcement** contains 10 questions. Candidates should understand reinforcing steel, placement, cover, spacing, embedded materials, development concepts, and related ACI requirements.

**Concrete-Cutting** also accounts for 10 questions. Candidates should prepare for topics involving saw cutting, joint cutting, flatwork cutting, drilling, coring, equipment use, timing, and practical cutting methods.

**Concrete-Earthwork** accounts for seven questions and may include excavation, site preparation, grading, subgrade conditions, trenching relationships, compaction, and other earthwork considerations that affect concrete construction.

**Safety** accounts for six questions. Candidates should understand common hazards associated with reinforcing steel, formwork, excavation, concrete placement, cutting equipment, tools, personal protective equipment, and other jobsite operations.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-5 Concrete classification authorizes work involving surface preparation and placement of reinforcing steel and other embedded materials essential to concrete construction.

The classification also covers pouring, placing, finishing, and installing concrete and constructing and assembling forms, molds, slipforms, and pans used in concrete construction.

Additional authorized work includes applying concrete sealants and waterproofing products and sawing, cutting, drilling, and coring concrete and asphaltic paving material.

The broad scope means candidates should prepare for the complete concrete construction process rather than focusing only on finishing. Study should include site preparation, reinforcement, formwork, placement, finishing, curing, waterproofing, cutting, drilling, coring, and related safety practices.

Formwork and reinforcement are particularly important areas because together they account for 22 of the 60 exam questions. Candidates should understand how forms support concrete during placement and how reinforcing steel and embedded materials contribute to the completed system.

Concrete cutting is also a major part of the classification and examination. Sawing, joint cutting, coring, drilling, and related equipment should receive dedicated preparation time.

Passing the C-5 trade examination does not automatically result in contractor license issuance. Applicants must also satisfy applicable experience, management qualification, financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other Nevada licensing requirements.

## How does the Concrete Contractor (C-5) exam work?

The Concrete Contractor (C-5) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
