## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor examination is an open-book examination. The 2018 International Building Code and ACI 318-14 included with this package are approved references for the C-5 exam. PSI also identifies ACI 318-11 as an allowed alternative edition, but this package includes ACI 318-14.

The Nevada CMS examination is also open book, and the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada is permitted in the examination center for that exam.

PSI permits approved references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. References may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted. References may not contain additional loose or attached papers, and candidates may not write in or highlight the references during the examination session.

Because these tests are open book, reference navigation should be part of your preparation. Knowing that the answer is somewhere in a book is not enough when the examination is timed. Practice identifying keywords in a question, determining which reference is most likely to contain the information, moving quickly to the correct chapter or section, and confirming the answer without spending unnecessary time searching.

## Licensing Steps

1. **Establish the applicant and qualifying individual.** Each Nevada contractor license must have a qualified individual who meets the applicable experience and qualification requirements. A person may serve in the required management and trade qualifying roles when eligible, or those responsibilities may be filled by separate individuals.
2. **Document qualifying trade experience.** The trade qualified individual generally must document at least four full years of qualifying experience gained within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Nevada recognizes experience at the journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor level in the requested classification.
3. **Prepare the required business and financial information.** The Nevada State Contractors Board requires applicants to provide the information and supporting documentation required by the contractor license application, including applicable business, financial, experience, and background information.
4. **Submit the contractor license application.** The application is submitted to the Nevada State Contractors Board for review.
5. **Receive examination eligibility.** When testing is required and the Board has determined that the applicant is eligible, the applicant receives authorization to schedule the required examination or examinations.
6. **Schedule with PSI.** PSI administers Nevada’s contractor licensing examinations. Candidates schedule their authorized tests after receiving examination eligibility.
7. **Pass the required examinations.** For the C-5 classification, the qualifying process generally includes the CMS Business and Law examination and the C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination unless the Board grants an applicable examination waiver.
8. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** After approval, applicants must satisfy the Board’s remaining requirements, including the contractor license bond amount established by the Board and any other items identified in the approval process before the license is issued.

## State Requirements

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates contractor licensing in Nevada. Classification C-5 is the state specialty classification for concrete contracting.

A C-5 license authorizes qualified contractors to perform work within the classification’s scope, including preparing surfaces and placing reinforcement steel and other embedded materials that are integral to concrete construction; pouring, placing, finishing, and installing concrete; constructing and assembling forms, molds, slipforms, and pans; applying concrete sealants and other waterproofing products; and sawing, cutting, drilling, and coring concrete and asphaltic paving material.

The trade qualifying individual generally needs at least four years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately before filing the application. Nevada may recognize certain education or training toward a portion of the experience requirement in accordance with Board requirements, and other approved methods of documenting qualifications may apply.

The applicant must also demonstrate financial responsibility. Nevada requires financial information as part of the contractor licensing process, and the type of financial statement required depends on the monetary limit requested.

Each Nevada contractor license also requires a contractor license bond. The Nevada State Contractors Board determines the required bond amount during the licensing approval process based on factors established by the Board. Applicants receive the specific bond requirement as part of the approval process rather than selecting the amount themselves.
