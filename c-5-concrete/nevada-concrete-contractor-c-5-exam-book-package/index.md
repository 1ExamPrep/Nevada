## Exam Details

The Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates authorized to test through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-5 Concrete Contractor
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 60
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 42 correct answers

The current examination content is organized into the following subject areas:

- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Concrete-Earthwork:** 7 questions
- **Concrete:** 15 questions
- **Concrete-Reinforcement:** 10 questions
- **Concrete-Formwork:** 12 questions
- **Concrete-Cutting:** 10 questions

**Concrete** is the largest individual category with 15 questions. Candidates should review concrete materials, mixing, placement, consolidation, curing, finishing, joints, quality-control concepts, surface preparation, sealants, waterproofing, and other practical concrete construction principles.

**Concrete-Formwork** accounts for 12 questions. Candidates should understand form construction, materials, layout, bracing, supports, stripping, loads, molds, slipforms, pans, and practical considerations involved in keeping forms stable and properly aligned during concrete placement.

**Concrete-Reinforcement** and **Concrete-Cutting** each account for 10 questions. Reinforcement preparation should include reinforcing steel, placement, cover, spacing, development concepts, embedded items, and applicable ACI requirements. Cutting preparation should include saw cutting, joint cutting, drilling, coring, timing, equipment, and field methods.

**Concrete-Earthwork** accounts for seven questions. Candidates should review excavation, grading, preparation, compaction, subgrade conditions, trenching relationships, drainage, and site preparation associated with concrete work.

**Safety** accounts for six questions and should not be overlooked. Candidates should understand construction hazards, personal protective equipment, tools, excavation safety, concrete operations, reinforcing work, formwork hazards, and cutting equipment safety.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-5 Concrete classification authorizes a contractor to prepare surfaces and place reinforcement steel and other embedded materials essential to concrete construction; pour, place, finish, and install concrete; construct and assemble forms, molds, slipforms, and pans; apply concrete sealants and other waterproofing products; and saw, cut, drill, and core concrete and asphaltic paving material.

The scope requires candidates to understand several stages of concrete construction rather than focusing only on placement and finishing. A C-5 contractor may encounter earthwork preparation, reinforcing steel, formwork, placement, finishing, curing, cutting, sealing, drilling, and other related tasks within a project.

Formwork and reinforcement are especially important because they directly influence the shape, stability, and structural behavior of concrete construction. Candidates should understand proper preparation, bracing, reinforcing placement, embedded materials, and practical field procedures.

Concrete cutting is also a significant part of the classification and represents 10 questions on the examination. Sawing, coring, drilling, and joint cutting should be treated as major study subjects rather than minor specialty topics.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the qualifying individual generally must document at least four years of qualifying experience within the applicable experience period required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Passing the C-5 trade examination alone does not automatically result in contractor license issuance. Applicants must also complete applicable management, financial responsibility, bonding, background, and other Nevada licensing requirements.

## How does the Concrete Contractor (C-5) exam work?

The Concrete Contractor (C-5) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
