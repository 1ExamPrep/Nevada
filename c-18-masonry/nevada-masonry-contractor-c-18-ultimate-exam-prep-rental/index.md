## Exam Details

The Nevada C-18 Masonry Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates who receive examination eligibility through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-18 Masonry Contractor
- **Number of Questions:** 50
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 35 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The C-18 examination content outline is divided into five technical areas:

- **Safety:** 5 questions
- **Concrete Block:** 20 questions
- **Brick:** 11 questions
- **Specialty Masonry:** 6 questions
- **Mortar and Grout:** 8 questions

Concrete Block is the largest individual examination category with 20 questions. Candidates should make concrete masonry units a major focus of their study. Preparation should include masonry layout, wall construction, bond patterns, block installation, openings, cutting, alignment, reinforcement concepts, tools, materials, and practical field methods.

Brick contains 11 questions and is another major section of the C-18 examination. Candidates should review brick types, bonds, coursing, joints, layout, cutting, installation, openings, workmanship principles, and practical brick construction.

Mortar and Grout contains eight questions. Candidates should understand the purpose and use of mortar and grout, mixing concepts, application, placement, joints, handling, and the relationship between masonry units, grout, reinforcement, and completed masonry assemblies.

Specialty Masonry contains six questions. Preparation should include stone, artificial stone, precast masonry products, structural glass brick or block, specialty applications, and installation methods that extend beyond standard concrete block and brick work.

Safety contains five questions. Candidates should review OSHA-related subjects such as scaffolds, ladders, personal protective equipment, fall hazards, cutting operations, tools, material handling, and other common construction hazards associated with masonry projects.

With 50 questions and two hours available, candidates should develop both trade knowledge and efficient reference-navigation skills. The goal is to recognize when a question can be answered directly and when a targeted lookup in an approved code book is appropriate.

## State Requirements

The Nevada C-18 Masonry classification is a specialty contractor classification. It authorizes qualifying masonry work involving brick and other baked-clay products, rough-cut and dressed stone, artificial stone, precast blocks, and structural glass brick or block.

The materials may be installed at random or in courses and may be laid with or without mortar. Contractors should understand the limits of the classification because C-18 does not authorize applying tile to existing surfaces.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must have at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific classification requested.

The qualifying experience must generally have been obtained within the 15 years preceding the contractor application. Experience documentation should clearly demonstrate relevant masonry work and the duration of that experience.

Nevada contractor licensing also distinguishes between Management and Trade Qualified Individuals. One person may serve in both roles when the applicable requirements are satisfied, or separate people may fulfill the management and trade qualification responsibilities.

The Trade Qualified Individual establishes the required experience and trade qualification for C-18. The Management Qualified Individual generally satisfies the Nevada Business and Law requirement unless an applicable waiver is approved.

Financial responsibility, background requirements, contractor bonding, and a monetary limit are also part of the Nevada licensing process. Passing the C-18 trade examination is an important step, but it does not by itself complete the contractor licensing process.

## How does the Masonry Contractor (C-18) exam work?

The Masonry Contractor (C-18) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
