Is the NASCLA Business, Law and Project Management exam open book?

ICC and NASCLA exams are open book for most categories. The bulletin for your exam lists the references allowed and the editions.

Who issues the NASCLA Business, Law and Project Management license or card in Nevada?

It comes from the city, county or state that uses the exam. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Which code edition is the exam on?

Each ICC and NASCLA bulletin names the edition. Check it before ordering, since jurisdictions adopt new codes on their own schedules.

Can I bring these books into the exam?

For open-book exams, yes, if the edition is the one in your bulletin. Nevada trade exams are closed book except for the references named in the PSI outline.
