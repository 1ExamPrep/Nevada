## Exam Details

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor examination is administered through PSI for the Nevada State Contractors Board. Before scheduling the examination, the contractor application must be submitted and the qualifying individual's experience must be accepted so examination eligibility can be issued.

The C-15A Roofing Contractor trade examination contains **50 questions**. Candidates must answer at least **38 questions correctly** to meet the published minimum passing score. The examination allows **2 hours** for completion.

The published C-15A examination content outline includes:

- **Safety:** 5 questions.
- **Roofing Substrates:** 7 questions.
- **Steep Roofing:** 17 questions.
- **Roofing Repairs:** 4 questions.
- **Elastomeric Roofing:** 6 questions.
- **Built-up Bituminous Roofing:** 7 questions.
- **Sheet Metal and Flashing:** 2 questions.
- **Photovoltaic Systems:** 2 questions.

Steep roofing is the largest individual content area, accounting for 17 of the 50 questions. Roofing substrates and built-up bituminous roofing each account for seven questions, while elastomeric roofing accounts for six. Safety, repairs, flashing, and photovoltaic roofing systems complete the published outline.

Nevada contractor applicants generally must also satisfy the Construction Management Survey, commonly called the CMS exam. The CMS is the state's contractor business and law examination and covers topics such as licensing, contracts, estimating, bidding, financial management, lien law, labor requirements, taxes, project management, risk management, and related construction-business subjects.

## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor trade examination is an **open-book test**. The current PSI examination outline allows the International Building Code, 2018 in the examination center for the C-15A trade exam.

The Nevada Construction Management Survey business and law examination is also open book. Candidates may use the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada during the CMS examination.

PSI requires candidates to bring their own approved reference materials. References may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. References may not contain writing, and candidates are not permitted to write or add highlighting to the books during the examination session.

Permanent tabs are allowed for organizing approved references. Temporary tabs, including removable flags or Post-It-style tabs, are not permitted. Candidates also may not bring additional loose or attached papers with the approved reference materials.

Because only the approved code reference may be used in the C-15A trade examination room, candidates should not depend on bringing every study resource used during preparation. Trade knowledge remains important. Candidates should study roofing materials, installation practices, repair procedures, roofing systems, safety, flashing, substrates, and photovoltaic roofing concepts while also becoming comfortable navigating the International Building Code.

## State Requirements

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates contractor licensing in Nevada. A C-15A license is a roofing subclassification under the broader C-15 Roofing and Siding classification.

The C-15A scope authorizes installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof materials used on roofs. Examples listed in Nevada regulations include asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, flax, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and photovoltaic roof tiles that replace traditional roofing materials.

The C-15A license does not authorize installation of siding or devices used for internal control of weather effects. It also does not authorize installation of conduits, inverters, batteries, or electrical connections associated with photovoltaic systems.

The trade qualified individual generally must have at least four full years of applicable experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the specific classification requested within the 15 years immediately before filing the application. Nevada may accept qualifying education or training toward a portion of the experience requirement within the limits established by the Board.

Nevada contractor applicants are also subject to financial responsibility requirements. The Board reviews financial information as part of determining the contractor's approved monetary limit and licensing status.

A contractor bond is required after approval. The amount is determined by the Nevada State Contractors Board based on factors associated with the applicant and license. Examination completion alone does not issue the contractor license; all applicable Board requirements must be satisfied.

## How does the Roofing Contractor (C-15A) exam work?

The Roofing Contractor (C-15A) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
