Roofing contractors need more than familiarity with one roof covering. Exam preparation should include substrate conditions, steep-slope applications, low-slope and membrane systems, flashing, repairs, safety, estimating, and the relationship between roofing assemblies and building-code requirements. Exam Details

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor examination contains 50 questions. Candidates have 2 hours to complete the examination and must answer 38 questions correctly to pass.

The examination content is divided into the following subject areas:

  • Safety: 5 questions
  • Roofing Substrates: 7 questions
  • Steep Roofing: 17 questions
  • Roofing Repairs: 4 questions
  • Elastomeric Roofing: 6 questions
  • Built-up Bituminous Roofing: 7 questions
  • Sheet Metal and Flashing: 2 questions
  • Photovoltaic Systems: 2 questions

Steep Roofing is the largest category with 17 questions. Candidates should devote substantial preparation time to steep-slope roof coverings, underlayments, roof decks, flashing, drainage relationships, installation practices, fastening, materials, and repair considerations.

Roofing Substrates and Built-up Bituminous Roofing each account for 7 questions. Elastomeric Roofing accounts for 6 questions, while Safety accounts for 5. Roofing Repairs contributes 4 questions, and Sheet Metal and Flashing and Photovoltaic Systems each contribute 2.

The exam therefore requires broad roofing knowledge. Candidates should prepare for both steep- and low-slope concepts instead of concentrating only on the roofing systems they work with most often in the field.

State Requirements

Nevada identifies C-15A Roofing as a subclassification within Classification C-15 Roofing and Siding.

The C-15A classification authorizes the installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof material used on roofs. Roofing materials can include asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, flax, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and photovoltaic roof tiles intended to serve in place of other existing roofing materials.

The classification does not authorize the installation of siding or devices used for the internal control of the effects of weather. It also does not authorize a C-15A contractor to install conduits, inverters, batteries, or electrical connections associated with photovoltaic systems.

The distinction is important when studying the photovoltaic portion of the examination. Roofing contractors may encounter photovoltaic roof products within the roofing scope, but electrical installation work requires the appropriate electrical authorization.

The C-15A trade examination is one component of Nevada contractor licensing. Applicants must also satisfy the applicable experience, qualification, application, business, financial, and other licensing requirements associated with obtaining a Nevada contractor license.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Nevada C-15A preparation plan should begin with Steep Roofing because it represents 17 of the 50 scored questions. Study roof coverings, underlayments, fastening, flashing relationships, roof deck conditions, drainage, slopes, installation practices, and common steep-slope repair issues.

Roofing Substrates should include deck and sheathing conditions, structural support, surface preparation, attachment, compatibility, deterioration, and how substrate conditions affect the performance of the finished roof system.

Built-up Bituminous Roofing preparation should include components, membranes, bituminous materials, surfacing, flashing, application concepts, repair, and basic system construction.

Elastomeric Roofing should also receive substantial attention. Review membrane systems, seams, flashing, penetrations, attachment concepts, roof edges, drainage, and repair considerations.

Roofing Repairs should be approached as a diagnostic subject. Candidates should understand how to identify damaged materials, locate potential leak sources, recognize failed flashing or membrane conditions, and select appropriate repair approaches.

Sheet Metal and Flashing represents a smaller category, but flashing is fundamental to watertight roofing. Review valleys, roof-to-wall intersections, penetrations, edges, curbs, transitions, and other locations where water must be directed away from vulnerable areas.

Photovoltaic Systems accounts for 2 questions. Study roofing considerations associated with photovoltaic components and understand the boundary between roofing work and electrical work within the C-15A classification.

Safety accounts for 5 questions and is especially important in roofing because much of the work occurs at elevation. Study fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, personal protective equipment, material handling, tools, access, and common construction hazards.

The International Building Code, 2018 should be used regularly during study. Practice locating roofing provisions through the table of contents and index, then follow the applicable sections, subsections, tables, and exceptions.

Use the NRCA manuals to develop a broader understanding of how different roofing systems are constructed. Compare steep-slope, metal, SPF, and membrane systems so differences in materials and application methods become easier to recognize.

Roofing Construction and Estimating can be used to strengthen measurement and estimating skills. Candidates should be comfortable interpreting roof dimensions, calculating quantities, recognizing roof geometry, and relating measurements to material requirements.

The carpentry reference can reinforce knowledge of roof framing and substrates. Roofing questions may require an understanding of what lies beneath the finished roof covering, including sheathing and structural components.

More on test Information and Study Materials

Because most references in this package are not allowed into the examination room, gradually shift study from book-supported learning to recall. Read a subject, close the reference, and work practice questions without looking up every answer.

For the International Building Code, use a different approach: practice timed navigation. Identify the subject, locate the appropriate code provision, read the complete requirement, and return to the question.

Mixed practice should be added near the end of preparation. The exam can move quickly from steep roofing to safety, substrates, membrane systems, repairs, flashing, or photovoltaic topics. Candidates should become comfortable changing subjects without losing their pacing.

How does the Roofing Contractor (C-15A) exam work?

The Roofing Contractor (C-15A) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
