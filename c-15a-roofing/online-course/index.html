Nevada Roofing Contractor (C-15A) Exam - Online Practice Questions

Prepare for the Nevada Roofing Contractor (C-15A) examination with online practice questions focused on roofing systems, steep-slope applications, membrane roofing, metal panel systems, spray polyurethane foam roofing, roof substrates, repairs, flashing, estimating, construction safety, rooftop photovoltaic considerations, and building-code navigation. This practice-oriented product is designed to help candidates strengthen recall, recognize common roofing exam concepts, and become more comfortable applying trade knowledge under timed conditions.

The Nevada C-15A Roofing classification is regulated by the Nevada State Contractors Board. The classification covers qualifying installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof materials used on roofs. Roofing work within the classification can include asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and other qualifying roofing materials.

The C-15A examination requires candidates to understand more than one roof system. Preparation should include steep-slope roofing, membrane systems, metal panel roofing, spray polyurethane foam systems, built-up bituminous roofing, elastomeric roofing, substrates, repairs, flashing, roof penetrations, safety, estimating, and roof-system conditions involving photovoltaic equipment.

The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is the International Building Code, 2018. Candidates should become comfortable navigating roofing-related provisions within the IBC because the C-15A examination is open book with specific approved reference material.

Additional technical references used for study include the NRCA Roofing Manual: Steep Slope Roof Systems, 2025; NRCA Roofing Manual: Metal Panel and SPF Roof Systems, 2024; NRCA Roofing Manual: Membrane Roofing Systems; Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA); Roofing Construction and Estimating by Daniel Atcheson, 1995; NRCA Guidelines for Roof Systems with Rooftop Photovoltaic Components, 2018; and Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016.

The additional books are useful for building roofing knowledge before test day, but they are not allowed into the C-15A trade examination. Online practice questions can help candidates turn the information studied from these books into faster recall, better subject recognition, and stronger exam pacing.

A productive way to use online practice questions is to identify patterns in missed answers. If a candidate repeatedly misses questions involving steep-slope roofing, flashing, membranes, substrates, safety, or estimating, that subject can receive additional targeted review before returning to another round of practice.

Exam Details

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for qualifying candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor
  • Number of Questions: 50
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Minimum Passing Score: 38 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The C-15A examination covers major roofing knowledge areas, including safety, roofing substrates, steep roofing, roof repairs, elastomeric roofing, built-up bituminous roofing, sheet metal and flashing, and photovoltaic-related roofing considerations.

Steep Roofing is a major focus area. Candidates should review asphalt shingles, clay and concrete tile, wood shakes and shingles, slate, underlayments, roof decks, fastening, valleys, ridges, penetrations, flashing, drainage, and installation details.

Roofing Substrates requires knowledge of roof decks, sheathing, substrate conditions, compatibility, fastening surfaces, deterioration, preparation, and how the condition of the supporting roof structure can affect the performance of the finished roofing system.

Built-Up Bituminous Roofing and Elastomeric Roofing require familiarity with low-slope systems, membranes, insulation, surfacing, attachment, flashing, drainage, maintenance, and repair concepts.

Roofing Repairs can involve recognizing failed materials, deteriorated flashing, drainage problems, membrane damage, substrate deterioration, penetrations, fastener problems, and other conditions that can compromise weather resistance.

Sheet Metal and Flashing is important because flashings protect many of the roof's most vulnerable transitions. Candidates should understand flashing at roof edges, walls, valleys, penetrations, curbs, transitions, and similar conditions.

Safety should also receive focused preparation because roofing work can involve significant fall hazards, ladder use, scaffolds, tools, material handling, roof-edge exposure, and other construction risks.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference used for this preparation is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should practice locating roofing provisions in the International Building Code before test day. Useful code-navigation practice can include roof coverings, roof assemblies, materials, weather protection, structural conditions, and other roofing-related requirements.

Open-book testing still requires strong preparation. Candidates have limited time and should not expect to research every question from the beginning. A stronger approach is to answer familiar roofing questions directly and use the IBC when a precise code requirement needs to be located or confirmed.

The following study references are not allowed into the C-15A trade examination:

  • NRCA Roofing Manual: Steep Slope Roof Systems, 2025
  • NRCA Roofing Manual: Metal Panel and SPF Roof Systems, 2024
  • NRCA Roofing Manual: Membrane Roofing Systems
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
  • Roofing Construction and Estimating, Daniel Atcheson, 1995
  • NRCA Guidelines for Roof Systems with Rooftop Photovoltaic Components, 2018
  • Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016

Because these books cannot be used during the trade examination, practice questions can help reinforce the information learned from them before test day. The goal is to develop enough familiarity with roofing materials, systems, installation practices, estimating methods, safety concepts, and construction details to answer many questions without depending on a study-only reference.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates the C-15A Roofing Contractor classification. Passing the trade examination is one part of the overall contractor licensing process.

  1. Confirm the C-15A classification. Determine that Roofing Contractor matches the scope of work the business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Designate the individual who will establish the applicable roofing experience and technical qualification.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare records demonstrating relevant roofing work within the requested classification.
  4. Prepare the Nevada contractor license application. Organize the required business, qualifier, experience, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application. The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews the application and qualification information before examination eligibility is established.
  6. Complete applicable background requirements. Follow the required fingerprint and background procedures associated with Nevada contractor licensing.
  7. Provide applicable financial information. Financial responsibility is evaluated as part of the licensing process.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Eligible applicants can proceed with applicable testing after authorization.
  9. Schedule the C-15A examination through PSI. Arrange the Roofing Contractor trade exam after examination eligibility is established.
  10. Prepare for the C-15A examination. Review steep roofing, substrates, membrane systems, repairs, flashing, safety, estimating, photovoltaic-related roof conditions, and building-code navigation.
  11. Prepare for Nevada Business and Law. Nevada contractor applicants generally must satisfy the applicable management qualification requirement unless an approved waiver applies.
  12. Complete applicable examinations. Meet the required trade and management examination requirements.
  13. Complete final licensing requirements. Address applicable financial responsibility, bonding, and other Nevada State Contractors Board requirements before license issuance.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-15A Roofing subclassification covers qualifying installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof roofing materials within the scope established for the classification.

Roofing work under the classification can involve asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and other qualifying roof materials. Candidates should understand the classification scope when preparing for both the examination and the work they intend to perform after licensing.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must establish four years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the application.

Applicants should maintain organized experience records describing the roofing work performed, responsibilities held, and the period during which the experience was obtained. The experience should relate to the classification requested.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes management qualification. The applicable Management Qualified Individual generally must satisfy the Contractor Management Survey and Business and Law requirement unless an approved waiver applies.

Financial responsibility, contractor bonding, background procedures, and other licensing requirements are handled separately from the trade examination. Passing the C-15A exam alone does not complete the Nevada contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

Online practice questions can be used as more than a final review tool. They can help candidates identify patterns, measure progress, and decide where additional roofing study should be concentrated.

Prioritize steep-slope roofing. Practice questions should reinforce shingles, tile, slate, wood roofing, underlayments, roof decks, fasteners, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, and installation details.

Review membrane and low-slope roofing. Practice should include built-up roofing, elastomeric systems, membrane materials, insulation, attachment, flashing, drainage, and repairs.

Study roofing substrates. Candidates should be comfortable recognizing deck conditions, sheathing concerns, surface preparation, deterioration, and compatibility issues.

Prepare safety for recall. OSHA is not allowed into the exam, so practice questions can help reinforce fall protection, ladder use, scaffolds, PPE, tools, and material-handling concepts.

Practice estimating. Review roof measurements, materials, quantities, waste considerations, and general roofing estimating concepts.

Practice IBC navigation. When a practice question involves a code requirement, use the 2018 International Building Code to locate the answer and become more familiar with the structure of the reference.

Review photovoltaic roof conditions. Study how rooftop photovoltaic components can affect penetrations, flashing, drainage, maintenance access, and roof-system performance.

Track missed questions by subject. Instead of only reviewing the correct answer, identify why the question was missed. Was it a knowledge gap, a code-navigation issue, a calculation problem, or a misunderstanding of the roofing system? That information can guide the next study session.

Use timed question sets. Since the examination contains 50 questions in two hours, timed practice can help candidates develop a consistent pace and become more comfortable deciding when to search the IBC and when to answer directly from roofing knowledge.

How does the Roofing Contractor (C-15A) exam work?

The Roofing Contractor (C-15A) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
