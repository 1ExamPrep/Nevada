Exam Details

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for qualifying candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor
  • Number of Questions: 50
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Minimum Passing Score: 38 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The examination covers major roofing knowledge areas, including safety, roofing substrates, steep roofing, roof repairs, elastomeric roofing, built-up bituminous roofing, sheet metal and flashing, and photovoltaic-related roofing considerations.

Steep Roofing represents the largest individual area of preparation. Candidates should review asphalt shingles, clay and concrete tile, wood shakes and shingles, slate, roof decks, underlayments, fastening methods, valleys, ridges, penetrations, flashing, drainage, and other installation details associated with steep-slope systems.

Roofing Substrates is another important area. Candidates should understand roof decks, substrate condition, preparation, compatibility, fastening surfaces, deterioration, structural concerns, and how the condition of the existing roof structure can affect performance.

Built-Up Bituminous Roofing and Elastomeric Roofing require knowledge of low-slope roof systems, membranes, insulation, surfacing, flashing, attachment, drainage, repairs, and installation practices. The NRCA roofing manuals provide useful study material for these topics.

Roofing Repairs requires candidates to recognize common roof problems and appropriate corrective approaches. Study should include leaks, damaged membranes, deteriorated flashing, failed fasteners, substrate problems, drainage issues, and conditions that reduce weather resistance.

Sheet Metal and Flashing may represent a smaller portion of the examination, but it remains essential to watertight roof construction. Candidates should understand flashing at walls, roof edges, penetrations, valleys, transitions, curbs, and other vulnerable locations.

Safety should receive serious attention because roofing work includes significant fall hazards, ladder use, scaffold work, material handling, tools, weather exposure, and other jobsite risks.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference included with this package is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should become familiar with roofing-related provisions in the International Building Code before examination day. Effective open-book preparation involves learning how the code is organized and practicing targeted searches for requirements involving roof coverings, roof assemblies, materials, structural conditions, weather protection, and related building requirements.

An open-book examination still requires efficient time management. Candidates should not expect to search every answer from the beginning. A stronger strategy combines roofing knowledge with targeted use of the IBC when a precise code requirement needs to be confirmed.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates the C-15A Roofing Contractor classification. Passing the trade examination is one part of the complete Nevada contractor licensing process.

  1. Confirm the C-15A classification. Determine that Roofing Contractor matches the scope of work the business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Designate the person who will establish the applicable roofing experience and technical qualification.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare records demonstrating relevant roofing work within the requested classification.
  4. Prepare the Nevada contractor license application. Organize the required business, qualifier, experience, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application. The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews the application and qualification information before examination eligibility is established.
  6. Complete applicable background requirements. Follow required fingerprint and background procedures.
  7. Provide applicable financial information. Financial responsibility is evaluated as part of the licensing process.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Eligible applicants can proceed with the required testing after authorization.
  9. Schedule the C-15A examination through PSI. Arrange the Roofing Contractor trade examination after eligibility is established.
  10. Prepare for the technical exam. Study steep roofing, substrates, repairs, low-slope systems, flashing, safety, estimating, photovoltaic roofing considerations, and IBC navigation.
  11. Prepare for Nevada Business and Law. Use the included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada for Contractor Management Survey preparation.
  12. Complete applicable examinations. Meet the required trade and management qualification requirements unless an applicable waiver is approved.
  13. Complete final licensing requirements. Address applicable financial responsibility, contractor bonding, and remaining Nevada State Contractors Board requirements.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-15A Roofing subclassification covers qualifying installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof materials used on roofs. The authorized work can involve asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and other qualifying roofing materials.

Candidates should understand the scope of the classification before pursuing licensing. Nevada contractor classifications define the type of work a contractor may perform, so the C-15A scope should correspond with the roofing services the business intends to offer.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must establish four years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the application.

Applicants should maintain organized experience records describing the roofing work performed, the responsibilities held, and the period during which that experience was obtained. Experience should relate directly to the classification being requested.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes management qualification. The Management Qualified Individual generally satisfies the Contractor Management Survey and Nevada Business and Law requirement unless an approved waiver applies.

Financial responsibility, background procedures, contractor bonding, and other licensing requirements are separate from the trade examination. Passing the C-15A trade exam alone does not complete the contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-15A preparation plan should devote significant attention to steep-slope roofing while also covering substrates, membrane systems, repairs, flashing, safety, estimating, and photovoltaic-related roofing conditions.

Prioritize steep-slope roofing. Review asphalt shingles, tile, slate, wood shakes and shingles, underlayments, roof decks, fastening, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, and installation details.

Study roofing substrates. Understand roof decks, sheathing, surface preparation, structural conditions, deterioration, compatibility, and the way substrate problems can affect roof-system performance.

Review membrane and low-slope roofing. Study membrane systems, built-up roofing, elastomeric systems, insulation, attachment, flashing, drainage, and repairs.

Prepare safety for recall. OSHA is not allowed into the trade examination, so candidates should learn roofing safety concepts before test day, especially fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, PPE, tools, and material handling.

Practice IBC navigation. Work with the 2018 International Building Code under timed conditions and practice finding specific roofing requirements efficiently.

Study roofing estimating. Review roof measurements, materials, quantities, waste factors, and practical estimating concepts using Roofing Construction and Estimating.

Review rooftop photovoltaic conditions. Understand how photovoltaic components can interact with roofing systems, including penetrations, flashing, drainage, maintenance access, and roof performance.

Use timed practice questions. With 50 questions in two hours, candidates should develop a consistent pace and become comfortable balancing trade knowledge with targeted reference navigation.

How does the Roofing Contractor (C-15A) exam work?

The Roofing Contractor (C-15A) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
