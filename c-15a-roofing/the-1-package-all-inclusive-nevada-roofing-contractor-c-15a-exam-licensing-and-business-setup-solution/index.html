Exam Details

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor trade examination is administered through PSI for qualifying candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

  • Exam: Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor
  • Number of Questions: 50
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Minimum Passing Score: 38 correct answers
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The C-15A examination covers major roofing knowledge areas, including safety, roofing substrates, steep roofing, repairs, elastomeric roofing, built-up bituminous roofing, sheet metal and flashing, and photovoltaic-related roofing considerations.

Steep Roofing represents the largest individual area of the examination. Candidates should spend significant preparation time on asphalt shingles, clay and concrete tile, wood shakes and shingles, slate, roof decks, underlayments, fastening, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, drainage, and installation practices associated with steep-slope systems.

Roofing Substrates is another important area. Candidates should understand roof decks, substrate conditions, preparation, compatibility, fastening surfaces, deterioration, and how the existing roof structure can affect the performance of a roofing system.

Built-up Bituminous Roofing and Elastomeric Roofing require knowledge of low-slope roofing systems, membranes, insulation, flashing, attachment, surfacing, repairs, and installation practices. The NRCA roofing manuals are valuable study resources for these subjects.

Roofing Repairs requires candidates to recognize common roofing problems and appropriate repair approaches. Preparation should include leaks, damaged materials, deteriorated flashing, substrate problems, drainage concerns, membrane damage, and other conditions that affect roof performance.

Sheet Metal and Flashing may represent a smaller portion of the examination, but flashing is fundamental to watertight roof construction. Candidates should understand the role of flashings at walls, penetrations, valleys, edges, roof transitions, and other vulnerable locations.

Safety should receive serious attention because roofing work involves significant fall, ladder, scaffold, material-handling, and equipment hazards. OSHA is a study reference for this preparation but is not allowed into the trade examination.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-15A Roofing Contractor examination is an open-book test. The approved exam-room reference included with this package is:

  • International Building Code, 2018

Candidates should become familiar with roofing-related provisions in the International Building Code before examination day. Effective open-book preparation includes learning how the code is organized and practicing targeted searches for requirements involving roof coverings, roof assemblies, materials, structural considerations, weather protection, and related building requirements.

An open-book test should not be approached as an opportunity to research every question from the beginning. Candidates have limited time, so roofing knowledge and code-navigation skill should work together. Familiar trade questions can be answered directly, while the IBC can be used when a precise code requirement needs to be located or confirmed.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada State Contractors Board regulates the C-15A Roofing Contractor classification. Passing the trade examination is one part of the overall Nevada contractor licensing process.

  1. Confirm the C-15A classification. Determine that Roofing Contractor matches the scope of work the business intends to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. Designate the individual who will establish the applicable roofing experience and trade qualification.
  3. Document qualifying experience. Prepare records demonstrating relevant roofing work within the requested classification.
  4. Prepare the Nevada contractor license application. Organize the required business, qualifier, experience, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application. The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews the application and verifies experience before examination eligibility is issued.
  6. Complete applicable background requirements. Follow the fingerprint and background procedures required during Nevada contractor licensing.
  7. Address financial responsibility. Provide applicable financial information required as part of the licensing process.
  8. Receive examination eligibility. Eligible applicants can proceed with the applicable examinations after authorization.
  9. Schedule the C-15A examination through PSI. Arrange the Roofing Contractor trade examination after receiving eligibility.
  10. Prepare for the trade examination. Study steep-slope roofing, membranes, substrates, repairs, built-up systems, elastomeric roofing, flashing, safety, photovoltaic roofing considerations, and IBC navigation.
  11. Prepare for Nevada Business and Law. Use the included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada for Contractor Management Survey preparation.
  12. Complete applicable examinations. Meet trade and management examination requirements unless an approved waiver applies.
  13. Complete final licensing requirements. Address applicable financial responsibility, bonding, and remaining Nevada State Contractors Board requirements.

State Requirements

The Nevada C-15A Roofing subclassification authorizes qualifying installation, application, alteration, and repair of watertight and weatherproof materials used on roofs. This can include asphalt, pitch, tar, felt, shakes, shingles, aluminum, tile, slate, urethane, and other roofing materials within the authorized classification.

The C-15A subclassification is focused on roofing. Candidates planning to perform additional work should understand the boundaries of the classification and make sure the license held corresponds with the work being contracted.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the qualifying individual generally must establish four years of relevant experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Experience should relate to the roofing classification being requested.

Applicants should maintain organized records describing the roofing work performed, responsibilities held, and the period during which the experience was obtained. The Nevada State Contractors Board reviews experience before issuing examination eligibility.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes the Contractor Management Survey Business and Law examination unless an applicable waiver is approved. The Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada is included with this package for management examination preparation.

Financial responsibility, background procedures, contractor bonding, and other licensing conditions are separate from the trade exam. Passing the C-15A examination alone does not complete the Nevada contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-15A study plan should devote significant attention to steep-slope roofing while still covering substrates, low-slope systems, repairs, flashing, safety, and photovoltaic-related roofing considerations.

Prioritize steep-slope roofing. Review asphalt shingles, tile, slate, wood shakes and shingles, underlayments, roof decks, fastening, flashing, valleys, ridges, penetrations, and installation details.

Study roofing substrates. Understand roof decks, sheathing, surface preparation, deterioration, structural conditions, and compatibility with roof systems.

Review membrane and low-slope roofing. Study membrane systems, built-up bituminous roofing, elastomeric roofing, insulation, attachment methods, flashings, drainage, and repairs.

Prepare safety for recall. OSHA is not allowed into the trade examination, so candidates should learn roofing safety concepts before test day, especially fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, PPE, and material handling.

Practice IBC navigation. Work with the 2018 International Building Code under timed conditions and practice finding specific roofing requirements efficiently.

Study estimating. Review roofing measurements, materials, quantities, waste considerations, and practical estimating concepts using Roofing Construction and Estimating.

Review rooftop photovoltaic considerations. Understand how photovoltaic components can interact with roof systems, penetrations, flashing, drainage, maintenance, and roof performance.

Use timed practice questions. Candidates have two hours for 50 questions, so study sessions should include timed review and deliberate practice with reference navigation.

How does the Roofing Contractor (C-15A) exam work?

The Roofing Contractor (C-15A) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
