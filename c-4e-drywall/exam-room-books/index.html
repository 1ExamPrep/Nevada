Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall trade examination contains 20 questions. Candidates are allowed 1 hour to complete the examination, and the minimum passing score is 14 correct answers.

The examination content is divided into four primary subject areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.

Installation and Taping and Finishing represent the largest portions of the C-4E examination, with six questions in each category. Materials and Special Applications and Accessories each contain four questions.

With 20 questions and one hour available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. This makes efficient decision-making important. A candidate should be prepared to answer familiar trade questions directly and use the International Building Code strategically when reference research is necessary.

The examination may include questions based on trade knowledge and general industry practices in addition to information found in listed reference materials. Candidates should therefore avoid depending entirely on the code book and should build a solid understanding of drywall construction before test day.

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination is an open-book test. The International Building Code, 2018 is the reference permitted inside the examination center.

Special Applications and Accessories accounts for four questions. This category should not be overlooked simply because it contains fewer questions. Candidates should review specialized drywall applications and accessory components in addition to standard installation practices.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
