Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination is designed to evaluate knowledge associated with drywall contracting work. Candidates should prepare for practical trade concepts as well as applicable building-code information.

The C-4E examination contains 20 questions. Candidates have 1 hour to complete the exam, and the minimum passing score is 14 correct answers.

The examination is organized around four primary subject areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.

Installation and Taping and Finishing represent the largest portions of the examination. Materials and Special Applications and Accessories also make up an important part of the test and should not be overlooked during preparation.

With 20 questions and one hour available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. A successful preparation strategy should therefore include both knowledge review and efficient use of the approved reference.

Candidates should avoid depending on the International Building Code for every answer. The exam also requires understanding of drywall trade concepts, so practical knowledge developed from the full study set remains important.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination is an open-book test with the International Building Code, 2018 used as the permitted exam-room reference.

The fact that the examination is open book does not mean that every reference used for preparation can enter the testing center. Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 and the Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition are not allowed inside the exam.

This makes the preparation period especially important for the two study-only references. Candidates should use them to develop a working understanding of drywall installation, finishing, construction materials, gypsum systems, accessories, and related trade topics.

The International Building Code should be studied differently because candidates can use it during the examination. Practice navigating the index, chapters, sections, tables, and cross-references so you know how to locate applicable information efficiently.

The highlighted and tabbed format can make this process easier. Tabs can help you move quickly to major areas of the book, while highlighting can make selected information easier to recognize after you reach the correct section.

Repeated book-navigation practice is important. Candidates should avoid waiting until test day to begin learning how the International Building Code is organized. The more familiar the book becomes during preparation, the more efficiently it can be used under examination conditions.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada C-4E Drywall trade examination is one part of the contractor licensing process. Candidates must also complete the applicable application and qualification requirements associated with Nevada contractor licensing.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor classification. Confirm that the requested Nevada classification corresponds to the contracting work you intend to perform.
  2. Prepare qualifying experience information. Organize documentation showing applicable experience for the classification requested.
  3. Complete the contractor license application. Provide the required business, qualifying individual, experience, and supporting information.
  4. Submit the application for review. The licensing application and applicable supporting documents must be reviewed.
  5. Receive examination eligibility. Eligible applicants receive instructions for completing the required contractor examinations.
  6. Prepare for the C-4E examination. Study all tested trade areas and become familiar with the International Building Code, 2018.
  7. Complete the applicable examinations. Candidates must satisfy the examination requirements associated with the license application.
  8. Complete remaining licensing requirements. Any additional requirements must be completed before the contractor license can be issued.

Exam preparation and licensing preparation can be managed together. While organizing application materials, candidates can begin working through the highlighted and tabbed books so they are better prepared when examination eligibility is established.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong preparation plan for the Nevada C-4E Drywall examination should combine practical trade review, reference study, practice questions, and repeated book navigation.

Installation contains six questions. Candidates should review drywall systems, gypsum panels, components, terminology, installation procedures, and practical considerations associated with drywall work.

Taping and Finishing also contains six questions. Preparation should include finishing materials, application methods, terminology, procedures, and the sequence of work involved in preparing completed drywall surfaces.

Materials contains four questions. Candidates should become familiar with common drywall and gypsum products, accessories, components, and their intended applications.

Special Applications and Accessories contains four questions. Candidates should review applications that go beyond basic gypsum board installation and understand the accessories associated with different drywall systems.

The International Building Code, 2018 should be used regularly during preparation. Practice beginning with a topic or question, identifying the most useful keyword, and locating that term through the index.

After locating a section, read enough surrounding information to understand the requirement in context. Code provisions can include exceptions, related tables, or cross-references, so candidates should avoid stopping at the first highlighted line they see.

The tabs help guide you toward major portions of the code, but candidates should still learn how to use the index. Questions may require information located outside a section directly identified by a tab.

Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 can support preparation by reinforcing broader construction principles. Since the book cannot enter the examination room, use it to build knowledge that can be recalled and applied on test day.

The Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition supports more detailed drywall and gypsum review. Use it to strengthen your understanding of materials, installations, finishing practices, and specialized systems before the exam.

The highlighted and tabbed format can make study sessions more efficient across all three books. Candidates can more quickly return to important areas during review and spend additional time understanding the concepts.

Practice questions should be used to identify weaknesses. When a question is answered incorrectly, review the appropriate subject and determine why the correct answer applies rather than memorizing the answer alone.

For code-related practice, repeat difficult lookups. If you take too long to locate an applicable provision, use the highlighted and tabbed book to practice the search again until the process becomes more familiar.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
