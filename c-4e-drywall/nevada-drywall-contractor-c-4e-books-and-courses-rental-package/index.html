Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall classification relates to drywall contracting work, including the installation and coating of gypsum wallboard and sheathing and the installation of suspended ceiling systems made of gypsum wallboard.

Candidates preparing for this classification should build a strong understanding of drywall materials, installation practices, finishing concepts, gypsum systems, assemblies, and other practical trade knowledge associated with the work.

Nevada contractor licensing includes a Business and Law examination along with the applicable trade examination unless an examination waiver applies. That makes preparation for both trade knowledge and Nevada contractor business requirements important for many applicants.

The rental package supports both areas. The trade books help candidates prepare for drywall-related subjects, while the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports study of the business and law material associated with Nevada contractor licensing.

Licensing Steps

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination is one component of the contractor licensing process. Candidates should prepare for the exam while also keeping the application and qualification requirements organized.

  1. Determine the appropriate classification. Confirm that the Nevada C-4E Drywall classification corresponds to the contracting work you intend to perform.
  2. Identify the qualifying individual. The contractor application must identify the individual who will qualify the business for the requested classification.
  3. Prepare applicable experience documentation. Organize the trade experience information required for the classification.
  4. Complete the contractor license application. Provide the required business, qualifying individual, experience, financial, and supporting information.
  5. Submit the application for review. Examination eligibility is determined through the Nevada contractor licensing process.
  6. Prepare for the C-4E trade examination. Study drywall trade subjects and practice using the International Building Code, 2018.
  7. Prepare for Business and Law. Use the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada and the included course materials to study contractor business topics.
  8. Complete required examinations. Candidates must satisfy the examination requirements applicable to their license application unless an approved waiver applies.
  9. Complete remaining licensing requirements. Any additional requirements must be satisfied before the contractor license can be issued.

Combining trade preparation and business preparation into one study routine can help candidates keep both parts of the licensing process moving forward.

Nevada contractor applicants must complete the applicable licensing process and demonstrate qualification for the classification requested. The application process includes review of the qualifying individual and supporting documentation.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
