## Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall trade examination is administered through PSI as part of the Nevada contractor licensing process. Candidates must first meet the applicable Nevada State Contractors Board requirements and receive authorization to test.

- **Exam:** Nevada C-4E Drywall Contractor
- **Testing Provider:** PSI
- **Number of Questions:** 20
- **Time Allowed:** 1 hour
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 14 correct answers

The examination content is divided into four primary categories:

- **Installation:** 6 questions
- **Taping and Finishing:** 6 questions
- **Materials:** 4 questions
- **Special Applications and Accessories:** 4 questions

Installation and Taping and Finishing together account for 12 of the 20 questions. Candidates should give these two categories substantial study time while still preparing for Materials and Special Applications and Accessories.

Installation preparation should include gypsum board layout, measuring, cutting, fastening, attachment methods, wall systems, ceiling systems, sheathing, openings, and proper installation procedures. Candidates should understand how drywall components work together rather than relying only on isolated facts.

Taping and Finishing preparation should include joint treatment, tape, compounds, corners, surface preparation, finishing sequences, and the procedures used to produce a completed gypsum wallboard surface.

The Materials category includes knowledge of gypsum products, fasteners, compounds, trims, accessories, sheathing, and other components associated with drywall work. Product recognition is useful, but candidates should also understand how materials are selected and used in a drywall system.

Special Applications and Accessories includes work beyond basic wallboard installation. Candidates should review suspended gypsum wallboard ceilings, specialty conditions, trims, accessories, and other applications that may require different installation or finishing methods.

## Open Book Test

The following study references are **not allowed into the C-4E trade examination:**

Because the examination allows one hour for 20 questions, efficient use of the approved reference matters. Spending too much time searching for basic trade concepts can reduce the time available for questions that genuinely require a code lookup.

## State Requirements

This is a specialty classification. Contractors must operate within the scope of the classification for which they are licensed and should not treat C-4E as unrestricted authority for unrelated structural or specialty work.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the trade qualifying individual generally must demonstrate at least four years of qualifying experience as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor in the classification requested.

The qualifying experience generally must have been obtained within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. Experience documentation should clearly describe the type of drywall work performed and how the work relates to the C-4E classification.

Applicants generally must also satisfy contractor management and Business and Law requirements unless an applicable waiver is approved. One qualifying individual may sometimes satisfy multiple qualification roles when eligible, while other businesses may designate separate qualifiers.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-4E study plan should follow the exam outline rather than reviewing drywall topics randomly. Candidates can divide preparation into Installation, Taping and Finishing, Materials, Special Applications and Accessories, and International Building Code navigation.

**Focus heavily on Installation.** Installation accounts for six examination questions. Study wallboard layout, cutting, attachment, fastening, walls, ceilings, openings, sheathing, and general installation procedures.

**Give Taping and Finishing equal attention.** The category also contains six questions. Review joint treatment, tape, compounds, corner treatments, finishing sequences, surface preparation, and completed wallboard finishes.

**Build strong material knowledge.** Materials account for four questions. Review gypsum products, sheathing, fasteners, compounds, trims, and accessories. Understand how each component is used rather than memorizing names alone.

**Review Special Applications and Accessories.** The category also includes four questions. Study suspended gypsum wallboard ceilings, specialty applications, trims, accessories, and conditions that differ from standard wall installations.

**Practice code navigation.** The International Building Code is the approved technical exam reference. Work with it regularly so the organization of the book becomes familiar before test day.

**Study the non-exam-room books before testing.** Carpentry and Building Construction and the Gypsum Construction Handbook should be used to build practical understanding because candidates cannot rely on them during the examination.

**Use timed practice.** One hour for 20 questions gives an average of three minutes per question. Timed study sessions can help candidates become more disciplined about when to search the code and when to answer from trade knowledge.

**Review missed questions by category.** When several missed questions involve the same subject, direct additional preparation toward that area. This creates a more focused study plan than repeatedly reviewing material that is already familiar.

## How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
