## Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall trade examination contains **20 questions**. Candidates are allowed **1 hour** to complete the examination and must answer at least **14 questions correctly** to meet the minimum passing score.

The examination content is divided into four primary subject areas:

- **Installation:** 6 questions
- **Taping and Finishing:** 6 questions
- **Materials:** 4 questions
- **Special Applications and Accessories:** 4 questions

Installation and Taping and Finishing together represent 12 of the 20 questions, making those two areas especially important during preparation. Candidates should build both practical knowledge and familiarity with the terminology used in drywall construction.

Installation preparation should include gypsum wallboard layout, measuring, cutting, fastening, attachment methods, panel placement, sheathing, walls, ceilings, openings, and the sequence of work involved in installing drywall systems.

Taping and Finishing preparation should include joint treatment, tape, compounds, corner treatments, surface preparation, finishing sequences, and the procedures used to create completed gypsum wallboard surfaces.

The Materials category should include study of gypsum products, wallboard, sheathing, fasteners, compounds, trims, accessories, and other commonly used components associated with drywall work.

Special Applications and Accessories should include suspended gypsum wallboard ceiling systems, specialty installations, trims, accessories, and conditions that may require more than standard wallboard installation methods.

Because the exam contains only 20 questions, each question represents a meaningful portion of the total score. Candidates should prepare across all four content categories rather than relying only on jobsite experience or concentrating on one subject area.

Nevada contractor applicants generally must also satisfy applicable contractor management and Business and Law requirements unless an approved waiver applies. The included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for that separate component of the licensing process.

## How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
