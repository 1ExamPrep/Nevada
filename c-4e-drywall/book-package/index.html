Exam Details

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination contains 20 questions. Candidates are given 1 hour to complete the exam, and the minimum passing score is 14 correct answers.

The examination is divided into four primary content areas:

  • Installation: 6 questions.
  • Taping and Finishing: 6 questions.
  • Materials: 4 questions.
  • Special Applications and Accessories: 4 questions.

Installation and Taping and Finishing represent the largest portions of the exam, with six questions in each category. Materials and Special Applications and Accessories each account for four questions. Candidates should prepare for all four areas because the 20-question examination requires a broad understanding of the drywall trade.

With 20 questions and one hour available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. That makes time management important. Strong preparation can help you answer familiar trade questions without unnecessary searching and reserve reference lookups for situations where the International Building Code can provide useful information.

A balanced study plan should include practical drywall knowledge, understanding of materials and accessories, familiarity with finishing procedures, and regular use of the International Building Code. Candidates should also review weaker areas repeatedly rather than concentrating only on subjects they already know well.

Open Book Test

The Nevada C-4E Drywall examination is an open-book test. The International Building Code, 2018 is the reference allowed inside the examination center.

Although the examination is open book, the other study references associated with the C-4E exam are not permitted in the testing room. Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 and the Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition are not allowed inside the examination center.

The distinction is important when preparing. Use the two non-exam-room books to learn and reinforce practical trade concepts before test day. Since you cannot depend on those books during testing, your goal should be to understand the material well enough to apply it from your own knowledge.

The International Building Code should be approached differently because it can be used during the exam. Practice using the index, chapter organization, tables, section numbers, and cross-references so that locating information becomes more efficient.

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs may be used, while temporary tabs are not permitted. Candidates should become familiar with their approved reference before test day rather than learning how to navigate the book during the examination.

Open-book testing works best when trade knowledge and reference familiarity support each other. Candidates who understand the subject can recognize what a question is asking more quickly, while familiarity with the code helps when a specific requirement needs to be located.

Test Information and Study Materials

The Nevada C-4E examination focuses on four core subject areas, and each should be included in your study plan. Candidates should combine reading, practice questions, review of missed material, and reference-navigation exercises.

Installation contains six questions. Preparation should include drywall installation concepts, common components, gypsum panels, wall and ceiling systems, terminology, and practical procedures associated with the trade.

Taping and Finishing also contains six questions. Candidates should review materials, terminology, application procedures, finishing processes, and the sequence of work involved in preparing completed drywall surfaces.

Materials contains four questions. Review commonly used drywall and gypsum products, components, accessories, and their intended applications. Understanding how and why materials are used is often more valuable than memorizing names alone.

Special Applications and Accessories contains four questions. Candidates should review specialized drywall applications and accessory components that go beyond basic gypsum board installation.

The International Building Code, 2018 should be used as part of regular preparation because it is permitted inside the examination room. When practicing with the book, start by identifying the main keyword in a question and using the index to locate the most likely section.

After finding a section, read enough surrounding material to understand the requirement in context. Code provisions often connect to other sections, tables, or exceptions, so candidates should avoid relying on a single isolated sentence without reviewing related information.

Carpentry and Building Construction, 2016 can help strengthen broader building knowledge that supports practical exam questions. Use it to reinforce construction terminology, methods, materials, and concepts before test day.

The Gypsum Construction Handbook, 7th edition provides focused material related to gypsum systems and drywall construction. Use it to review installation, finishing, materials, and specialized applications that align with the C-4E exam outline.

Because these two books cannot enter the examination room, repeated study is especially important. Candidates should build enough familiarity with the information to answer trade questions without expecting to research them during the test.

Practice questions can help identify areas where additional review is needed. When you miss a question, return to the appropriate book and study the underlying concept. The goal is to understand why the correct answer applies rather than memorizing a single response.

How does the Drywall Contractor (C-4E) exam work?

The Drywall Contractor (C-4E) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
