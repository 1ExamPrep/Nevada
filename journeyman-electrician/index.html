How does the Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) card is part of the Electrical Qualifications ID card program run by Electrical Safety Professionals for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno. You pass the ICC national exam, then apply to ESP with the score report.

  • The ICC G17 Journeyman Electrician exam has 80 questions in 4 hours and is open book with the NEC.
  • The ID card is valid for 3 years and renews with continuing education from an approved provider.
  • Nevada has no state electrician license; Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno require an Electrical Qualifications ID card on every job site.
  • Electrical Safety Professionals (ESP) issues the cards, after you pass an ICC national exam: G17 journeyman, G16 master, G18 residential or G21 low voltage.
  • ICC electrician exams are open book with the NEC, and ICC offers them in more than one code edition, so match the edition to the exam you book.
  • Cards are valid for 3 years; one expired for more than a year means taking the exam again.

Is the Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) exam open book?

Yes. The ICC national electrician exams are open book with the National Electrical Code edition named for your exam, and ICC lists a few other references, such as Ugly's Electrical References, as allowed.

Who issues the Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) license or card in Nevada?

It comes from Electrical Safety Professionals (ESP), which runs the ID card program for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno, after an ICC exam. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Does Nevada have a state electrician license?

No. Nevada licenses electrical contractors through the NSCB (C-2), but individual electricians carry an Electrical Qualifications ID card required by Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno, issued by Electrical Safety Professionals after an ICC exam.

Which NEC edition should I study?

The one named for the ICC exam you book. ICC offers its national electrician exams in more than one edition, so check the exam title in the ICC catalog, then pick the package for that code year.

What is the Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) exam like?

The ICC G17 Journeyman Electrician exam has 80 questions in 4 hours and is open book with the NEC. The ID card is valid for 3 years and renews with continuing education from an approved provider.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice exams are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations that point to the reference section.
