## Exam Details

Nevada journeyman electrician qualification testing is commonly tied to the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination used for electrical qualification card purposes. ICC electrician exams are scheduled through the ICC exam system and administered through Pearson VUE testing options, including testing center appointments and approved online testing where available.

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam has 80 multiple-choice questions and a 4-hour time limit. The exam is open book. The content outline includes general knowledge, services and service equipment, feeders, branch circuits and conductors, wiring methods and materials, equipment and devices, control devices, motors and generators, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions.

The content distribution for the National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 6% general knowledge, 11% services and service equipment, 4% feeders, 19% branch circuits and conductors, 26% wiring methods and materials, 13% equipment and devices, 4% control devices, 6% motors and generators, and 11% special occupancies, equipment, and conditions. General knowledge may include electrical theory, general electrical load calculations, code definitions, terms, and plan reading. Branch circuits and conductors may include branch circuit calculations, overcurrent protection, conductor rules, required outlets, GFCI protection, AFCI protection, and equipment grounding. Wiring methods and materials may include raceway installations, underground wiring methods, boxes, enclosures, cabinets, panelboards, switchboards, clearance and guarding, flexible cords, and cables.

## How does the Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician (ICC G17) card is part of the Electrical Qualifications ID card program run by Electrical Safety Professionals for Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno. You pass the ICC national exam, then apply to ESP with the score report.

- The ICC G17 Journeyman Electrician exam has 80 questions in 4 hours and is open book with the NEC.
- The ID card is valid for 3 years and renews with continuing education from an approved provider.
- Nevada has no state electrician license; Las Vegas, Clark County and Reno require an Electrical Qualifications ID card on every job site.
- Electrical Safety Professionals (ESP) issues the cards, after you pass an ICC national exam: G17 journeyman, G16 master, G18 residential or G21 low voltage.
- ICC electrician exams are open book with the NEC, and ICC offers them in more than one code edition, so match the edition to the exam you book.
- Cards are valid for 3 years; one expired for more than a year means taking the exam again.
