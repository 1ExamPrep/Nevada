## How does the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam work?

The Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- The B-7 Restricted License covers jobs up to $7,000 including labor and materials, needs no trade exam and is valid for 2 years.
- A restricted license holder may not do life-safety trades such as electrical, plumbing or HVAC work.
- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.

### Is the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam open book?

Partly. The Business and Law (CMS) exam is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada. Trade exams are closed book except for the code references named in the PSI content outline for your classification, which is why the exam-room book packages are sold as allowed in the exam.

### Who issues the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) license or card in Nevada?

It comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB), with exams by PSI. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### What does the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam cost?

PSI charges $95 for one exam portion and $140 when the trade exam is taken with the Business and Law (CMS) exam. The NSCB application fee of $300 is separate.

### Can I use the NASCLA exam for a Nevada B license?

Yes. Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building score in place of the B General Building trade exam. You still take the Nevada Business and Law (CMS) exam and meet the experience, bond and financial rules.

### What is the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam like?

The B-7 Restricted License covers jobs up to $7,000 including labor and materials, needs no trade exam and is valid for 2 years. A restricted license holder may not do life-safety trades such as electrical, plumbing or HVAC work.
