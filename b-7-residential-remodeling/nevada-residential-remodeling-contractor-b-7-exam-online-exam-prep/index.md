## Exam Details

The Nevada B-7 Residential Remodeling trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates pursuing the classification through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-7 Residential Remodeling
- **Number of Questions:** 50
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 35 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The examination is divided among the following major content areas:

- **Carpentry:** 12 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 7 questions
- **Windows and Doors:** 5 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 15 questions
- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Estimating and Plan Reading:** 5 questions

**Drywall and Finishes** is the largest individual section with 15 questions. Candidates should spend substantial study time on gypsum materials, drywall installation, finishing methods, accessories, interior surfaces, tile-related finish work, and the practical sequence of interior remodeling.

**Carpentry** contains 12 questions and represents another major portion of the examination. Study should include framing, rough carpentry, finish carpentry, structural wood components, cabinets, trim, fastening methods, tools, and common residential remodeling procedures.

**Thermal and Moisture Protection** contains seven questions. Candidates should review weather-resistant construction, flashing, moisture control, insulation-related concepts, sealants, roofing-related concerns, and methods used to protect existing residential structures from water intrusion and environmental exposure.

**Safety** contains six questions. Preparation should include fall protection, ladders, scaffolds, personal protective equipment, tools, material handling, and other construction hazards relevant to residential remodeling.

**Windows and Doors** and **Estimating and Plan Reading** each contain five questions. Candidates should understand openings, components, installation principles, construction drawings, dimensions, quantities, and project information used to plan and complete remodeling work.

## State Requirements

The Nevada B-7 Residential Remodeling classification applies to qualifying remodeling and improvement work involving existing residential structures within the scope established for the classification.

The classification includes work involving an existing detached, stand-alone single-family residence or a single-family residential unit within a structure that does not extend more than three stories above the ground and one story below the ground.

A key limitation is that the B-7 classification does not authorize the contractor to increase the existing enclosed space of the residential structure. Candidates should understand this restriction when determining whether the B-7 classification matches the work they intend to perform.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must establish at least four full years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. The experience should relate directly to the classification being requested.

Qualifying experience may include applicable work performed as a journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor. Applicants should maintain organized documentation describing the type of remodeling work performed and the period during which the experience was gained.

Nevada contractor licensing also includes management qualification. One person may satisfy both management and trade qualification when all applicable requirements are met, or separate individuals may be designated.

Other requirements can include financial responsibility review, contractor bonding, background procedures, and final licensing conditions. Passing the B-7 trade exam alone does not complete the Nevada contractor licensing process.

## How does the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam work?

The Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- The B-7 Restricted License covers jobs up to $7,000 including labor and materials, needs no trade exam and is valid for 2 years.
- A restricted license holder may not do life-safety trades such as electrical, plumbing or HVAC work.
- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
