## Exam Details

The Nevada B-7 Residential Remodeling trade examination is administered through PSI for candidates whose examination eligibility is established through the Nevada State Contractors Board.

- **Exam:** Nevada B-7 Residential Remodeling
- **Number of Questions:** 50
- **Time Allowed:** 2 hours
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 35 correct answers
- **Testing Provider:** PSI

The B-7 examination content is divided among the following subjects:

- **Carpentry:** 12 questions
- **Thermal and Moisture Protection:** 7 questions
- **Windows and Doors:** 5 questions
- **Drywall and Finishes:** 15 questions
- **Safety:** 6 questions
- **Estimating and Plan Reading:** 5 questions

**Drywall and Finishes** is the largest individual category with 15 questions. Candidates should spend substantial preparation time on gypsum products, drywall installation, finishing methods, interior surfaces, tile-related work, and other finishing subjects associated with residential remodeling.

**Carpentry** contains 12 questions and is another major portion of the examination. Study should include residential framing, rough and finish carpentry, cabinets, trim, structural wood components, installation methods, remodeling procedures, and practical carpentry concepts.

**Thermal and Moisture Protection** contains seven questions. Candidates should review weather protection, moisture control, insulation-related concepts, flashing, roofing-related remodeling concerns, sealants, and methods used to protect residential construction from water and environmental exposure.

**Safety** contains six questions. OSHA preparation should include personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolds, fall protection, power tools, material handling, and other construction hazards that may be encountered during remodeling work.

**Windows and Doors** and **Estimating and Plan Reading** each contain five questions. Candidates should understand installation and remodeling considerations for doors and windows while also practicing interpretation of plans, dimensions, quantities, and project information.

## State Requirements

The B-7 Residential Remodeling classification authorizes remodeling and improvement of an existing detached, stand-alone single-family residence or a single-family residential unit within a structure that does not extend more than three stories above the ground and one story below the ground.

A key limitation of the B-7 classification is that it does not authorize the contractor to increase the existing enclosed space of the residential structure. Candidates should understand this scope when deciding whether B-7 matches the projects they intend to perform.

For the standard Nevada contractor licensing pathway, the Trade Qualified Individual generally must have at least four full years of qualifying experience within the 15 years immediately preceding the application. The experience should be in the classification requested and at an applicable journeyman, foreman, supervising employee, or contractor level.

Nevada contractor licensing also uses management and trade qualification. One individual may satisfy both roles when all applicable requirements are met, or separate individuals may serve as the Management Qualified Individual and Trade Qualified Individual.

The Management Qualified Individual generally must satisfy the Nevada Contractor Management Survey Business and Law requirement. The included Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada supports preparation for this portion of the licensing process.

Other contractor licensing requirements can include financial responsibility review, background procedures, a contractor license bond, and completion of the final licensing conditions established by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

## How does the Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) exam work?

The Residential Remodeling Contractor (B-7) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

- The B-7 Restricted License covers jobs up to $7,000 including labor and materials, needs no trade exam and is valid for 2 years.
- A restricted license holder may not do life-safety trades such as electrical, plumbing or HVAC work.
- Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
- The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
- PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
- Nevada accepts a passing NASCLA Commercial General Building exam in place of the B trade exam, and since March 2026 issues a B-7 Restricted License for jobs up to $7,000.
