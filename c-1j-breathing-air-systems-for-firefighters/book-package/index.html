Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Nevada C-1J study plan should begin with the scope of the system. Candidates should understand that these systems are designed to replenish breathing air for firefighters and require reliable piping, connections, components, and installation practices.

Piping materials should receive careful study. Candidates should understand how code requirements address acceptable materials and how material selection can affect system reliability and compatibility.

Fittings and joints are also important. Review how piping sections are connected and why proper joining methods are necessary for a dependable system.

Supports and hangers should be included in preparation. Piping must be properly supported and arranged so that the installed system remains secure and functional.

Valves should be studied in relation to flow control, isolation, and system operation. Candidates should understand the purpose of valves and how they fit into a complete piping system.

Testing is another useful area of study. Candidates should understand why installed piping systems must be checked before being placed into service and how testing relates to system integrity.

The Uniform Plumbing Code, 2018 should be used throughout preparation rather than only during the final days before the exam. Repeated code use helps candidates remember where major subjects are located.

Definitions can be important because code questions may depend on the exact meaning of a technical term. Practice locating definitions quickly whenever terminology is unclear.

Tables can contain important requirements that may not be repeated in paragraph form. Candidates should practice reading headings, notes, and conditions carefully.

Exceptions can modify general requirements. Read enough of the surrounding code language to determine whether an exception applies to the installation described in the question.

Cross-references can direct the candidate to another section containing additional requirements. Practice following these references during study so the process becomes routine.

Timed code-navigation drills can help candidates prepare for the 60-minute exam. Choose a piping topic and practice locating the relevant code section within a limited amount of time.

A useful drill might begin with a subject such as pipe materials, supports, fittings, valves, or testing. Use the index to locate the topic, read the section carefully, and identify any notes, exceptions, or tables that affect the answer.

More on test Information and Study Materials

Because the examination contains 25 questions in 60 minutes, candidates should learn to recognize when a question can be answered from trade knowledge and when a code lookup is necessary.

Active recall is also useful. After studying a piping topic, close the book and explain the installation concept from memory. Then reopen the reference and check whether any important requirement was missed.

Practice questions can help identify weak areas. When a question is missed, determine whether the problem was unfamiliar terminology, weak piping knowledge, slow reference navigation, or difficulty interpreting the code language.

Correcting the underlying weakness is more useful than memorizing a single answer. The goal is to become comfortable applying plumbing-code concepts to the specialized work covered by C-1J.

System reliability should remain a central theme during preparation. Firefighter breathing-air replenishment systems perform an important life-safety function, so candidates should approach piping, connections, supports, valves, and testing with attention to proper installation practices.

A structured study routine can alternate between code review and practical trade review. One session might focus on piping materials and fittings, while another focuses on supports, valves, system components, or testing.

As test day approaches, move into mixed-topic drills and timed practice. This can help candidates transition quickly from one subject to another while maintaining a consistent exam pace.

How does the Breathing Air Systems for Firefighters Contractor (C-1J) exam work?

The Breathing Air Systems for Firefighters Contractor (C-1J) license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board. You apply to the NSCB with your experience and financial statement, the Board sends an exam eligibility letter, and you book the CMS and the trade exam with PSI. Scores come right after the exam.

  • Every Nevada contractor license needs the Business and Law (CMS) exam plus a trade exam for the classification, both given by PSI.
  • The CMS is open book with the Construction Business and Law Manual for Nevada; trade exams are closed book except for the code references their outline allows.
  • PSI charges $95 for one exam portion, or $140 for a trade exam taken with the CMS, and you get three attempts before the application is void.
  • The Board sets a bond from $1,000 to $500,000 and a monetary limit on every license, based on your experience and financial statements.
